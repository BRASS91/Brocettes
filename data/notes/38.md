## **Overview**

The party answered the Question of the Day, then Paul recapped the tentacled creature beneath Tresendar Manor before the group attended Tharden Rockseeker’s funeral in the rain, where Levon carved the grave marker. After three days of downtime, Blue attuned to the Drowned Pan Flute while Francis suffered repeated acid pain from his unexplained affliction. Sister Garaele delivered a scroll of Remove Curse and revealed that she is a Harper, and the party decided that Vexhar would try to copy the scroll into his spellbook instead of casting it. The party then joined Phandalin’s festival, where they played a fortune teller’s game, entered a cooking contest that Levon won, and competed in an archery contest against Phelan the Red, where Broc tied her score. The session ended halfway through the festival with several games and the prize tent still ahead.

## **Recap and Tharden’s Funeral**

Paul recapped the previous session: the party fought a tentacled creature beneath Tresendar Manor, Francis landed a 55 damage critical hit, Allyanna finished it, Blue found the pan flute, and Francis was left covered in ichor that Sister Garaele could not cure. The party met Gundren Rockseeker at the Shrine of Luck and followed him to Tharden’s grave, where Nundro, Sildar Hallwinter, Harbin Wester, and Sister Garaele gathered in the rain. Gundren gave a short eulogy and asked Levon to make a grave marker, and Levon carved Tharden’s name and a decorative border into a hardwood board. Gundren toasted Tharden from a flask that the party passed around, and he placed something under the first spade of dirt before Nundro filled the grave. Gundren decided to keep Levon’s marker, which was set at the head of the grave, and Levon promised to maintain it. The party offered condolences and left Gundren and Nundro at the grave.

## **Three Days of Downtime**

Paul skipped ahead three days, and each character took a long rest and two downtime activities. Blue researched the Feywild language and people who had returned from the Feywild, failed both Intelligence checks with a 6 and a 10, and gained information that pointed her in the wrong direction. Blue attuned to the pan flute, which Paul revealed as the Drowned Pan Flute, granting Fly, Invisibility, Levitate, Protection from Evil and Good, Barkskin, Cure Wounds, and Fog Cloud once each per long rest, and it keeps humming when she is not playing it. Broc swapped his Ray of Frost cantrip for Friends, and Broc and Francis agreed to assist each other on future downtime activities.

## **Francis Suffers Unexplained Acid Pain**

Paul ruled that on the night after the funeral Francis’s skin became itchy and then painful, dealing 1d12 acid damage. Francis called for help, and Broc, Vexhar, and Francis made medicine checks against DC 15, but none of them identified the cause. The pain recurred through the night, and Francis feared his condition was getting worse. Paul ruled that Francis could heal himself with magic about once a day to manage it, and the party never learned what caused or eased the pain.

## **Garaele Delivers Scroll and Harper Secret**

On the morning of the 19th of Hammer, Sister Garaele met the party at the Shrine of Luck and gave Francis a scroll case holding a scroll of Remove Curse from her contacts. She explained that Vexhar could read it for an automatic success, that Francis could read it at DC 13 with a risk of failure, or that Vexhar could try to copy it into his spellbook, which might fail and destroys the scroll either way. Garaele revealed that she is a Harper who has reported to the network for years and was sent to Phandalin to investigate, and she asked the party to keep this secret. Broc identified the Harpers immediately as loosely linked cells that oppose tyranny, while Francis only vaguely recognized the name. Garaele said the Harpers have taken an interest in the party after the goblins, the lost mine, and the creature beneath the manor, and that they want to sit down with Francis about joining or working alongside them. Francis agreed to meet them, and Broc said he would explain everything to the others and that the party would make excellent Harpers.

## **Deciding Whether to Cast or Copy**

Francis asked Vexhar to cast Remove Curse immediately, and Vexhar said he would rather copy it into his spellbook, then offered to cast it now if Francis owed him a favor. Francis agreed to repay the favor by keeping Vexhar alive, and he took 9 acid damage to his right shoulder in the middle of the sentence. Broc pointed out that a copied spell would cure future curses permanently while casting would cure Francis now, and Allyanna first urged casting it immediately before telling Francis he could suffer through the festival if he wished. Francis chose to endure the pain and handed the scroll to Vexhar, who agreed to try copying it. Paul explained that copying takes about six hours and 150 gold, requires a DC 13 check, and needs a full day afterward to prepare the spell, and Vexhar said the gold was no problem. Paul said Vexhar could spend heroic inspiration on the check and could do the copying during downtime, short rests, or trance, so the party shelved it for the moment.

## **Festival Opens and Torra’s Wood**

After 10 to 15 minutes, Harbin Wester declared the festival open, and the party walked down to the fairgrounds, where the circus tent stood among food stalls, games, and mingled townsfolk and circus performers. Harbin greeted the party and called over Torra Ironwell, who said the manor roof was coming along. Torra told Levon she had found a pile of Realm stock wood while clearing the manor and offered either to build something from it or hand it over. Levon asked her to set it aside for the renovation or for furniture once the party moves in. The party voted five to nothing to visit the fortune teller, with Broc rolling a 16 on persuasion to convince Blue, who was already willing.

## **Madame Vane’s Riddles and Card Reading**

Madame Ilsbeth Vane asked the party two riddles, and the party answered a cold for what you can catch but never throw, while Vexhar answered a shadow for what follows you all day and vanishes at night. She awarded tickets to everyone, then offered a mind reading game in which each player bet tickets, picked a card, and let her guess it. Madame Vane correctly named The Fool for Broc, The Sun for Francis, The Moon for Allyanna, and The Star for Vexhar, and she kept their tickets. She guessed The Moon for Blue, but Blue had chosen The Tower, so Blue’s two ticket bet came back as four tickets. She named The Sun for Levon, who had bet three tickets, after her crystal ball dimmed. Allyanna accused Madame Vane of cheating, and Madame Vane denied it.

## **Harbin Sees Francis Take Acid Damage**

As the party walked toward the roasting spit, Francis took 10 acid damage in front of Harbin. Harbin asked whether Francis was okay and told him to see Sister Garaele. Blue said Francis might have picked something up and was getting it looked at, and Harbin stepped back from him. Harbin then pointed the party toward the cooking competition.

## **Cooking Contest and Toblen’s Verdict**

All six party members entered a cooking contest run by a gruff cook at a roasting spit, with Toblen Stonehill as the strict judge. During seasoning the party spoke privately with Trilena Stonehill, and Broc’s magic sparked visibly from his fingers, which sent Trilena back to her seat. Broc’s dish ended up buried in smoky seasoning, Blue’s pork burned in the hottest part of the fire, Francis’s dish blackened, Vexhar’s came off raw in the middle, Levon’s was raw inside and burnt outside, and Allyanna’s chicken divan cooked well. Toblen called Broc’s dish different, called Allyanna’s brave, said he had eaten worse in a goblin cave about Francis’s, and said Vexhar’s had texture, while Blue secretly tossed her meatballs under a table and said the fire ate them. Levon argued with the cook over the spit, but Toblen said he would serve Levon’s dish at the inn and declared him the winner, awarding the cook’s second best ladle. Trilena handed out tickets to the party, with some players earning more than others.

## **Goat Bolts From the Mine Road**

A ribboned fairground goat stopped stiff in the path, flattened its ears, and turned its head toward the old mine road. It let out a sharp cry that did not sound like a goat, backed away, and bolted, and it stayed away from that end of the grounds for the rest of the day. A man near the ale tent called after it, and a bystander laughed it off and said it probably smelled the wolf. Blue joked that it smelled her cooking, and the party chose the archery contest next.

## **Archery Contest Against Phelan the Red**

Phelan the Red, a wood elf archer, posted a score of eight on the chalkboard and challenged the party to beat it with three arrows each, calling a ring before every shot. Francis hit the outer and inner rings but called the bullseye on his last arrow, and a shifting wind carried it wide, leaving him with four points, while Allyanna hit the inner ring once and missed twice for three. Blue used Lucky on her shots, curved one arrow into the inner ring, and finished with six points after her called bullseye landed in the inner ring. Broc rolled a natural 20 on his first arrow, hit the bullseye while calling the inner ring, then hit the inner ring, and his last arrow fell short at Phelan’s boot, so Phelan called it a draw at eight points. Levon used a longbow, missed all three shots, and finished with zero points after his called bullseye sailed over the tent. Vexhar missed once, then hit the inner ring twice to the crowd’s cheers, but still lost to Phelan.

## **End of Session**

Paul ended the session after the archery contest, about halfway through the festival, with the carousel, Fenwin’s cups, the tug of war, and the prize tent still ahead. Broc led the ticket standings, followed by Levon, Allyanna, Vexhar, Francis, and Blue. Francis remains cursed and in pain, and Vexhar has not yet copied the Remove Curse scroll into his spellbook. The goat’s fright toward the old mine road remains unexplained.
