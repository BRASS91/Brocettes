## **Overview**

After a long rest outside Wave Echo Cave, the party returned at dawn with Gundren to hunt down Nezznar the Spider and rescue Nundro Rockseeker. On the way in, they examined the elongated goblin corpse in the pit again and found that neither magic nor anything from the Feywild explained why it was not decomposing. In a pillared hall beneath a great dwarf statue, Nezznar threatened to have Nundro killed and revealed that his operation had flagged Vexhar long before the party arrived, which set the group against each other. When a staged argument failed to fool him, Nezznar trapped the party in webs and kept fighting through darkness and invisibility until Francis grappled him and Allyanna killed him with an Eldritch Blast. Moments later, Nundro escaped his captors and ran straight into the party, who cut down the two bugbears chasing him, and Gundren was reunited with his last living brother.

## **Breaking Camp and Returning to the Cave**

Before setting out, Francis swapped Thunderous Smite for Compelled Duel among his prepared spells, and Blue replaced Chill Touch with Toll the Dead. At dawn, Broc proposed killing the Spider that day, and the party agreed to go after breakfast. The thirty minute trip back was uneventful, and the group arrived as the sun rose over the mountainsides. Inside, Gundren stopped at the campsite to cover Tharden's body with a blanket and promised him a proper burial once their work was done. Blue slowed her pace to stay with him, and Gundren said he still hoped Nundro was alive.

## **The Goblin That Will Not Rot**

At the bottom of the pit, the party found the elongated goblin corpse surrounded by flies and maggots but showing no decay after at least a day. Vexhar examined it and could tell only that something was clearly wrong with how well preserved it was, and he found no gear or markings on it. Blue cast Detect Magic and sensed no school of magic at work, and her memories of the Feywild told her this was nothing Fey but something colder and emptier. When Broc asked about the Forge of Spells, Gundren explained that old stories said the forge could enchant weapons and tools right at the source, though he could not say whether it had anything to do with the goblin. Broc and Francis guessed that the Spider might be using the forge to make these goblins.

## **Into the Spider's Camp**

Passing the pool where they had fought the snake, Blue used a minor illusion of a bear and heard the snake dive deeper, which confirmed it was still there. The party followed the smell of wood smoke to a set of doors, and Francis and Levon threw them open on a count of three. Inside, six cracked pillars lined a hall ending in a nine foot statue of a dwarf with emerald eyes, and an organized camp of bedrolls surrounded a firepit near a wooden table. Nezznar stood by the table between two bugbears, holding a black staff topped with a carved spider, and said he would have to deal with them himself.

## **Negotiating for Nundro**

Nezznar confirmed that Nundro was locked in a room under guard and warned that the dwarf would die before the party reached him if a fight broke out. Broc cast Message to pass this to Blue, who was waiting outside with Gundren, but Nezznar noticed the spell and called it rude. At Nezznar's demand, Francis sheathed his sword and raised his hands and Levon put his weapon on his back, while Blue tried to judge his sincerity from outside and learned nothing from his cold voice. Nezznar said he wanted to claim the Forge of Spells, that a fight was expensive, and that the party could leave freely as long as Nundro stayed behind. Broc, Levon, and Allyanna argued that he could get a map from Nundro instead of holding him, but Nezznar refused to discuss it further.

## **Nezznar Singles Out Vexhar**

Nezznar turned to Vexhar and said his people had flagged him as a drow running errands in the Neverwinter underworld before he ever set foot in the manor, and that someone else in the operation had taken an interest in him first. Vexhar told Broc he would not want to know, denied working for Nezznar or his boss, and said he was not sure he even knew his employer's name. Furious, Broc shoved Vexhar out the door to buy time for a rescue while Francis staged a loud argument, but Nezznar saw through the act and called it a distraction. He then demanded to know which of them actually meant to act against him. When Francis warned that they would kill him if they had to, Nezznar sighed and cast Web over the doorway.

## **Battle With Nezznar the Spider**

  - Nezznar's Web restrains Broc, Vexhar, Francis, and Blue. Vexhar's Hold Person fails to hold Nezznar, and a bugbear hammers Vexhar for 9 damage.
  - Francis tears free of the web and casts Shield of Faith on Vexhar, then keeps concentration through two Poison Blasts from Nezznar that deal 22 damage.
  - Allyanna steps out of the web, casts Hex, and spends Heroic Inspiration to land a 19 damage Eldritch Blast on a bugbear. Levon rages and swings recklessly but misses twice, even with Francis's Heroic Inspiration.
  - Broc uses Tides of Chaos and Blue's Bardic Inspiration to shove Blue out of the web, then burns it away with a Quickened Fire Bolt that frees everyone and deals 7 fire damage each to himself, Vexhar, and Vexhar Jr.
  - Vexhar's Chromatic Orb hits Nezznar for 16 and leaps to the wounded bugbear for 12. Francis commands Nezznar to grovel, which knocks him prone and costs him his next turn.
  - Blue casts Mirror Image and uses Mantle of Inspiration to give her allies 8 temporary hit points. Levon's opportunity attack kills the bugbear Allyanna had wounded with her greatsword as it rushes to Nezznar's aid.
  - Broc's Magic Missile finishes the other bugbear, and a Wild Magic Surge hits Nezznar for 22. Francis follows with a Divine Smite that leaves Nezznar near death before he stands and fills the room with magical Darkness.
  - Broc uses Detect Magic to find Nezznar in the darkness and hits him with a crossbow bolt, and Francis grapples him in a full nelson and invokes Vow of Enmity. Nezznar resists Blue's Vicious Mockery and turns invisible in Francis's grip, and after Levon fails to tie him up, Allyanna kills him with an Eldritch Blast.

## **Nundro Breaks Free**

During the fight, Blue scouted east and found a locked door and stairs leading down, but no sign of Nundro. As Francis reached for Nezznar's staff, a battered dwarf with raw wrists came running down a side passage begging for help, with two bugbears right behind him. Blue cast Charm Person on both bugbears to stop them, and Broc's upcast Magic Missile and the rest of the party cut them down before they reached the dwarf. Gundren recognized his brother Nundro and rushed to his side, and Nundro asked about Tharden before learning he had not survived. Nundro said he got loose by sheer luck when the fighting started and told Gundren he had a lot to tell him.

## **End of Session**

Nezznar and four bugbears lie dead, and Gundren is reunited with Nundro among the fallen bugbears by the stairs. The locked room to the east and the Forge of Spells are still unexplored. The question of who in Nezznar's operation took an interest in Vexhar also remains open. The party will advance to 5th level before the next session.
