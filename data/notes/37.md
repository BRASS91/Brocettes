## **Overview**

The session opens with a Question of the Day and a recap from Blue of the party’s apple picking, the meeting with Sildar and Gundren, the deed to Tresendar Manor, and the descent into the drains beneath it. In a flooded chamber below the manor, a telepathic aboleth refuses to let the party repair the pipes and demands they serve it. Talks fail, Broc opens combat with Shatter, and the party kills the aboleth after Francis lands a critical hit for 55 damage and Allyanna delivers the final blow. A slimy ichor from the fight clings to Francis and blocks nonmagical healing, and Blue recovers a magical pan flute from the wreckage. Sister Garaele takes a sample of the ichor, and the party heads toward Tharden’s funeral as a festival arrives in Phandalin.

## **Question of the Day and Recap**

Paul opens by asking each player the Question of the Day, and Levon, Blue, Allyanna, Francis, Vexhar, and Broc answer in turn. Paul notes that everyone holds a rolling inspiration except Blue and offers one in exchange for a recap. Blue recounts that the party brought apple picking proceeds back to the inn, ordered pies and danishes, and that Broc had an awkward interaction with Trelenna. Blue adds that the party met Sildar and Gundren, discussed the Nundro duplicate, the beholder sent home, the undead, and Craig’s goblin commune, and received the deed to Tresendar Manor. Blue finishes with Cora Ironwell’s refurbishing work, the corroded pipe she asked them to investigate, the rat swarms in the drains, and the voice that ended the session. Paul confirms the recap and grants Blue inspiration.

## **A Voice Beneath Tresendar Manor**

The party stands in a wide rocky hollow around a sunken pool, with crushed iron pipes leaking into it and a rope that found no bottom at 20 feet. Broc clings to a crushed pipe, Francis, Levon, and Allyanna stand nearby, and Vexhar has followed them in. A telepathic voice demands to know why they came, and everyone hears it in their heads. Broc says they only want to fix a plumbing problem, and the voice replies that it merely reminded the old iron of its weakness. It orders them to leave and refuses to let them repair the pipe, and ripples begin spreading from the center of the pool.

## **Negotiating With the Aboleth**

The voice offers the party a choice to stay and serve it in exchange for teaching, or refuse and learn how fragile their lives are. The party declines service, and Broc, Blue, Francis, and Allyanna argue they are new neighbors, with Broc offering the manor’s basement river as a new lair. Paul sets a very high Persuasion DC for Broc, while Blue rolls a 9 on Arcana and senses something evil and wrong. Vexhar rolls well and identifies the creature as an aboleth that is smaller and less developed than expected, and he relays this to the group. Broc withdraws his persuasion and tells the creature the pond is too shallow for it, and the aboleth declares them useless. Broc responds by upcasting Shatter on the ceiling above it, which starts initiative.

## **Combat: Aboleth in the Flooded Chamber**

  - Broc upcasts Shatter on the ceiling, dropping rubble on the aboleth for 14 thunder damage, then casts Mind Sliver and a Quickened Enlarge/Reduce, which the aboleth shrugs off by spending a Legendary Resistance.
  - Francis wades into knee deep water, strikes the aboleth with Talon and a free Divine Smite, and holds position after its first grapple attempt fails to hold him.
  - Blue casts Mirror Image on herself and uses Mantle of Inspiration to give temporary hit points to four allies, then later casts Dissonant Whispers at third level, which the aboleth resists with another Legendary Resistance and takes 8 damage.
  - Allyanna casts Hex and fires Eldritch Blast, then takes a critical tentacle hit for 28 damage when the aboleth grapples and drags both her and Broc into its reach.
  - Vexhar abandons Fireball because of the small room and casts Wither and Bloom instead, and the aboleth saves and takes 3 necrotic damage.
  - The aboleth grapples Francis, who fails an Intelligence save and takes 15 psychic damage, while Broc resists a charm with Fey Ancestry advantage and Allyanna passes her Intelligence save against memory draining.
  - Levon rages, charges in with Choppy for 10 damage, and misses his second throw, after which the aboleth dives and spreads a slime film that only Francis fails to resist, taking 9 acid damage.
  - Francis, still grappled, crits with Talon and a doubled Divine Smite for 55 damage and severs tentacles, and Allyanna then finishes the aboleth with her greatsword as it speaks a final warning that what was broken will be remembered elsewhere.

## **Detect Magic Finds a Pan Flute**

With the chamber quiet, the party starts back through the pipes, and Blue senses a faint magical pull from the wreckage. She casts Detect Magic and finds a pulsing aura beneath a collapsed support, then wades over and digs out a wooden pan flute that hums a faint, unfinished tune. Detect Magic shows a mix of abjuration, transmutation, illusion, and conjuration tangled together in the instrument. Blue pockets the flute to study it above ground, and the spell also shows Francis glowing, which worries the party. Francis scrubs at the slime, Broc tells him to get out of the water, and the group heads back up.

## **Hosing Down at the Ruined Manor**

The party climbs out of the drains to find Cora Ironwell waiting with notes, and Broc tells her an eldritch horror was breaking the pipes and is now dead. Cora says the repairs will take work and she is glad she sent none of her crew down. She lends Broc a hose, and Broc washes Francis down for several minutes, but the ichor stays on him. Blue and Broc examine Francis and find his skin pale, slick, and clinging, with a steady breathing rate, and Paul states it will not fade with rest or showers. Broc casts Detect Magic and senses no magic from Francis’s body, so the group decides to take him to Sister Garaele.

## **Sister Garaele Samples the Ichor**

The party walks through Phandalin to the Shrine of Luck and finds Garaele rushing out of her house toward the funeral at the cemetery. She examines Francis, scrapes a sample of the green ichor into a vial, and says the cause is beyond her and she will take it to the clergy. She advises everyone to avoid touching Francis, so Broc has his Mage Hand wrap Francis in bandages. She expects answers in two to four nights and tells Francis to stay out of trouble. Gundren arrives alone and quiet, without Sildar, Harbin, or his hat, and Blue explains that a slimy eldritch horror in the sewers touched Francis before dying. Garaele also casts Cure Wounds on the injured Allyanna.

## **Healing and Curse Removal Attempts Fail**

Garaele hands Francis a potion of healing via the Mage Hand, but it does nothing, and she explains the ichor stops him from resting and from healing by ordinary means. Dousing him with a jug of water also changes nothing. Allyanna’s Healing Light does heal him, but her Lesser Restoration fails when it fights the affliction and fades. Allyanna’s Arcana check suggests that only magical healing works on him and that something stronger than Lesser Restoration is needed. Blue notes Remove Curse is third level and not on her list, Allyanna’s call to her patron Solace gets no answer, and Francis says he cannot rest.

## **The Festival Arrives at Dusk**

The party prepares to go pay respects at Tharden’s funeral while Francis jokes about attending it smelly. As the last daylight fades, red and cream wagons with swinging lanterns roll along the far road toward an open field. A fiddle and drum sound in the distance, and poles rise to raise a striped red and cream tent. The festival has come to Phandalin.

## **End of Session**

The festival has arrived in Phandalin but will not open for a few days, and Tharden is about to be laid to rest. Francis is wrapped in bandages and still afflicted, unable to rest or heal except by magic, and Garaele’s findings are two to four nights away. The origin of the pan flute is unknown, and the aboleth’s last words that its destroyed body will be remembered elsewhere are unexplained.
