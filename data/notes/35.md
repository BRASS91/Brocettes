## **Overview**

Vexhar stays alone in the Forge of Spells to study it, learns it runs at a fraction of its power, and finds a ledger of lent and sealed items. Four zombies and a ghoul arrive, destroy his zombie of Nezznar, and force him to flee the cave after he casts Darkness. Meanwhile the rest of the party brings Tharden Rockseeker’s body to Phandalin, where Sister Garaele agrees to keep him overnight. The party celebrates at the Stonehill Inn on free drinks, Vexhar arrives with news of the zombies, and Blue casts Charm Person on Francis to get him to bed. The group floats building a bastion at Tresendar Manor, takes a long rest and downtime, and goes apple picking at Daran Edermath’s orchard, ending with six baskets of apples while Harbin Wester still waits.

## **Recap and Heroic Inspiration**

Paul opened with the Question of the Day about spicy food, and each player answered in turn. He checked who held heroic inspiration, the table rolled, and Francis rolled lowest with a 6 and received heroic inspiration. Paul delivered the recap himself because the session opened right after the close of a chapter. The recap covered Wave Echo Cave, the death of Nezznar, the false Nundro, the Forge of Spells, and the loose ends still open for Vexhar, Francis, and Blue. Paul then set the scene with Vexhar alone in the forge.

## **Vexhar Studies the Forge**

Vexhar chose to study the forge and rolled Arcana with advantage for a 29. He learned the flame lends magic drawn up from the cave itself and that the carved lines in the floor were made for a much stronger flow. Paul told him the forge currently runs at about a quarter of its potential. An Investigation check on the scattered papers turned up a ledger with entries marked “lent” and “sealed,” where each sealed entry carried a round stamp. Halvard Price’s name was legible beside the stamps, and other master names had been scratched or burned away.

## **Four Zombies and a Ghoul at the Forge**

  - Vexhar hears the undead coming and casts Darkness over the corridor outside the forge door, where four zombies and a ghoul are packed together.
  - A ghoul reaches Vexhar’s zombie first and destroys it with 32 slashing damage, ending what remained of Nezznar.
  - Vexhar rolls a low initiative and declines to spend heroic inspiration on it.
  - Vexhar learns the undead cannot see in darkness but track him by smell, and he chooses to flee through the other door instead of fighting.
  - Vexhar closes the door as a free action and dashes away, and he considers drinking his invisibility potion, which costs an action.
  - The zombies pile against the weakened door and keep battering it, unaware that Vexhar has slipped past them.
  - Vexhar runs through the flame skull room, holding an action to drink the potion if anything comes near, and the zombies finally break through as the scene ends.

## **Tharden Reaches the Shrine of Luck**

In Phandalin, Francis rode the wolf with Tharden’s body, and the party agreed to take Gundren to the shrine before anything else. Gundren asked Sister Garaele for help, and she agreed to keep Tharden in a quiet room of her house that night. She said the family would decide together tomorrow where he should rest. Francis lowered Tharden into Nundro’s arms, and Nundro stayed with the body. Gundren went to tell Harbin and Sildar everything and asked the party to meet him at the town master’s hall when ready. Francis’s Foundry screen stuck on a black screen during this scene, and Paul restarted the server and later moved his token for him.

## **Free Drinks at the Stonehill Inn**

The common room went quiet when the party entered, and Toblen offered a free first round and free rooms. Broc put down his gold to buy the house a round, and Toblen agreed to comp the table’s drinks. Orders were Redbrand fire brandy for Francis, Miner’s Black Stout for Blue, a tall glass of water with lemon for Allyanna, and a large flagon of Triboar apple cider for Levon. Broc told Toblen that Vexhar had stayed behind in the caves. Toblen said Harbin had been sending a boy to check on them almost every day and that Sildar had been with him since sunrise.

## **Tankard Prank and Letter to Craig**

Allyanna healed Francis, and Toblen asked the party to keep magic to a minimum in the inn. Broc tried to cast Minor Illusion on Francis’s tankard while Blue distracted him, and Francis noticed Broc with a perception roll. Levon tried to help by pushing his flagon at Francis, tripped, and spilled the whole thing on him. Broc asked Toblen to send a letter to the leader of a goblin camp, and Toblen said he would ask the carrier but could promise no delivery. Broc offered extra gold on confirmation of delivery and kept the letter with him.

## **Vexhar Returns With News**

Vexhar arrived at the inn covered in dust, and the party greeted him and shared a pipe. He explained that he ran into undead, that his zombie was lost, and that the zombies are still in the forge. He also told them the forge is not running at full capacity and that he had not yet worked out how to restore it. The papers he was reading were left behind in the forge. The party agreed to tell Gundren and floated sending Craig’s goblins to clear the zombies, as good public relations for the goblins. A man at the bar who bought the table drinks thanked them for dealing with the Redbrands, and Blue’s natural 20 recalled that he had usually been dirty and sooty but now looked dapper.

## **Bastion Plans for Tresendar Manor**

Broc proposed building their bastion in Phandalin, now that the party is level 5. He suggested Tresendar Manor on the hill, and the table liked its defensible position and tunnels. Levon offered to handle the woodworking repairs, and the party discussed buying the manor from the town or raising it with Harbin, who owes them an apology. Broc noted they will receive 10 percent of the forge’s earnings once it is working. Paul confirmed that Phandalin was a booming trade center when the forge was active centuries ago and that the party can build the bastion if they choose.

## **Blue Charms Francis to Bed**

Blue asked Francis’s player for permission to cast Charm Person on Francis, and he agreed. Francis failed the Wisdom save against Blue’s save DC of 15, and Blue walked him to his room and tucked him in. Paul reminded the table that Francis will know he was charmed when the spell ends. Broc passed an Arcana check to recognize what Blue had cast. He thanked Blue for getting Francis to rest, saying Francis forgets he is safe.

## **Broc and Blue Talk About the Feywild**

Broc asked about Blue’s father and brother, and Blue said it was a complicated question. Blue asked what Broc knows of the Feywild, and Broc rolled a 9 on Arcana and recalled only that returning has consequences. Blue said every day she spends here is a day she does not spend there, and she called that a blessing. Broc and Levon both promised nobody would drag her back, and Allyanna added that Blue is underestimating them. Blue answered that they do not know what they would be up against.

## **Night’s End and Downtime**

Broc winked at a woman in the inn and pointed at Levon, and she turned out to be Toblen’s wife and was offended. Levon also burst into the women’s rooms while looking for a free bed. Paul ran the automated downtime system, and Vexhar rolled a natural 20 on his herbalism kit while practicing and training. Vexhar and Francis each earned two research points toward the Forge of Spells. Levon gained nothing but eye strain and a rumor that smells like a trap, and Allyanna learned an account that is only half true. Blue researched things related to herself, and the party took a long rest.

## **Breakfast and the Apple Picking Vote**

The party woke around 7:30 in the morning. Francis remembered he had been charmed, and Blue escaped through a window using Feather Fall. Allyanna beat Francis’s Insight with Persuasion to send him down to eat, and Francis forgave Blue after Broc told him to let it go. Toblen served a large spread, and Levon ate most of the bacon. Toblen mentioned that Harbin’s boy had come at first light again, saying “no rush” twice. Broc then proposed apple picking to make Harbin wait, and the party agreed. Francis tried to leave five gold as a tip, rolled a natural 20 and a natural 1 with disadvantage, and Toblen refused it.

## **Apple Picking at Daran’s Orchard**

Daran Edermath gave the party six baskets and told them not to bruise the fruit or pick the green ones. Allyanna passed a DC 13 Performance check by cheering “apples, apples, apples,” which gave the others advantage. Levon filled a basket with a single shake, Vexhar used Nature to drop apples neatly into his basket, and Broc collected fallen apples with a 20 on Perception. Francis rolled a 26 and tore a whole tree out of the ground and onto the gate, and Blue used Mage Hand to fill her basket. Levon, Broc, and Francis replanted the tree, and Levon’s Nature check showed it should survive with Daran’s care. Daran charged one silver per basket, Levon paid two silver, and Broc covered Allyanna’s.

## **End of Session**

The party walks back toward the Stonehill Inn with six full baskets of apples, and Broc asks what they will do with them. Broc suggests Toblen’s wife might know how to make apple pie. Harbin is still waiting for the party, and the bastion at Tresendar Manor is still only an idea. Vexhar’s undead problem in the Forge of Spells remains unresolved.
