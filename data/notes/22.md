## **Overview**

Session 22 resumed mid-combat inside Cragmaw Castle with Allyanna unconscious on the ground and Francis barely standing after the previous session's brutal exchange with hobgoblins. The party fought through the remaining enemies, with Francis landing the killing blow to end the first combat, then stabilized their fallen members and took a short rest behind a barricaded door. They discovered two voices beyond a sealed chamber to the north, opened negotiations with King Grol and a mysterious gray-skinned elvish woman, and nearly ransomed Gundren Rockseeker for 750 gold and a bracelet before Vexhar fired a Spellfire Flare through the open doorway and collapsed the deal. ith Vicious Mockery before the session ended mid-round, with Gundren on the floor and a direwolf preparing to charge.

## **Broc's Mage Hand Saves Allyanna**

Picking up at the bottom of the prior round, Broc controlled his Mage Hand to deliver a healing potion to Allyanna, who was prone and unconscious on the corridor floor. Allyanna regained consciousness with 7 hit points after Broc rolled the potion manually, and the Mage Hand waved at her and pointed back at Broc. The round closed with the party still in crisis: Blue cornered by a hobgoblin in the corridor, and Francis prone but breathing.

## **Corridor Combat: Bane, Mockery, and Dropping Blue**

Francis rose from prone and cast Bane on the remaining hobgoblins, landing the curse on two of three targets, then retreated through a door. Blue cast Minor Illusion to project a false door in the corridor, buying Francis a round of cover while he moved. Blue's Vicious Mockery on the hobgoblin blocking her failed to rattle him. A hobgoblin then drove Francis down again with a hit of 19, while a second dropped Allyanna a second time with 18 slashing damage while she lay prone. Levon granted Allyanna 9 temporary hit points through his barbarian feature to carry her through until healing arrived. Vexhar deflected a longbow shot with Shield. A hobgoblin then landed a critical hit on Blue for 33 slashing damage, dropping her unconscious. Levon killed that hobgoblin with Choppy, spending his Heroic Inspiration to reroll a failed attack, though the cleave attempt on a second target narrowly missed.

## **Francis Ends the Fight**

With Allyanna back on her feet from Francis's last Lay on Hands point, Francis stepped over her, swung on the final hobgoblin, and the DM called for a kill description. Still weak and barely able to lift his sword, Francis caught the hobgoblin in the gap beneath his armor, drove the blade in, and the sword pulled free of his hands as the body dropped, remaining lodged in the corpse. The DM took the party out of combat. Allyanna was up; Blue remained unconscious and stable on the floor.

## **Stabilizing Blue**

With combat over, Francis used his healer's kit on Blue, rolling a failure that Heroic Inspiration turned into a success, stabilizing her. Blue no longer needed to make death saves but remained unconscious. Allyanna then cast Cure Wounds on Blue, bringing her back to consciousness. Blue woke gasping, looked at Broc and Vexhar, and told them both to go to hell for their performance in the corridor.

## **Short Rest Behind the Barricade**

The party retreated into the cleared room and agreed on a short rest. Levon stacked boulders and debris against the door while the others argued about next steps. The castle settled into uneasy silence during the hour, broken only by the distant sound of stone shifting and braziers crackling. Blue spent the rest using Minor Illusion to conjure caricature projections of the party and attack them with small fists, a detail Levon observed with mild concern. The party spent available Hit Dice, Allyanna recovered her spell slots, and Levon's rage ended, removing any temporary hit points tied to it.

## **Listening at the North Door: King Grol Identified**

Once the short rest concluded, the party moved to explore the uncleared portions of the castle. Francis listened at an eastern door and heard only the sound of a smoldering fire inside an empty-seeming room with straw pallets. Levon pressed an ear to a second door further north and heard two voices in muffled conversation: a deep, rough, angry voice and a second that was calm and smooth. Levon caught one distinct phrase through the wood before Vexhar shouted "murder" into the corridor, silencing whatever was happening on the other side instantly. Levon quietly told the group he believed King Grol was behind the door.

## **Negotiating with King Grol**

Levon knocked on the north door and improvised a story about being lost, drawing a curt reply from the rough voice to chain his "mental patient" and stay away. Broc took over as speaker, claiming through the door that the party had been sent by the Black Spider to collect Gundren Rockseeker. The exchange that followed was extended: King Grol was suspicious, the calmer elvish voice urged caution, and Broc offered 750 gold pieces and a bracelet as ransom. King Grol weighed the offer, announced his terms, leave the gold and the bracelet, take the dwarf, and walk out without tricks, blades, or spells, then told Broc to show his face. Broc opened the door.

## **King Grol's Chamber and the Deal's Collapse**

The room revealed thick furs on stone floors, hunting trophies on the walls, and a battered Gundren Rockseeker unconscious on the ground, his face bruised and clothes torn. King Grol stood over him: an aging bugbear with gray-streaked fur, stooped shoulders, and silver-capped fangs. A massive wolf fixed its gaze on Broc from King Grol's side. A gray-skinned, white-haired elvish woman in simple robes watched from the north wall throughout, her expression unreadable. Francis rushed into the room behind Broc. As King Grol acknowledged the payment and the deal appeared to close, Vexhar fired a Spellfire Flare past Levon's attempted restraint, striking King Grol for 12 radiant damage. The elvish woman stepped back and said "So much for negotiation." Combat resumed.

## **Combat in King Grol's Chamber**

  - As the elvish woman repositioned away from the center of the room at the top of initiative, Francis took an opportunity attack on her with his Talon Dagger, landing a critical strike for 9 damage; she absorbed part of it, winced briefly, and continued to the back wall without breaking composure.
  - Francis marked King Grol with Vow of Enmity, struck with Talon, and added a free Divine Smite for 16 total damage with 4 radiant, bloodying the bugbear and staggering him hard against the furs.
  - Francis moved south and found Gundren unconscious on the floor beside him.
  - Blue, unable to see into the room, targeted King Grol with Vicious Mockery using the "creature you can hear" clause, rolling maximum damage of 6 psychic and imposing disadvantage on his next attack.
  - King Grol failed the Wisdom save, staggered, clutched at his head as the mockery took hold, and collapsed across the furs: his knees buckled, his jeweled fangs went dark, and Blue secured her second kill-by-words of the campaign.
  - The session ended mid-round with King Grol dead, the direwolf turning toward the corridor and preparing to lunge, and the elvish woman alive and unreadable at the north wall.

## **End of Session**

The session ended mid-combat in King Grol's chamber. Gundren Rockseeker is visible on the floor, alive but badly beaten and unconscious. King Grol is dead. The direwolf that served him has turned its attention toward the party and appears ready to attack. The gray-skinned elvish woman who had been advising King Grol retreated to the north wall and remains an unresolved threat. The party has not yet secured Gundren or cleared the room, and they are heading into level 4.
