## **Releasing Caine at the Cave Entrance**

The party responds to the hostage situation by backing off and trying to talk the surviving enemies into leaving instead of killing Caine. Francis and Broc help steer the exchange while the others hold position and avoid provoking a shot. During the negotiation, the orc reveals that he has seen a red-robed man with undead at old ruins nearby, giving the group a new lead. The enemies accept a narrow escape, release Caine, and retreat north rather than continuing the fight. Once the orc and bugbear flee, the party regains Caine alive and secures the area around the cave mouth.

## **Caine Leaves the Party**

After the immediate danger passes, Caine tells the group that he cannot keep living this way and that this is where he steps away from the road. He explains that there are people who still need him at the monastery and that rebuilding has not even begun, which makes him realize he should never have left them behind. The party accepts his decision, thanks him for everything he has done, and shares a brief farewell before he turns back down the trail. Caine also keeps his share of the money so it can go toward rebuilding the monastery rather than staying with the adventuring group. By the end of the scene, he has fully departed, leaving the remaining party members to continue without him.

## **Searching the Wyvern Tor Cave**

With Caine gone, the party enters the cave and searches the marauders’ camp for anything useful. Inside, they find a rough but lived-in space along with a locked chest that clearly held the group’s valuables. They also discover a crude wall drawing that appears to show one of the strange goblins the party has been discussing, including a crystal protruding from its skull. That image reinforces the idea that these goblins are tied to a larger and more unusual threat. Craig manages to open the chest, giving the party access to a cache of treasure and magical gear.

## **Magic Items from the Chest**

The party divides the key magical items from the chest after identifying what they do. Broc takes a **Sylvan Talon Dagger**, which helps him communicate with Fey and carry short magical messages. Craig receives a **Cloak of the Manta Ray**, giving him strong underwater mobility and the ability to breathe below the surface. Francis takes **Dust of Dryness**, a packet of magical dust that can absorb large amounts of water and can also harm water-based creatures. Levon claims a **Candle of the Deep**, a candle that continues burning even underwater. Vexhar takes a **Hat of Wizardry**, which works as a wizard focus and gives him a chance to cast a wizard cantrip he does not already know.

## **Goblin Ambush Near Old Owl Well**

  - As the party closes in on Old Owl Well, goblins hidden among rocks and brush spring an ambush and force the group into another roadside fight.
  - Francis pushes forward under heavy arrow fire, absorbs several hits, and keeps pressure on the goblins while trying to break their line.
  - Craig uses cover effectively, lands a clean shot that drops one goblin early, and helps thin the group before they can reposition.
  - Levon enters a rage and keeps advancing into melee, turning himself into the party’s main answer to the goblins once the distance closes.
  - Vexhar supports from range with offensive spells, burning down one goblin and helping wear down the enemy leader.
  - Broc fires off Magic Missile at the goblin boss, then triggers Wild Magic and vanishes into the Astral Plane for a turn before suddenly returning to the battlefield.
  - Francis finishes the goblin leader with a thrown javelin, and Levon cuts down the last fleeing goblin when it tries to escape.

## **End of Session**

The session ends with the goblin ambush defeated and the party continuing on without Caine after his departure. They are only a few minutes from Old Owl Well, where the orc said a red-robed man with undead had been seen. The immediate unresolved goal is reaching the ruins and learning what is waiting there.
