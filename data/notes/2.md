## **Overview**

The session begins at the roadside ambush, where the party subdues Craig and questions him about the missing dwarf and armored traveler, learning both were taken alive to Klarg’s hideout several miles north. The group tends wounds, negotiates Craig’s cooperation, hides their wagon and horses, and then travels on foot up the narrow goblin trail under Craig’s warnings about traps. Francis scouts ahead and detects a snare and a concealed pit, which he safely triggers with a thrown stone before the party continues toward the cave. At the cave mouth, the group is ambushed by goblins hidden among the shadows, resulting in a chaotic fight involving healing, spellcasting, melee pressure, and the goblin boss’s redirect ability. After defeating the ambushers and glimpsing wolves in a side chamber, the party decides they are too wounded and low on resources to delve deeper toward Klarg. They withdraw to a safer point along the trail and set up a forest camp, assigning watches and settling in for a long rest.

## **Interrogating Craig and Managing the Ambush Site**

The party gathers around Craig as Levon restrains him, prompting discussions about who should question the goblin and who is most intimidating. Through Broc’s translations, Craig reveals that Klarg ordered the ambushes and that the “tiny dwarf and big armor guy” were taken alive to the hideout. Caine insists on “payment” before healing Craig, leading to a negotiation culminating in Craig telling a joke, which Caine accepts before patching him with a healer’s kit. The party also searches the slain goblins, identifying only crude scimitars and shortbows and adding them to their shared loot. They confirm that Craig fears Klarg’s retaliation if seen with them and determine that he will guide them only as far as the entrance before hiding.

## **Hiding the Wagon and Proceeding on Foot**

Before leaving the roadway, Francis calms the horses, ties them to a tree behind the wagon, and camouflages both among the brush, while Broc assists with a Minor Illusion to distract the animals. The group considers the narrow terrain and agrees the wagon cannot follow them, making it necessary to proceed on foot. Levon and others check the fallen goblins’ gear one last time, finding nothing useful beyond what they already collected. With Craig healed and now unrestrained but closely supervised, the group selects a marching order and begins following the trail northward. As they depart, Craig repeatedly warns them that “the path is heavily trapped,” encouraging Francis to keep a close eye on the ground ahead.

## **Navigating the Trapped Trail**

Following Craig’s warnings, Francis makes careful checks and soon identifies a nearly invisible snare and a suspicious patch of earth covering a pit. The party halts while he examines the signs of disturbed soil, faint tension in the ropes, and the placement of branches concealing the hazard. To avoid risking a fall, Francis throws a heavy stone onto the weak spot, triggering the mechanism and revealing a ten-foot pit beneath. Discussion follows on how to maneuver around it given the thick underbrush, and the party ultimately bypasses the trap by hugging the edges of the trail and guiding Craig through the safe line. Once past the danger, they continue north with renewed caution.

## **Approaching the Cave Entrance**

Eventually, the trail leads them to a cave carved into a wooded hillside, where a shallow stream emerges and dark interior passages stretch beyond sight. The party positions characters with darkvision near the front and remarks on the difficulty of seeing inside, especially for their human members. Craig reiterates that the hideout lies within and mentions wolves in a side chamber, though he refuses to go any closer for fear of being killed by Klarg’s forces. As they ready themselves and move forward to inspect the cave, motion and sound suggest hidden figures nearby. Their cautious advance triggers an ambush, and combat begins.

## **Ambush at the Cave Mouth**

  - Hidden goblins fire from the brush and cave shadows, striking Broc and others and forcing the party into immediate defensive repositioning.
  - Caine drags Broc out of the front line and casts Healing Word, restoring enough vitality to keep Broc conscious and active in the ensuing fight.
  - Broc blasts a goblin with Magic Missile earlier in the encounter, contributing ranged damage while repositioning to avoid further ranged fire from the ambushers.
  - Francis engages goblins in the shallow stream, trading melee attacks and later casting Command to force a fleeing goblin to grovel, stopping his escape and turning the momentum.
  - Broc, keeping some distance, spots wolves in a side cave with his elven eyes as he moves and shouts a warning to Levon to get out of there.
  - Levon pushes into the cave entrance, absorbing attacks, helping hold the choke point, and blocking enemies attempting to retreat deeper inside.
  - During a later attack on the goblin boss, the boss uses Redirect to grab a nearby goblin and throw him into the line of fire of Vexhar’s fire bolt, causing the minion to take the incoming damage instead.
  - The combined pressure of melee, spells, and healing eventually breaks the ambushers’ morale, and the remaining goblins are killed or routed, leaving the cave entrance temporarily secure.

## **Retreating and Making Camp**

After securing the area, the party discusses their remaining health, spells, and resources and determines they cannot safely press deeper into the hideout. Craig provides additional information about wolves and passages inside, confirming the danger of continuing without rest. The group debates short rest versus long rest mechanics, noting limited recovery options, before agreeing to retreat to a safer location along the trail. They move away from the cave, find a defensible campsite, and decide to light a fire for visibility despite potential risks. Watches are assigned between party members, and the forest quiets as they settle in, ending the night with Craig tied and monitored while everyone prepares for a long rest.

## **End of Session**

By the session’s end, the party has retreated from the cave and established a campsite, complete with assigned watches and a lit fire to support those without darkvision. Their wagon and horses remain hidden far behind them, and Craig stays in custody as they plan to confront Klarg after resting. The goblins, wolves, and deeper tunnels of the hideout remain unexplored, and the fate of the abducted dwarf and armored human is still uncertain. The session closes as the party prepares to complete their long rest and return to the hideout at dawn.
