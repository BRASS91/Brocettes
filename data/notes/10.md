## **Telepathic Intrusion in the Crevasse**

Craig sets the scene in the cold cavern by the dark crevasse as a formless mental voice reminds the party they are “louder in here than you think,” drawing their attention to an unseen watcher. Vexhar recognizes the telepathy as the work of something innately powerful, while Francis is singled out as a “soldier” whose vigilance once slipped, forcing him to relive a painful failure in a forced wisdom save. When Vexhar demands the entity show itself instead of hiding behind magic, a hunched, clawed figure with a single unblinking eye steps from behind a stone column so everyone can see it. Caine immediately invokes the Earth Mother with Channel Divinity to turn undead, learning through the creature’s mocking telepathic reply that it is no shambling corpse, only a being obsessed with their secrets. The group begins cautiously probing it with questions, testing whether this thing can be bargained with rather than fought outright.

## **Warped Goblins and Ssarnak’s Bargain**

Broc climbs down into the crevasse and discovers goblin corpses with elongated skulls, vicious claw marks, and chunks of missing flesh, confirming that some monster has been feeding here. When Francis and Craig ask what lies below, the creature—calling itself Come-Later or Ssarnak—admits it “solved” goblin trouble and turned them into food, further unsettling the party. Later, after skirmishing and retreating, Ssarnak reappears fully healed, telepathically insisting it needs only blood and offering a new deal: if they slay the red-cloaked thugs, he will claim their bodies and will not hunt the party. Francis pushes back with a powerful intimidation attempt, demanding that the game of half-truths end and that Ssarnak tell them what they need to know. In response, the Nothic finally gives more concrete help, pointing out that the wizard’s study lies to the south, hinting at skeleton-guarded crypts, and then descending to reveal a shining chest in the crevasse that it offers in exchange for more “food.”

## **Battle with Ssarnak the Nothic**

  - Caine calls on the Earth Mother with Channel Divinity, attempting to turn the creature and learning from its rasping telepathic reply that it is no undead but something that hungers for their secrets instead.
  - Vexhar eventually opens hostilities with a Fire Bolt, provoking Ssarnak to fight back, and later suffers the creature’s rotting gaze, taking heavy damage before it slips into the shadows to reposition.
  - Francis closes for melee in the renewed clash, swinging his longsword in the narrow space around the crevasse and repeatedly trying to land solid blows as the Nothic clings to walls and columns.
  - Broc hammers Ssarnak with Magic Missile during the later exchange, steadily eroding its remaining vitality when ranged spells become the safest way to hurt the creature.
  - Levon and Craig support with jumps across the gap, handaxes, and ranged attacks, helping box the monster in until the party’s combined pressure finally brings it down and removes it as a threat in the caverns.

## **Treasure Chest and Tactical Debates**

Once Ssarnak is gone, the party pulls the chest from the crevasse and opens it, finding malachite gems, a potion of healing, and a finely worked longsword later recognized as Talon, a legendary blade tied to Aldith Tresendar. Francis claims the sword and learns it is a +1 weapon that will require attunement, while the group moves the gems into party loot and hands the new potion to Vexhar after his earlier injuries. They pause to tally spell slots, scrolls, potions, and healing feats, weighing whether to press directly toward the wizard or withdraw before they are overwhelmed. Concerns about exhausted casters, the wizard possibly fleeing, and the world moving on without them drive a lengthy tactical discussion, but the group ultimately leans toward securing a safer position rather than risking a depleted assault.

## **Downtime in Town and Bugbear Greeting**

The group returns to town, where a friendly NPC provides pipeweed and they enjoy a brief, mellow interlude at the tavern before heading back to their headquarters. Over a long rest, Broc brews a healing potion using party funds while Vexhar watches and begins training with the alchemy kit, and Francis and Vexhar also pursue downtime training in skills like Deception. Levon spends part of the night earning coin and then chooses to practice acrobatics, flavoring it as flashy axe work, while others tend wounds or secure their base. After the rest, they return to the manor via their known route and descend into a cellar room where four bugbears are already waiting, leading to a tense “new recruit” bluff that quickly unravels. The bugbears cite orders from “the wizard” to kill unfamiliar intruders, and the moment one is slain, the rest commit fully to a brutal fight on and around the stairs.

## **Bugbear and Redbrand Battle on Stairs**

  - As the ruse fails, Caine steps forward and smashes the bugbear leader Nosk’s skull with his mace, killing him in a single, vividly described blow that momentarily shakes the confidence of the remaining guards.
  - Francis fights on the front line with his new longsword and radiant magic, soaking and dealing damage while trying to keep the bugbears and red-cloaked thugs from overrunning the party’s position on the stairs.
  - Vexhar repeatedly rings out tolling magic over the melee, attempting to land Toll the Dead on the toughest bugbear and forcing the enemies to split focus between the martial frontliners and the spellcasting backline.
  - Broc and Craig remain in the rear, with Broc later stepping out to land a clutch Mind Sliver that kills a wounded foe instantly before retreating back behind Craig’s cover fire.
  - Levon holds the middle of the staircase with wide axe swings and blocks, helping bottle enemies up even as surviving bugbears and red-cloaked humans trade blows and arguments about the wizard’s orders, leaving the battle unresolved at session’s end.

## **End of Session**

The session closes with the party still in the thick of the staircase battle against bugbears and red-cloaked thugs inside the hideout. Several enemies have fallen, including the bugbear Nosk and at least one other foe dropped by Broc’s Mind Sliver, but multiple opponents remain engaged with the front line. The heroes have resources left but are clearly in a dangerous position, and the wizard who commands these guards is still unaccounted for somewhere deeper in the complex. Open questions include whether the remaining enemies can be broken or convinced to stand down and how quickly the wizard will react to the ongoing fighting.
