## **The Hostage on the Ledge**

The session opened with Yeemik holding Sildar Hallwinter at knifepoint on a narrow ledge overlooking the cave floor. Sildar was bound, unconscious, and bleeding out as Yeemik warned the party not to advance. Initiative was suspended to allow negotiation as Yeemik demanded to know who the party was and why Sildar mattered to them.

The party identified Sildar as their employer and attempted to negotiate his release. Yeemik remained calm but aggressive, repeatedly emphasizing how little time Sildar had left and how easily he could be killed if the party made a wrong move. The physical positioning made any sudden action extremely dangerous, with Sildar’s body already partially over the ledge.

## **Negotiation Breakdown**

Multiple party members attempted persuasion and intimidation to defuse the situation. Yeemik rejected early offers and asserted full control, pressing his advantage by describing Sildar’s injuries and tightening his grip. During the exchange, Yeemik spoke openly about Klarg and King Grol, stating that Klarg answered to stronger powers and that goblin leadership extended beyond the cave.

He denied being controlled himself, insisting that he followed power where it was strongest. In doing so, he referenced the Spider as a real and active figure influencing Cragmaw operations. This marked the first clear indication that the goblins were acting as part of a larger and coordinated effort rather than simple raiders.

As the exchange dragged on, Yeemik visibly lost patience. He pressed the blade deeper and deliberately shifted Sildar’s weight toward the drop, signaling that the standoff was reaching its breaking point.

## **Combat on the Ledge**

The party executed a coordinated attempt to save Sildar.

  - Caine cast Healing Word on Sildar while Broc created a Minor Illusion to distract Yeemik
  - Yeemik noticed the healing magic and immediately slashed Sildar’s throat, triggering initiative
  - Caine followed with Guiding Bolt, which missed Yeemik
  - Levon entered a rage and rushed Yeemik, engaging him at close range on the ledge
  - Francis cast Command, forcing Yeemik to grovel and release his grip on Sildar
  - Additional goblins rushed into the chamber as the fight broke out
  - The party prevented Sildar from falling and secured control of the ledge

The confined terrain and vertical danger made the opening rounds especially volatile.

## **Securing Sildar**

With Yeemik forced prone, Sildar collapsed onto the ledge, barely conscious and still critically injured. The party repositioned immediately to shield him as goblin reinforcements pressed in. Yeemik, now badly wounded and stripped of leverage, attempted to bargain for his life. He offered information about Klarg and claimed he could turn against him if spared.

The party restrained Yeemik rather than immediately killing him and finished securing the chamber. Once the immediate threat was contained, Sildar was stabilized. Though weak, he confirmed that Yeemik’s mention of the Spider aligned with what he already suspected, reinforcing that the goblins were being directed by forces beyond the hideout.

Despite his condition, Sildar urged the party to press on, warning that leaving Klarg alive would allow the goblins to reorganize and strike back.

## **The Inner Cave Fights**

Pushing deeper into the hideout, the party encountered additional goblins guarding interior passages. These goblins fought defensively, using narrow tunnels, cover, and choke points in an attempt to slow the party’s advance. Some attempted to flee or reposition, but coordinated pressure prevented them from escaping or warning Klarg in time.

These skirmishes steadily drained party resources and reinforced that the hideout remained dangerous even as goblin morale began to crack. Each cleared passage reduced resistance and closed off escape routes, eventually leading the party to the final chamber.

## **The Bugbear Den**

The party entered Klarg’s den and immediately identified him as the central authority of the Cragmaw goblins. Klarg fought alongside his trained wolf, attempting to overwhelm the party with brute force. The wolf was killed during the fight, significantly reducing the pressure on the party.

As the battle turned against him, Klarg attempted to flee through the chimney leading out of the den. He became stuck while trying to escape, leaving him exposed and unable to maneuver. Trapped and unable to retreat, Klarg was killed by the party, ending his leadership in a moment of desperation rather than dominance.

## **End of Session**

With Klarg dead and Yeemik neutralized, the Cragmaw hideout fell silent. Remaining goblins scattered or surrendered, unable to mount further resistance. The party secured the cave and assessed the aftermath of the fighting.

Sildar remained alive but critically injured, his survival owed entirely to the party’s actions during the standoff. Yeemik’s statements and the evidence within the den raised unresolved questions about King Grol and the Spider, making it clear that this victory had exposed a far larger threat.

The session ended with the party in control of the hideout, aware that Klarg’s death was not the end of the conflict, but the beginning of a much wider struggle.
