## **Descending Into the Pit**

Gundren voiced fear of losing another brother while Francis reassured him they would find Nundro. Broc listened again for the sound he had heard earlier from the campsite but picked up nothing beyond the ordinary noises of the cave. The party found the pit rigged with a sturdy hemp rope tied to a stalagmite and combined it with two collapsible ladders to fashion a twenty foot descent. Gundren followed them down, impressed that the group had come prepared. At the bottom, the air grew colder and tighter, and the party found a body slumped against the wall amid collapsed rubble.

## **The Unfamiliar Goblin Corpse**

Vexhar, Allyanna, and Blue examined the body and recognized it as goblin like but unlike any Cragmaw goblin they had fought, with an elongated skull and faint green streaks in its skin matching descriptions from Sildar and the Kragmaws. Broc investigated the scene and determined the creature had likely died from blunt force trauma after falling or being pushed from the cliff above. No blood or stolen belongings were found nearby. The pit opened into two tunnels, one heading northwest and one east, forcing the party to pick a direction.

## **Splitting Up and Marking the Way**

The party flipped a coin and chose to head east together rather than fully divide. The passage opened into a roughly thirty foot cavern carved with reliefs of dwarf and gnome miners, littered with two dozen skeletons, rusted armor, and unlit brass lanterns. Broc remarked that the space felt more like a tomb than a mine. Blue arranged bones from the skeletons into an arrow pointing back toward the entrance so the party could find its way out later.

## **Stirge Swarm in the Miners' Cavern**

  - Six stirges dropped from the ceiling, catching Blue, Broc, and Vexhar off guard while Levon and Allyanna spotted them in time to act normally.
  - The first stirge latched onto Broc's thigh; Francis used his Sentinel reaction to cut it in half before it could be pulled loose.
  - Levon's lightning retaliation feature killed multiple stirges the instant they attached to him, prompting jokes about him being a living bug zapper.
  - Allyanna repositioned to a ledge for a clear angle and killed a stirge latched onto Levon with an Eldritch Blast.
  - Vexhar and Broc both fired bolts at stirges but missed their targets.
  - Levon, bloodied from repeated bites, declined to use a healing potion mid fight, choosing to save it and trust the party's healers afterward.
  - Francis and Levon finished off the last two stirges together, Francis striking one with Talon while Levon's cleaving swing with Choppy took out the other.
  - Allyanna used Lay on Hands and Healing Light to heal Levon and Broc after the fight, bringing Levon out of bloodied.

## **Two Doors and a Wrecked Barracks**

The party reached a dead end corridor south of the cavern with two doors, one east and one west. Levon inspected the eastern door, found it made of thick stone, and pushed it open to reveal a room wrecked by a ceiling collapse, containing splintered stone benches, rusted weapon racks, and eight corpses, two of them ogre sized. Broc noted the ogres had been dead for at least a couple of weeks while Blue stepped in to look around. As the party entered, all eight corpses lurched upright at once, rising as ogre and dwarf zombies.

## **Undead Guardroom Ambush**

  - Broc cast Enlarge on Levon before disengaging behind cover, boosting Levon's size and damage right as the fight began.
  - Levon, raging and enlarged, charged and landed a critical hit on an ogre zombie for 55 points of damage, then cleaved into a second ogre zombie for 35 more; Undead Fortitude kept both standing.
  - Eliana revealed a previously unused greatsword through Pact of the Blade and decapitated one dwarf zombie, later helping finish a second alongside Francis's opportunity attack that halted it mid charge.
  - Vexhar's chromatic orb bounced between three targets in succession, dealing thunder, fire, and cold damage across the ogre zombie and two dwarf zombies.
  - Blue's Vicious Mockery failed to land against the zombies twice, and Blue instead granted Allyanna bardic inspiration and stayed near Broc.
  - Gundren twice distracted advancing zombies by waving and shouting to draw their attention away from the wounded party.
  - Allyanna's staff granted shield spell repeatedly turned potentially lethal hits into misses, protecting her from two zombies attacking at once.
  - An ogre zombie critically struck Francis for 31 points of bludgeoning damage, knocking him unconscious as the session ended mid combat with one ogre zombie still standing due to Undead Fortitude.

## **End of Session**

The session ended mid fight in the wrecked guardroom, with Francis knocked unconscious by a critical hit from the sole remaining ogre zombie while the rest of the undead lay cleared around him. The party chose to stop rather than push through the final round, planning to resume at the top of round three with Allyanna acting first. Nundro remains unaccounted for deeper in Wave Echo Cave, and Francis's condition is the party's immediate concern heading into the next session.
