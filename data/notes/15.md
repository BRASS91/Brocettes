## **Old Owl Well and Hamun Kost**

The party approached the ruins of Old Owl Well expecting trouble and quickly drew the attention of the undead inside the broken tower. Once the zombies were held off, a red-robed man named Hamun Kost emerged from the tent in the courtyard and established that the undead were under his control. The group explained they were looking for information connected to Cragmaw Castle, while Kost made clear that he was studying the well and had also been watching the goblins in the area. He described a nearby cave used by goblins, bugbears, and a humanlike figure, and he offered directions in exchange for a favor. The party accepted the arrangement, took the marked parchment he provided, and chose to follow the goblin lead before seeking out Agatha.

## **Battle at Old Owl Well**

  - Zombies poured out of the tower as the party crossed the courtyard, forcing Francis, Levon, Broc, Vexhar, and Craig into a sudden fight at close range.
  - Francis cut into the first zombie and held position while more undead rushed from the tower to surround the group.
  - Levon used reckless strength and Choppy to drop a zombie in one swing, helping keep the front line from collapsing.
  - Broc misty stepped away from immediate danger and then landed a critical Sorcerous Burst that shook one zombie badly.
  - Vexhar cast *Shatter* into the clustered undead and dropped several at once, though a few kept moving even after the blast.
  - Craig’s first arrow passed harmlessly through a lanky corpse, but the party stayed upright long enough for the fight to shift into negotiation when Kost stopped the remaining zombies.

## **Cave Entrance and Common Room**

At the cave entrance, the party had the advantage over two goblin watchers but abandoned stealth when Levon shouted at them and started the confrontation openly. One goblin briefly grabbed Choppy after Levon’s thrown attack missed, but Craig shot that goblin down and Broc dropped the runner before it could escape deeper into the cave. Inside, the group used light sources to advance through the dark passage and listened at the first major chamber before opening the door. They found wolves, armed raiders, and a lived-in common room, then chose to use the doorway as a choke point rather than rush the whole room. The ensuing fight stayed messy, but the tactic limited how many enemies could reach them at once and let the party grind the room down while enemies tried to call for help.

## **Fighting Through the Hideout**

  - Francis opened the common-room fight by hurling a javelin into a raider and then held the doorway so the enemy line could not flood the tunnel.
  - Levon raged, traded repeated blows with the frontliners, and kept pressure on the doorway despite taking several retaliatory punches.
  - Broc landed two major chromatic orb blasts during the hideout assault, including one that critically burned a raider and another that later vaporized the ballista operator after Brock took a heavy bolt to the chest.
  - Vexhar supported the choke point with magic, including *Toll the Dead*, while Craig picked off targets with his bow and called out what he could see.
  - The raiders awakened both wolves, one enemy escaped through an inner door and locked it, and another guard later revealed a mounted ballista in a side room.
  - After breaking through to the next section, the party opened a prison chamber, found more guards, and saw two prisoners behind bars just as the fight expanded again.

## **Prison Cells Revealed**

The prison room changed the situation immediately because the party now knew captives were still alive inside the hideout. Blue and Allyanna were introduced as the two prisoners, both stripped of their equipment and trapped behind bars when the battle reached them. Allyanna took the first opening she had and fired *Eldritch Blast* from inside her cell at the guard who had been watching her. Blue was also ready at the bars as the rescue fight began, but the cell doors were still shut and the prisoners were not yet freed. Before the chamber could be secured, an inner door opened and a calm man with a staff stepped out to confront the intruders.

## **End of Session**

The party ended the session mid-combat inside the prison area with enemies still standing, Blue and Allyanna still locked in cells, and Craig poisoned. The man who appeared from the inner chambers implied the group should have stayed in Phandalin, strongly signaling that the confrontation with Glasstaff is beginning. The immediate unresolved goals are to survive the current fight, free both prisoners, and deal with the leader who has finally shown himself.
