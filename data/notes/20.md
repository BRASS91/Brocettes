## **Holding the Outside Wall**

Levon worked at the locked door with his greataxe, trying to split it at the hinges, but could not break through before goblin archers inside began firing through the arrow slits. Two arrows hit him through the narrow openings, both reduced to minimal damage by his rage, while a third goblin fired on Francis and missed. Vexhar attempted to push Poison Spray through an arrow slit using his Hat of Wizardry but succeeded only in coating the outside wall with the effect, wasting the attempt. Allyanna threaded Cloud of Daggers through a separate slit, summoning the spell blindly into the interior and listening as a goblin stumbled into it and died.

## **Blue Unlocks the Door and Francis Readies**

Francis surrendered his readied attack action to give Blue advantage on a DC 18 Sleight of Hand check to pick the castle door's lock. Blue pressed her ear to the wood, slid her thieves' tools in, and managed the lock on a second attempt, triggering a brief moment of distress tied to memories of sneaking through the Feywild. After succeeding, she stepped back and gave Francis her last bardic inspiration before repositioning. The goblin inside immediately heard the tampering and attempted to re-lock the door, but the mechanism was too damaged to reset, leaving it permanently open.

## **Chromatic Orb Wild Magic Surge**

With the door still closed at the time, Broc fired a Chromatic Orb through an arrow slit, burning through two heroic inspiration and a luck point before landing the shot. The orb struck a goblin for 27 points of thunder damage and then bounced automatically to a second target, which a goblin boss shoved a smaller goblin into rather than absorb himself. The bounced orb killed the substitute for 18 additional points. Broc's wild magic surge activated on the cast, granting maximum damage on his next damage-dealing spell. The table called this the highlight of the session.

## **Levon Charges the Open Door**

When Blue unlocked the door, Levon burst it open and walked directly into a corridor of five goblins with readied shortbow shots. All five fired, collectively dealing only two points of piercing damage to him as round after round of arrows flew wide or glanced off. The table erupted at the string of misses, with one goblin even muttering in Goblin that it could not understand why the giant would not fall. Levon swung back with a reckless attack that missed, then threw a hand axe blind into the dark corridor with disadvantage, which also missed, leaving him wounded but standing as the goblins scattered into the interior.

## **Francis Pursues Into the Castle**

Francis pushed through a steel door into a narrow connecting hall and caught the goblin named Yeg who had wounded him earlier trying to turn a doorknob to escape. He struck Yeg with Talon with advantage from his Vow of Enmity, badly injuring him but choosing not to spend a Divine Smite. On Yeg's turn, the goblin missed Francis twice with disadvantage, disengaged, and fled through the steel door into a room Francis could see was some kind of former chapel or shrine with a brazier and an altar along one wall. Allyanna blasted another goblin with Eldritch Blast, repelling it back into a wall, and Blue finished a near-death goblin with Vicious Mockery targeting its mother, which the table agreed was more effective than the previous mother-targeted phrasing.

## **Levon Takes Heavy Fire and the Corridor Clears**

In the later rounds, a goblin in the dark corridor landed a major arrow shot on Levon for 23 piercing damage, halved to 11 by rage, leaving him bloodied and with an arrow in his chest. A second hit followed for three more points. Vexhar readied a Fireball to fire if anything emerged from the northern corridor while Broc readied a Sorcerous Burst to cover the same lane. Allyanna moved the Cloud of Daggers and Francis finished off the last defenders in the interior hall, clearing the banquet room entirely of living enemies.

## **Broc Discovers the Reinforcements**

Broc dashed through the cleared interior toward the southwest tower, spotted three recently used bedrolls in a ruined room, and pressed on into the north corridor. He came face to face with a goblin who had been under his Phantasmal Force effect the entire fight, still half-convinced an ogre was somewhere nearby. Behind the goblin stood a line of hobgoblins and goblin bosses. The hobgoblin in command slapped the terrified goblin and told him Broc was not an ogre. Broc used his free action to slam the door shut as the new group entered initiative. The session ended there with the party split, Levon badly wounded, most spell slots and bardic inspiration expended, and a fresh wave of enemies on the other side of a closed door.

## **End of Session**

The party holds the cleared banquet hall and connecting corridors of Cragmaw Castle but is in poor shape heading into the next fight. Levon is bloodied with an arrow wound, most casters are low on resources, and Blue has no bardic inspiration remaining. Broc is positioned at a closed door with an unknown number of hobgoblins and goblin bosses waiting on the other side. Gundren Rockseeker has not been found, and his location within the castle remains unknown.
