## **Prison Chamber Showdown**

  - Glasstaff opened by trying to lock down Levon and later Blue with magic, but both of them broke free of his control attempts and kept the pressure on him.
  - Craig fought through poison, failed to stay hidden for long, and still landed an important arrow into the venom priest who had targeted him earlier.
  - Blue insulted Glasstaff, kept his attention split, and used charm to manipulate one of the drow guards into opening a cell before that guard died realizing what had happened.
  - Broc used Mage Hand to grab the dropped keys and unlock Allyanna’s cell, then rushed Glasstaff and helped force the fight into a tighter corner.
  - Vexhar finished off the wounded venom priest with Fire Bolt and kept pressure on the field while Francis stepped up as the party’s main close-range threat.
  - Levon cut down the charmed drow, then later got his hands on Glasstaff and kept him grappled when the wizard tried to escape.
  - Allyanna joined the fight as soon as she was free, while Francis closed in with divine pressure and the whole group boxed Glasstaff in even after he teleported across the chamber.

## **Pressing Glasstaff for Answers**

With Glasstaff trapped and outnumbered, the party dropped out of initiative and demanded answers instead of ending the fight immediately. He admitted that Gundren was likely at Cragmaw Castle and, after Vexhar magically pressured him, marked its location on a parchment. Glasstaff described the castle as hidden northwest of the Triboar Trail in Neverwinter Wood and warned that his own operation was not the center of the larger threat. The group also made him identify the prisoners’ stored packs so Blue and Allyanna could recover their gear without searching the whole hideout. After that, the questioning ended and the party killed him rather than let him go.

## **Droop and Craig’s Departure**

After the fight, the party checked the remaining cell and found Droop still alive and hiding in the straw. He explained that Glasstaff’s people had captured him again after his earlier escape, and the group let him out at once. Droop then recognized Craig and urged him to leave the adventuring life behind and come back with the Scraptops, arguing that a goblin like him deserved a safer place to live. The party gave Craig room to choose for himself, and several of them encouraged him to go if it meant he could finally stop nearly dying in wizard lairs and monster tunnels. Craig accepted the offer, thanked the group for how they had treated him, and left the hideout with Droop.

## **End of Session**

The session ended with Glasstaff dead, Blue and Allyanna freed, and the party finally holding clear directions to Cragmaw Castle. Craig and Droop had just walked away together, leaving the group with two new companions and several choices for what comes next. The unresolved goals are deciding their next stop, following the lead to Cragmaw Castle, and figuring out how Blue and Allyanna fit into the party’s plans.
