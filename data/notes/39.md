## **Carousel Brass Ring Challenge**

The party rode the carousel run by a rock gnome operator, who said any horse was free except the swan, which bites. Broc cast Friends on the operator, with Vexhar announcing a Counterspell against him, and used tinted chapstick as the material component, after which the operator said there was no trick and that bigger riders had better odds. Broc considered enlarging Levon, but Paul said he would stand nearly 16 feet tall, so the group agreed to play it straight. Each rider chose on every pass whether to grab the ring or wait for a bigger payout, and Vexhar took the brass ring on the second pass for two tickets while Allyanna fell into the hay bale reaching for it. On the third pass Francis, Levon, and Blue each caught the gold ring for five tickets, and Broc fell into the hay beside Allyanna. Francis took offense when the operator, named Albrek, said children earlier had let the ring go past five, and he challenged him to show how it is done.

## **Blue Spots a Humming Stranger**

While the group gathered near the circus cart, Blue heard a slow, low humming that did not match the fiddler’s tune. She rolled a natural 20 on Perception for a 27 and traced it to a dwarven woman in blue robes sitting on a crate at the edge of the tent. The woman had white ink sigils winding up both forearms, a satchel stuffed with scrolls, and a book open on her knee that she copied into as she hummed. When a crowd of children ran between them the crate was empty, and Blue was the only one who noticed, so she chose not to mention it and went to find the others.

## **Fenwick’s Cup and Pea Game**

Fenwick Fingers Toll ran a cup and pea game that paid double tickets for finding the pea, and an older townsman shouted a warning that Fenwick had cost him his boots. Francis’s Insight check marked Fenwick as a trickster but turned up nothing more specific. Broc bet five tickets, picked cup three after an Insight check that told him only that Fenwick was messing with him either way, and lost because the pea was under cup two. Levon bet two and won four tickets on cup one, Allyanna lost her single ticket on cup two, and Blue used heroic inspiration on her roll, picked cup one, and won. Francis bet three tickets and won six on cup three, then gestured rudely at Fenwick, who answered in kind, while Vexhar declined to play despite Francis’s urging. Fenwick addressed each of them by name before closing the game.

## **Acid Pain and Carnival Suspicions**

As the group walked toward the festival green, Francis felt the itching in his shoulder turn into a stab of acid pain and took 4 acid damage from a 1d12. Paul described him as bloodied from taking this damage repeatedly through the day, and the party noted they had failed to learn whether pouring water on him would help. Francis said he would still fight the carnival workers, while Broc urged everyone to relax because nobody was trying to kill them today. Blue asked whether she sensed ill will toward the party, and Paul ruled that she felt no evil intent, only carnies who wanted tickets. Vexhar rolled a 9 on Insight about the carnival and sensed nothing wrong, and Paul suggested Harbin had been talking the party up, which explained why strangers knew their names.

## **Tug of War Against Big Hollis**

The party faced Big Hollis, an eight and a half foot half orc anchoring a circus crew that was undefeated in the last seven towns, under rules of no biting, no magic, no tickling, and no funny business. Broc cast Enlarge on Levon and claimed Levon was normally that size, and a wild magic surge made Broc radiate bright light in a 30 foot radius that blinded everyone near the rope. Blue sent Broc to sit out the first round, and Levon used heroic inspiration to reach a 17, but the crew won 86 to 64 on the strength of a natural 20. Broc rejoined for round two still glowing, Phandalin gained two marks, and Vexhar refused to spend his own heroic inspiration despite pleading from the table. Phandalin lost the third round, won the fourth with a pull that jerked the crew off their feet, and took the final pull to beat a crew that had never lost. Francis tried to tickle Big Hollis after the match until Harbin stopped him, Harbin gave each of them five tickets, and Broc dropped concentration on Enlarge.

## **Francis Discovers Mud Eases the Itching**

After the tug of war, Paul told Francis that his itching had eased, and Francis rolled an 11 on Medicine to work out why. He concluded that mud clinging to bare skin where he had no bandages stopped the itching from his curse, though he could not tell whether water mattered. Francis rubbed his old bandages in mud and wrapped them back on, resolving to keep mud on his skin at all times. A d100 roll for horse manure in the mud came out at 19 percent, and when Francis later jumped back into the pit it came out at 27 percent. The party agreed that Francis smells bad, that he should sleep in the stables, and that they should find or make mud without manure to learn which one is helping, while Vexhar reported he was about halfway through copying the scroll after roughly five hours at the fair.

## **Novelty Tent Spending Spree**

Corwin Thistlewick opened his novelty tent with Levon leading in tickets at 29, followed by Blue with 26, Francis with 25, Broc with 21, Vexhar with 20, and Allyanna with 19. Allyanna haggled down a singing stone and bought it, Blue sweet talked Corwin into selling her the giant stuffed owlbear plush for 18 tickets, and Broc bought a singing stone for 10 tickets after Corwin recognized him. Francis hinted at treasure stories to lower the unwavering compass from 18 to nine tickets, and Vexhar bought the lucky deck of 51 cards for eight tickets without haggling. Corwin then fixed his prices, so Levon, who had tried to bring a Phandalin roof charm down from nine to eight tickets, chose not to buy it. In the open shop Francis bought the portrait of the stranger for five tickets and Broc bought the tin whistle for six tickets. Paul said the portrait shows Scringle McGruffle, a thief from Waterdeep, and Francis said he would ask every NPC about it.

## **Festival Awards and Closing Fireworks**

At the circus tent Harbin announced the awards, starting with Keenest Wit for Broc and Allyanna, who tied with two riddles solved each at Madame Vane’s stage. Vexhar won Sharpest Eye with six points in Falin the Red’s archery contest, saying he was studying his scroll and not trying to shoot. Levon received the Golden Ladle for best dish along with a jar of the cook’s spice rub, Francis received Big Hollis’s rope bracelet for the mightiest single pull, and Blue won the Silver Tongue of the Fair for her bargain on the owlbear plush. Levon was then named champion of the festival for the most tickets earned. Pip Stonehill and other children handed out paper lanterns that Sister Garaele lit, the town released them, and fireworks followed. One lantern stayed aloft after the rest drifted away, turned against the breeze over the Old Mine Road, and winked out.

## **End of Session**

The festival ends with Phandalin asleep and the circus tent dark. Francis remains cursed, with the mud on his skin holding off the itching but drying out and needing frequent reapplication. Vexhar is about halfway through copying the scroll of remove curse, the Harpers’ request for a meeting remains open, and Francis still wants to learn whether the festival prizes carry any meaning. The lantern that drifted out over the Old Mine Road and Blue’s unshared sighting of the humming dwarven woman are unresolved.
