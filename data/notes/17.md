## **Camp Rest and Character Moments**

The party secured a safe place to rest and took a full long rest following the intensity of the hideout battles. During this time, they recovered resources and spent time talking through recent events and what they had learned about Cragmaw Castle and the surrounding threats. Blue and Allyanna integrated further into the group through conversation, establishing their roles and perspectives alongside the existing members. Several characters reflected their personalities through how they spent downtime, reinforcing differences in priorities and habits. The group used this period to stabilize and reset before committing to their next move. By the end of the rest, everyone was prepared both mechanically and mentally to continue.

## **Discussing Agatha and the Next Lead**

After resting, the party turned its attention to the task given by Hamun Kost involving the banshee Agatha. They reviewed what they knew about her and recognized that they would likely have limited interaction, making their choice of questions important. The group debated what information would be most valuable, including leads tied to Cragmaw Castle and broader threats. They considered both practical and strategic questions, attempting to maximize the usefulness of the encounter. The discussion helped align the party on a shared goal and approach before traveling. By the end of the planning, they committed to seeking out Agatha next.

## **Preparing Questions and Strategy**

The party spent time refining exactly how they would handle the interaction with Agatha once they found her. They discussed tone, approach, and how to avoid wasting the opportunity through poor communication or unclear questions. Some members focused on extracting direct information about their current objectives, while others considered broader or more long-term insights. The group also acknowledged the risks of dealing with a supernatural entity and the need to remain cautious. This preparation shaped a clear plan for the encounter rather than relying on improvisation alone. By the conclusion of the discussion, they had a structured idea of what they wanted to ask and how to proceed.

## **End of Session**

The session ended with the party rested, aligned, and ready to pursue the Agatha lead as their next objective. They had not yet reached her location, but their plan and questions were clearly established. The unresolved focus is how the interaction with Agatha will unfold and what information she will provide.
