## **Francis's Flashback: The Squad's Betrayal**

Francis relived a memory from his time in the Ironmark Legion, watching Captain Varron Maelor abandon the signal for reinforcements and leave the squad to die in the field. When the enemy surged forward, Francis chose to stay with his squad rather than pursue Maelor, dragging the wounded Sergeant Aldric Voss to cover and stabilizing his bleeding with a torn strip of his own cloak. In that moment he promised to always be there for his men, since command would not be. Aldric, Edden, and Davan all survived the night, though Francis never fully lost his trust in Maelor despite everything that happened. He received a one-time Flashback Reroll item usable after an athletics, persuasion, intimidation, or medicine check.

## **Allyanna's Flashback: A Blessing Made Real**

Allyanna relived a memory from her life as a charlatan on the road to Phandalin, encountering a terrified traveler fleeing a gray-skinned creature that had killed two of his companions near the old boundary stones. She sold him a fake protective blessing as she always did, but this time her words became real when Solas, a herald of Lathander, answered through her and offered a pact to make her false promises of safety true. Allyanna accepted the offer cautiously. The blessing worked, steadying the traveler and marking the first time her tricks turned into genuine celestial power. She received a one-time Flashback Reroll item usable after a deception, performance, religion, or arcana check.

## **Vexhar's Flashback: The Ritual Door**

Vexhar relived the night he completed a forbidden summoning ritual in his own hidden workshop, one built not to force a creature through but to invite contact while looking ordinary. After finishing the final rune sequence, a voice answered from the other side, offering power and knowledge in exchange for future acknowledgment that knowledge isn't free. Vexhar pushed for proof rather than waiting, and a mark began forming on him as the voice replied. Rather than erase the sigil that appeared afterward or leave it alone, Vexhar chose to copy it down for study. He received a one-time Flashback Reroll item usable after an arcana, investigation, or insight check.

## **Broc's Flashback: Opera House Triumph**

Broc relived a nerve-wracking early performance at an opera house, delivering a dramatic soliloquy from the balustrade of a castle-ruin set and winning over a skeptical crowd. Afterward he threw up from nerves, but stage manager Tavros reassured him he'd done well, and a colleague named Selise told him she was surprised and proud. A cloaked patron who lingered after the crowd left told Broc he'd been chasing control rather than art, and that he would learn another way. Broc struggled to respond, and the patron vanished before he could find the words. He received a one-time Flashback Reroll item usable after a performance, persuasion, or insight check.

## **Blue's Flashback: Escape from the Coral Court**

Blue relived a memory from her ten years of captivity in Lintri's Coral Court, noticing the fey domain's usual perfect stability beginning to crack and fail. Sensing this might be her only chance, she moved toward a glowing, unstable pool at the chamber's center despite her fear of the unknown. She stepped through and was pulled out of the Coral Court entirely, emerging free but hollowed out and changed. On the road afterward she chose the name Blue Glaucus for herself, one that revealed less than the name her parents had given her. She received a one-time Flashback Reroll item usable after an insight, performance, stealth, or deception check.

## **Levon's Flashback: Mending the Fence**

Levon relived a day on his family's farm when a section of pasture fence collapsed and sheep began scattering toward the treeline. He calmed the herd with hay before they could fully escape, then lifted the broken fence post back into place through sheer strength while his father Alvin watched without helping. Once the repair held, Alvin told him he'd "be fine," remarking that fences and storms come every year but implying his son was meant for more than the farm. Levon didn't fully understand the comment at the time. He received a one-time Flashback Reroll item usable after a survival, animal handling, or athletics check.

## **Guardroom Ogre Zombies Finished Off**

  - Allyanna's Eldritch Blast struck an ogre zombie for 5 force damage and shoved it back against the wall near Levon.
  - Broc's Chromatic Orb melted through a near-dead zombie, killing it, but triggered a Wild Magic Surge that turned Broc into an incapacitated potted plant and broke his concentration on Levon's Enlarge spell.
  - Levon, no longer enlarged, kept raging and struck the remaining ogre zombie with Choppy, eventually decapitating it after it failed its Undead Fortitude save.
  - Blue used Healing Word to revive Francis from unconscious, then landed a Vicious Mockery that nearly dropped a zombie to zero and left it with disadvantage on its next attack.
  - Francis struck the ogre zombie with Talon and a free Divine Smite for 20 combined damage, bloodying it badly.
  - Allyanna's Healing Light topped off Francis further, and Allyanna finished the wounded zombie with her Pact of the Blade greatsword while dodging past the ogre zombie's attack.
  - Vexhar's Chromatic Orb obliterated the last zombie's head outright, ending the fight.

## **End of Session**

With the last zombie destroyed, the wrecked guardroom finally went quiet, and Gundren emerged from cover where he'd been distracting the undead. Francis got back to his feet, breathing and alive after nearly dying twice in the fight. The party paused together in silence, taking in everything the flashbacks had just revealed about each other. Nundro Rockseeker remains unaccounted for somewhere deeper in Wave Echo Cave, and the party has not yet continued exploring beyond the guardroom.
