## **Overview**

The party opened with a recap roll-off that earned Francis Heroic Inspiration, then received Tharden's cloak from Gundren and identified it as a Cloak of Protection before handing it to Levon. After searching a ransacked storeroom for a locked strongbox and taking a short rest, the group pushed deeper into Wave Echo Cave, where an attempt to burn a patch of glowing fungus triggered a spore eruption that forced a retreat. The party then opened a door into an old barracks and fought off three ghouls after accidentally alerting them, before pressing north into a cramped mining tunnel maze. Following drag marks tracked by Levon, they were ambushed by an ochre jelly that split into smaller oozes every time it was struck, and the session ended mid-combat with two small fragments of the creature still active.

## **Recap Roll-Off Earns Francis Inspiration**

The session opened with a question of the day about how organized each character is, followed by a dice roll-off between Blue and Francis to decide who would recap the previous session for a chance at Heroic Inspiration. Francis won the roll and recapped the party's shared flashbacks, the one-time Flashback Reroll items each character received, and the fight that ended with the last of the ogre zombies and zombies cleared from the guardroom. Francis earned Heroic Inspiration for the accurate recap. The party remained in the wrecked guardroom roughly a hundred feet underground, with Gundren nearby and Francis back on his feet after the previous fight.

## **Gundren Gives Away Tharden's Cloak**

Gundren pulled Tharden's folded cloak from his pack and offered it to the party, saying Tharden would have wanted it to keep someone safe rather than sit at the bottom of a sack. Francis took the cloak, sensed it was magical with an Arcana check, then cast Detect Magic and identified it as abjuration-based warding magic. Broc later ran his own arcana check and confirmed it was a Cloak of Protection, granting a plus one bonus to armor class and all saving throws once attuned. Francis judged the bonus to saving throws would help Levon more given his weak wisdom and intelligence saves, and Levon accepted the cloak despite it being sized for the much smaller Tharden.

## **Storeroom Search Yields a Locked Strongbox**

Broc listened at a nearby door, heard nothing, and opened it to reveal an old office or storeroom with three dusty balance scales, cubbyholes stuffed with crumbling paper, and several long-dead skeletons on the floor. Vexhar investigated one of the skeletons and found nothing of use, while Blue searched the room and found no valuables among the bodies or worn clothing. Behind the counter the party found a locked iron strongbox, which Vexhar picked open with his thieves' tools after an initial failed attempt, revealing 600 copper pieces, 180 silver pieces, 90 electrum pieces, and 60 gold pieces added to the party's shared inventory. While Vexhar worked the lock, Levon and Broc piled rubble in front of the guardroom's entrance to barricade it.

## **Short Rest and Attuning to the Cloak**

The party settled into the storeroom to take a short rest, spending an hour undisturbed while tending wounds. Levon spent the hour attuning to the newly acquired Cloak of Protection, gaining its bonuses to armor class and saving throws. Several party members rolled hit dice to recover hit points, including Francis, who used both of his available hit dice. After the rest, the party spent several minutes clearing the rubble they had piled against the door before continuing on.

## **Three Paths from the Four-Way Cavern**

The party returned to the four-way cavern they had passed through earlier, marked by an arrow indicating the direction they had come from. From there, three routes remained: west back toward the campsite and a pit leading further northwest, an eastern passage sloping downward and lit by a faint sickly green glow, and a shorter passage ending at a door standing slightly ajar with firelight visible through the gap. Broc tested the eastern passage by smell and picked up a faint earthy scent that grew stronger the longer he stood near it, though nothing that smelled rotten. The party chose to investigate the glowing eastern passage first.

## **Torching the Glowing Fungus Backfires**

The eastern passage opened into a cavern crowded with glowing fungus and mushroom caps as tall as five feet, bright enough to light the whole area without a torch. Gundren warned the party to watch their step, recalling miners' stories about caves that grew unnaturally quiet. Levon threw a flask of oil into the fungus while Broc used Mage Hand to carry a second flask across the cavern and douse an unusually rigid, upright stalk, and Vexhar's Firebolt ignited the oil-soaked patch, burning it to a crisp. As the fire died down, two nearby fungal growths lashed out with vine-like motions and the surrounding puffballs began to swell and hiss before rupturing at once, releasing a thick green spore cloud that swallowed the cavern; the party retreated before the spores could take full effect, though Broc's throat briefly began closing at the edge of the cloud.

## **Approaching the Barracks Door**

The party backed away from the fungus cavern and returned to the shorter passage ending at the door standing ajar, where firelight showed through the gap and a faint, rhythmic gnawing sound came from inside. Levon repeatedly speculated that Gundren's missing brother, whom he nicknamed Sloppy Joe, was being eaten inside, prompting confusion from Gundren before Levon clarified he meant a sandwich rather than Nundro. Blue moved to a second door on the south side of the room and cast Minor Illusion to imitate Vexhar yelling for help, but the sound inside only paused briefly before resuming. Francis ultimately opened the door while the party discussed the situation loudly enough that whatever was inside noticed them before they could gain the advantage of surprise.

## **Ghoul Ambush in the Old Barracks**

  - Vexhar's Chromatic Orb strikes one ghoul with fire damage then bounces to the other two, bloodying all three ghouls in a single turn.
  - Broc finishes off the already-weakened ghoul with an acid Sorcerer's Burst, dropping it instantly.
  - One surviving ghoul lunges at Levon with a bite and claw attack, but his new Cloak of Protection deflects what would have been a solid hit.
  - Blue's Vicious Mockery lands a critical insult for psychic damage, further weakening the ghoul that attacked Levon.
  - Allyanna's Eldritch Blast misses after Levon blocks her line of sight in the doorway, and her follow-up bonus action dagger throw also misses.
  - Levon recklessly attacks and finishes one ghoul outright with Choppy.
  - Francis charges in and cleaves down the final ghoul, clearing the barracks.
  - Gundren takes the dodge action and stays out of the fight for its duration.

## **Tracking Drag Marks Through the Mining Tunnels**

Beyond the barracks, the party pushed north into a cramped maze of old mining tunnels with ceilings only six feet high, forcing Levon to stoop as the passages split repeatedly in every direction. Levon searched the dusty tunnel floor for tracks, and with Broc holding up his glowing cheese for better light, found smeared drag marks rather than footprints leading west, suggesting something without a fixed shape had passed through recently. The party followed the trail west deeper into the tunnels, finding no supplies, bodies, or other signs of the cave's former miners along the way. The tunnels were unnervingly quiet, with none of the dripping water sounds the party had noticed elsewhere in Wave Echo Cave.

## **Ochre Jelly Splits Apart in the Tunnels**

  - An ochre jelly drops from the ceiling and grapples Broc with a pseudopod strike as combat begins.
  - Francis invokes Vow of Enmity and strikes with Talon and a Divine Smite, but the damage splits the jelly into two smaller oozes instead of destroying it.
  - Vexhar's Firebolt and Allyanna's Eldritch Blast each connect and split their targets further, with Allyanna's blast also shoving one jelly back.
  - One of the jellies wraps its pseudopod around Blue for 12 acid damage before Blue kills a different, weakened ooze outright with Chill Touch.
  - Levon's reckless attack with Choppy passes cleanly through a jelly without leaving a wound or triggering a split.
  - Vexhar casts Shield for the first time as a pseudopod lunges at him, and the conjured black mist deflects the attack harmlessly.
  - Broc finishes off the ooze that struck Blue with a cold Sorcerer's Burst.
  - Gundren disengages and retreats behind Allyanna and Broc rather than fight, and the session ends with two small oozes still active.

## **End of Session**

The session ended mid-fight in the cramped mining tunnels, with two small ochre jelly fragments still active against the party. Nundro Rockseeker remains missing somewhere deeper in Wave Echo Cave, and the glowing fungus cavern to the east has not been further examined despite one patch reacting strangely to fire. The party has yet to determine what lies further along the western tunnel the drag marks led them into.
