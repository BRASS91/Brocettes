## **Overview**

The party resumed combat inside Cragmaw Castle with the group split across several rooms and a tentacle-armed Grick bearing down on Francis. Over the course of the session they cleared two separate engagements, looted a storage room, discovered a disturbing shrine with a blood-soaked altar, and took a short rest in a tower bunkroom. A second hobgoblin patrol ambushed the party during the rest, and the session ended in crisis: Francis at one hit point, Allyanna unconscious from a poison-laced critical hit, and the remaining hobgoblins still in the fight.

## **Grick and Goblin Boss Cleared from the Inner Corridor**

The session opened with Francis isolated in the northern rooms, grappling with a badly wounded Grick while Allyanna and Blue held a position near the southwest tower. Allyanna repositioned her Cloud of Daggers to keep pressure on the Grick as it attempted to flee north, dealing 10 points of slashing damage. When a goblin boss threw open doors to the north and reinforcements flooded in, Francis attempted to bluff the new arrivals as drink deliverers, but the goblin boss saw through it immediately and called in more help. Brock's cast of Magic Missile triggered a Wild Magic surge that turned Francis, Levon, and Allyanna invisible for up to a minute, buying the party a moment of repositioning advantage. Vexhar followed with a critical Chromatic Orb that struck the Grick for 44 fire damage, destroying it entirely. Allyanna finished the final goblin with a critical Eldritch Blast, ending the first engagement.

## **Combat: Grick and Corridor Goblins**

  - Francis held the Grick in melee with Talon and a Divine Smite, badly bloodying it before Vexhar's kill shot. He used Lay on Hands on himself to climb off one hit point and back into the fight.
  - Allyanna moved her Cloud of Daggers north to intercept the Grick as it fled, dealing 10 slashing damage and slowing its escape.
  - Brock's Magic Missile triggering a Wild Magic surge granted invisibility to Francis, Levon, and Allyanna, neutralizing the incoming goblin reinforcements for one critical round.
  - Vexhar's Chromatic Orb (fire) critted for 44 damage, one-shotting the already wounded Grick and ending the immediate threat to Francis.
  - Levon advanced into the dark corridor while raging, charged through the Cloud of Daggers taking 5 slashing damage, and swung blind at the fleeing goblin boss without connecting.
  - Blue used Chill Touch on the goblin boss for 6 psychic damage; the creature saved against the verbal component but briefly reacted to the cold effect.
  - The goblin boss disengaged and retreated after taking the hit rather than pressing its attack.
  - Allyanna's critical Eldritch Blast destroyed the last visible enemy in the room, ending combat.

## **Looting the Storage Room and the Shrine**

After the fight the party spread out across two rooms. Blue searched the goblin bodies and found only worn weapons and no keys. Levon investigated a storage area filled with rotting grain and found a small unlabeled cask and a pile of equipment: bloody chain mail, a short sword, a heavy crossbow, and a longsword with an emblem worked into the hilt. Levon took a shortbow from the goblin stores and held the longsword out to the group for inspection. Francis moved into the northern shrine room and found a stone altar covered in a blood-stained black cloth, with a gold chalice and censer arranged on top. A Religion check of 6 let him identify one of the two angelic pillar statues as Lathander, but the scene's full significance remained unclear. Vexhar crouched over the freshest goblin corpse to examine it for potential future necromantic use, drawing open distaste from Allyanna, Blue, and Brock.

## **Short Rest in the Southwest Tower**

The party pulled back to the rubble-strewn southwest tower to short rest. Blue improvised a bawdy sea shanty to coax Vexhar away from the corpse and into the room. Levon used Vitality of the Tree to grant Brock 10 temporary hit points. About 20 minutes into the rest, Francis heard faint whispered voices through the door: a goblin reporting "they're in there" before footsteps retreated into the castle. The party had no time to act before combat resumed.

## **Combat: Hobgoblin Patrol in the Corridor**

  - Blue opened with Vicious Mockery against the nearest hobgoblin in the doorway, landing 1 psychic damage and imposing disadvantage on its next attack.
  - Francis charged out the door with Talon, failed to pierce the hobgoblin's armor, and held position to deny it movement into the room.
  - A second hobgoblin used pack tactics to fire a poison-coated arrow, hitting Francis for 5 piercing and 8 poison damage.
  - A third hobgoblin scored a Nat 20 crit on Francis for massive damage, dropping him to exactly 1 hit point on the brink of instant death.
  - Allyanna used Healing Hands to restore 4 hit points to the prone Francis, stabilizing him at the cost of her position in the corridor.
  - A hobgoblin immediately scored a second Nat 20 crit against Allyanna for 37 points of damage (15 piercing and 22 poison), dropping her unconscious.
  - Vicious Mockery from Blue negated an otherwise-landing Nat 20 attack against Blue herself; the hobgoblin's disadvantage roll caused it to whiff despite the natural crit.
  - Levon raged, rushed the hobgoblin in the doorway with a Reckless Attack, and missed with a roll of 13 against the creature's armor class.

## **End of Session**

The session ended mid-combat in the hobgoblin corridor with Francis at 1 hit point prone on the ground and Allyanna unconscious from a poison arrow. Levon is raging in the doorway, Blue is the only party member not badly hurt, and the remaining hobgoblins are still active and pressing. The party has not long rested since before the bugbear fight at the castle's southern entrance, and most casters are critically low on spell slots. Gundren Rockseeker's whereabouts inside Cragmaw Castle remain unknown.
