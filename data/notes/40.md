## **Caine Reaches a Ruined Village**

Paul opened with a recap of Wyvern Tor, where the party negotiated Caine’s release from an orc and a bugbear and Caine left for his monastery with his share of the gold about two weeks earlier. After four or five days on the road toward the Star Mounts, Caine followed a rumor to a village that had been destroyed. He found roofless houses, a tipped cart, and a crater 30 feet across and five feet deep with a black stone about 10 feet long at its center. A hidden Investigation check on the stone told Caine nothing, and he could not tell whether it resembled the event at his monastery. He then walked toward a woman kneeling among fresh graves in the far corner of the village.

## **Caine Meets Nell Harrow**

The woman was packing dirt on a grave with her shovel, her right forearm wrapped in a dirty cloth, and Caine counted seven graves. Caine said a prayer to Chauntea for the dead, and the woman said she had buried her father there the other night and stayed to give the dead a proper burial. She offered Caine a handful of iron nails her father made as payment, and Caine declined them and asked instead that she live her life to the fullest. With a natural 19 on Medicine, Caine saw a clean burn running from her wrist almost to her elbow and healed it with Cure Wounds. She embraced him, gave her name as Nell Harrow, and described her brother Killthorn Harrow as an older man with short brown hair and green eyes who had been on the road for a year, and Caine promised to pass on word if he saw him. Caine split his last loaf of bread with her and asked whether his prayer could include Bless on the graves, which Paul allowed.

## **Puddle Blessing and Caine’s Decision**

Walking south, Caine found a puddle in front of a collapsed building and washed his face in it, and a sensation rushed over his body. A Religion check of 17 told him it was a warm blessing of healing and good health, like a protective aura that held sickness back while it lasted. Caine did not fight it and asked aloud whether it was his goddess’s doing, and he heard no answer. Looking into the water, he described seeing Levon, Francis, Vexhar, and Craig behind his own reflection. Caine decided the monastery would be rebuilt without him and that he knew where he was needed, so he headed back to Phandalin.

## **Reunion at the Stonehill Inn**

The festival ended, and the party returned to the Stonehill Inn, where Toblen Stonehill gave Francis a room on the condition that he keep the mud on. While they ate and drank, a man in a green hood sat in the doorway, and Francis recognized Caine and rushed to hug him until Broc and Allyanna warned him he was cursed and covered in manure. Caine noticed the bandages and the slimy ichor on Francis’s skin, and Broc introduced Caine to Blue and Allyanna. Caine explained that the party’s shared dangers and sacrifices reflected Chauntea’s teachings and that he could not forsake his vows, and he apologized for leaving. Caine asked to rejoin the group, and Broc replied that the empty chair at the table was now filled. The group joked that they had become “Broc and the Brockets,” a name Broc said he suggested while drunk.

## **Caine Cures Francis’s Curse**

Caine rolled Medicine with advantage because Broc had called the ailment a curse, and his 23 identified an otherworldly, mucus like curse that would stay on Francis unless cured. Paul explained that only water eased the pain, which is why Francis had been covering himself in mud. Caine doused Francis’s arm with water from his water skin and then cast Remove Curse on him. The ichor shook free, broke into beads, and crumbled to dust, and Francis said he felt better. The table noted that Vexhar was about 75 percent through studying a spell scroll that no longer had a deadline. The party also mentioned a meeting with Sister Garaele the next day, and Caine asked how she was doing.

## **Apple Treats from Trilena**

Toblen brought a tray of apple desserts that Trilena baked from apples picked at the Edermath Orchard, including a small, shriveled turnover she made specifically for Broc. Broc rolled a 2 on Investigation, ate it, and found it chewy but fine, and Trilena ducked back into the kitchen when people looked for her. Broc said he needed to apologize to her after admitting he had not recognized Toblen’s wife while drunk. Toblen greeted Caine, who said he had been lost for a while, and Caine took a treat after eating half rations all week. Francis ate with both hands while the table teased him about the mud still on him.

## **Washing Francis in the Yard**

Francis asked for a bucket to wash off the mud, and Broc led him outside to a hose beside the inn. Broc then cast Astral Flood on Francis to clean him without rolling damage, and the table argued over whether the spell would hurt him. Francis shouted that he was dead, but Paul ruled the spell should not have applied damage and retconned it, leaving Francis clean and uninjured. The casting left Broc radiating bright light again, and anyone within five feet of him was blinded for one minute. Broc went to bed, and Caine helped Francis, who said he felt better, back inside.

## **Caine Asks About Killthorn**

Before bed, Caine asked a woman at the inn about a man matching Killthorn Harrow’s description, and she pointed to a man in the back corner. The man was drunk, said he had no sister, and offered Caine a half empty mug, so Caine apologized and left him alone. Caine then told Francis that a village about six days away had been destroyed in a way similar to his monastery and that he had hoped to help someone there. Francis said it sounded like Caine to help people, and Caine reminded him of a promise to live a long life. The party finished the treats and ales, and Toblen showed them to their rooms.

## **Bell, Smoke, and a Red Sky**

A single bell woke the party, and they smelled smoke and saw a dull red sky with no moon or stars. Levon’s Perception check showed only thick smoke that hid everything past five feet from his window. Blue cast Detect Magic from the window and sensed no magic except her own pan flute. The common room was empty with chairs knocked over and the front door open, and the kitchen held a tray of apple treats but no sign of Trilena, Toblen, or Pip. Caine tried to sense ill intent and felt a dreadful evil presence that Paul said was not the same as the skeletons in the crypt. Broc cast Mage Armor, and the party ran outside.

## **Phandalin Burns and Initiative Begins**

Outside, all seven found the farm north of town burning and tall faceless figures of soot and ash walking down the north road. A farmhand shouted that people were still up at the farm, and Paul explained the rules: roll initiative, say where you run and what you do, roll once per turn, use each skill only once in the scene, and the fire spreads somewhere new at the top of every round. Embers set fire to the Sleeping Giant, Alderleaf Farm, and the north road, and the crowd inside the Sleeping Giant was stuck because its door opened inward. Francis used Misty Step to reach the second floor of Alderleaf Farm and pulled two children out of the path of a falling beam. Levon chopped the Sleeping Giant door in half with Choppy after an Athletics roll, and townsfolk poured out.

## **Soot Figures and Early Rescues**

Allyanna cast Armor of Agathys on herself, pushed through the flames at a home south of Alderleaf Farm, and carried out Mildra Merkel and her younger brother Jensen. Vexhar cast Chromatic Orb with cold damage and held the flames back from Alderleaf Farm. Blue played her new pan flute to give Levon a flight speed of 60 feet with tiny ethereal wings. Broc ran north, and his Web passed through the soot figures like smoke, so he cast Chromatic Orb with cold damage, spent sorcery points on Empowered Spell, and froze one figure that shattered. Two figures kept chasing people near the pumpkin patch. Caine used Preserve Life with Channel Divinity to heal two badly burned townsfolk and then ran north.

## **The Fire Spreads West**

At the top of the second round, burning scraps set fire to the Townmaster’s Hall and the Lionshield Coster, with people trapped inside. Francis rolled a 21 on Acrobatics to carry two children out of a window at Alderleaf Farm and then went to rescue another trapped person. Levon flew a barrel of water over the Townmaster’s Hall and dumped it, which pushed back the fire and let a few people out. Allyanna cast Minor Illusion to order people to the town green and the river, and Vexhar opened the Townmaster’s Hall door and freed Hovnar Tartesh. Blue cast Astral Flood on the Sleeping Giant, which put out the fire and washed five townsfolk onto the street. Broc used Mage Hand to open a fence and free animals and rushed into a house, and Caine cast Create or Destroy Water to douse the burning side of that house while Broc got two people out.

## **Bucket Brigade Contains the Fire**

In the third round the Edermath Orchard caught fire, and Daran Edermath ran out of his house shouting for help. Francis found a family of four hiding in a closet in a house that was not burning and led them toward the river. Levon refilled his barrel at the river and doused the orchard tree by tree, saving the trees but not the house. Allyanna used Celestial Revelation wings to fly buckets from a well to a burning building and freed the people inside. Vexhar led the remaining townsfolk out of the Townmaster’s Hall, and Blue used Charm Person on Narth to start a bucket brigade that put out the fires at the Townmaster’s Hall and the Lionshield Coster. Broc cast Shatter on the biggest fire at the farm and put out the farm, the pumpkin patch, and the last building, and Caine cast Mass Healing Word on six critically injured people in the town square.

## **The Bell Rings and a Voice Calls**

Despite the rescues, the fire kept spreading until the bell rang a second time and every fire, sound, and movement in town stopped. Everyone left alive stood still looking at the square, where Toblen, Trilena, Elsa, Elmina Barthen, and Sister Garaele stood watching the party. A voice spoke each of the seven names, and Paul called for a Wisdom saving throw to hold their tongues. Broc, Caine, Allyanna, Francis, and Vexhar did not answer, while Levon answered loudly and Blue counted to three and answered to her true name. Every fire flared, the ground in the square split open, and white light rose until the party could no longer see each other. The light made no sound.

## **Allyanna Meets Solas**

In the white, only Allyanna could see or hear Solas, who stood a few steps in front of her. Solas told her he could not answer when she called about Francis and said she should not see it as a failure because other hands lifted the curse. He said something had stood between them, a listener that had been listening to Phandalin for a long time and had let the party hear it back that night. Solas said his light was thin in the places they were going and that something wicked lay under the ground and inside some people. He warned that he might not answer the next time she called and that some people she loved would change and she should not look away. Solus said it was nearly dawn and told her to wake up.

## **End of Session**

Allyanna sat up in bed at the Stonehill Inn as morning light came through the windows, with a faint smell of smoke in the room and Phandalin appearing untouched. The party remains in their rooms at the inn with sunlight through the windows. The table asked whether the fire was an omen or a shared premonition, and Caine’s promise to pass word to Killthorn Harrow remains open.
