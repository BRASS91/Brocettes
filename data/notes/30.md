## **Overview**

The party finished off the last three bugbears holding the mine's northern quarters, capturing and interrogating the bugbear captain instead of killing him. He revealed that a drow calling himself the Spider, whose real name he gave as Nezznar, had hired his band and left them starving while withholding their pay, so the party sent him off to protect a goblin commune allied with Craig in exchange for his freedom, using a faked illusion mark to keep him honest. During the fight, Broc was grappled and dragged off as a hostage by a fleeing bugbear, forcing several rounds of missed rescue attempts before he broke free himself with a point-blank Sorcerer's Burst. While the party rested, Vexhar heard a voice in his mind urging him to use a sigil branded on his hand, and he reanimated one of the dead bugbears as a zombie familiar, cementing his shift into a Necromancy-focused wizard. Scouting ahead with the zombie, the party found a chamber where a drow matching the captive's description of the Spider was speaking with two bugbears, but decided they were too worn down to engage him and withdrew. Checking a second, barricaded door instead, they discovered a ruined dwarven forge chamber littered with old corpses and dominated by a motionless skull wreathed in green flame, and the session ended there.

## **Finishing the Fight in the Bugbear Den**

  - Levon distributes temporary hit points to Francis and Allyanna, then rages and cleaves two bugbears with Choppy for 10 points of slashing damage, swinging the fight's momentum firmly in the party's favor.
  - Blue finishes off a bloodied, unarmed bugbear with Vicious Mockery, the insult apparently overwhelming him as he drops his weapon and dies.
  - Allyanna lands Tasha's Hideous Laughter on the last conscious bugbear, leaving him prone and incapacitated with laughter right beside his already-sleeping captain.
  - Levon later cleaves the laughing bugbear for 15 points of slashing damage, killing him outright.
  - The bugbear cornered near the second door grapples Broc and drags him off as a hostage, fleeing through the doorway while Levon and Francis both miss their opportunity attacks.
  - With Broc used as a living shield, Francis, Levon, and Allyanna all miss repeated swings trying not to hit him, until Broc breaks the grapple himself with a point-blank Sorcerer's Burst that finally drops the bugbear.
  - Blue hogties the sleeping bugbear captain with rope before he can wake into the fight, while Vexhar's firebolt attempts against the captain and the wounded door-guard both miss due to bad angles and disadvantage.

## **Interrogating the Bugbear Captain**

Once the fighting ends, the party keeps the bugbear captain tied up and questions him about Gundren's missing brother, but an insight check confirms he genuinely doesn't recognize the name. He explains that a drow going by the Spider, whom he names as Nezznar, hired his band and left them starving without paying the rest of their promised gold. Rather than kill him, Francis and Blue strike a bargain: he'll travel to a nearby goblin commune and help protect it, using Craig as a reference to prove he's trustworthy. Blue smears his back with blood and fakes a tracking spell with Minor Illusion and a deception check to keep him honest, and the grateful captive warns the party that whatever is behind the mine's second, barricaded door is even more dangerous than the Spider before fleeing.

## **Vexhar's Sigil Awakens a Zombie**

While the party settles in to rest, Vexhar examines one of the dead bugbears and, on a strong Intelligence check, senses something unnatural about the corpse. A voice he recognizes speaks in his mind, calling him "little scholar" and urging him to use what he's learned, and the sigil branded on his hand flares as he channels it into the body. The bugbear reanimates as a shambling zombie under his telepathic control, marking Vexhar's shift into a Necromancy-focused wizard. The rest of the party reacts with alarm and dark humor, with Brock backing against the wall and Francis jokingly greeting the zombie as Vexhar's "boyfriend."

## **Scouting the Northern Corridor**

The party sends Vexhar's new zombie ahead to scout the passage beyond the bugbear den. It passes worn dwarven stonework into a wider corridor, where the sound of moving water drifts in from one direction and the smell of wood smoke from another. Following the smoke, it reaches a set of heavy iron-banded doors at the edge of its telepathic range, and the party moves up to investigate further. Francis and Levon both hear low voices and multiple sets of footsteps through the door, though the words are too indistinct to make out.

## **Discovering the Drow's Camp**

The party cracks the door and sends the zombie in to look, revealing a large hall with cracked pillars, a seated dwarf statue, and an established camp with bedrolls and a firepit. Two bugbears stand beside a drow in black leather who carries a staff topped with a carved spider, and he notices the zombie without reacting to the rest of the party outside. Believing they've found the Spider himself, and with no sign of Nundro in the room, the party decides they're too worn down to engage him and quietly withdraws. Gundren pushes to kill the Spider on the spot, but the group agrees their priority is finding his brother safely instead.

## **The Ruined Forge and the Green Skull**

The party turns instead to the second door they had left barricaded, and Levon and the others clear away the furniture and rubble blocking it. Behind it lies a huge ruined chamber with a dead furnace, a frozen water wheel, and abandoned carts of unrefined ore. Over a dozen long-dead dwarven bodies lie scattered across the floor near passages leading west, south, and east, still positioned where they fell centuries ago. Floating motionless above the wreckage is a skull wrapped in unflickering green flame, and the session ends as the party takes in the sight.

## **End of Session**

The session ends the moment the party lays eyes on the floating, green-flame-wrapped skull in the ruined forge chamber, before anyone reacts further. Nundro remains unaccounted for, and the Spider is still camped nearby with two bugbears, unaware the party spotted him. The skull's nature and intentions are completely unknown as the group is left to decide how to approach it.
