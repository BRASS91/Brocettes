## **Questioning and Releasing Droop**

The party discovers a goblin named Droop tied up in the Redbrand hideout and decides to free him after confirming he is not hostile. Droop explains that he belongs to a goblin clan called the Scraptops, known for scavenging and tinkering. He says he wandered into the hideout searching for supplies but was captured by the bugbears working for Glasstaff and kept as a prisoner. During the conversation, Droop shares what he knows about Glasstaff’s appearance and mentions that the wizard has a small demonic imp that accompanies him. He also confirms that the Redbrands and Cragmaw goblins are connected to the larger Cragmaw organization associated with a castle somewhere north in the forest. After giving all the information, the party releases Droop and he runs off through the tunnels the way he originally entered.

## **Aftermath of the Redbrand Hideout**

With Droop gone, the hideout grows quiet and the party reflects on the outcome of their assault on the Redbrands. They acknowledge that Glasstaff escaped but confirm that the rest of the hideout has been cleared of enemies. The group discusses the implications of the note addressed to Glasstaff and what it might mean about who is involved in the larger plot. Some members speculate about whether Sildar could be in danger or unknowingly connected to the situation due to his relationship with Iarno. The party considers whether confronting Sildar for more information would actually reveal anything useful. Ultimately they recognize that they still lack the exact location of Cragmaw Castle despite knowing it lies somewhere north.

## **Deciding the Next Destination**

The group reviews the different quests currently available to them before committing to a direction. One possibility is immediately searching the northern forests for Cragmaw Castle in hopes of finding Gundren. Another option is gathering more information in Phandalin before leaving the region. They also consider the report of marauders gathering near Wyvern Tor, which poses a direct threat to nearby settlements and travelers. After weighing the risks and uncertainty surrounding Cragmaw Castle, the party agrees that addressing the threat is a clearer and more immediate objective. With that, they prepare to travel toward Wyvern Tor.

## **Owlbear Encounter on the Road**

  - While traveling through the wilderness on the way toward Wyvern Tor, the party encounters a large owlbear near a narrow bridge with two cubs nearby.
  - Caine identifies the creature and warns the party about its reputation for violently defending its young.
  - The owlbear becomes aggressive when the party approaches, charging across the bridge and closing distance with the group while the cubs retreat toward a nearby cave.
  - Francis attacks from range with his bow while the party spreads out to avoid being clustered together near the bridge.
  - Vexhar supports the fight from range with damaging spells, striking the creature as it advances on the party.
  - Levon rages and charges into melee, landing a powerful critical strike with his axe that severely wounds the owlbear.
  - Broc casts Phantasmal Force, creating the illusion of a hunter grabbing the owlbear’s cubs, causing the creature to perceive a threat to them and chase the illusion away from the party. Broc’s spell also triggers a Wild Magic Surge, which transports him to the Nine Hells for a brief moment before he reappears back at the battlefield.
  - The party continues focusing their attacks on the creature until it collapses from the combined damage.
  - The owlbear dies near the bridge while its cubs remain inside the cave and do not return to the fight.

## **After the Owlbear Fight**

After defeating the owlbear, the party surveys the area around the bridge and the nearby cave entrance. They confirm that the creature is dead and that the immediate threat has been dealt with. The group notes that the cubs fled into the cave and may still be inside. They pause to regroup and consider whether investigating the cave is worth the risk. The party also keeps their larger goal of reaching Wyvern Tor in mind as they discuss their options.

## **End of Session**

The session ends with the party standing near the bridge after defeating the owlbear on the road to Wyvern Tor. The nearby cave still contains the owlbear’s cubs, and the party has not yet decided whether to investigate it. Their broader objective remains traveling to Wyvern Tor to confront the orcs reported in the area.
