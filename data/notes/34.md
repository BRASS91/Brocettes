## **Mormesk Appears in the Burned Room**

Francis, Levon, and Blue stood by a scorched iron chest in a burned room while Broc and Allyanna looked around. Vexhar was still back at the forge, and Gundren and Nundro waited by the staircase. A floating figure called Mormesk, once a wizard of this place, drifted toward them and ignored every question. Levon tossed a wooden board at it and learned that objects pass through it and plain weapons barely hurt it. Francis and Allyanna identified it as a wraith, and Broc messaged Vexhar to come help before the creature lurched forward and initiative was rolled.

## **Combat with the Wraith Mormesk**

  - Levon throws a board at Mormesk before the fight and finds that plain weapons barely harm the wraith. Broc messages Vexhar, who does not arrive in time.
  - Paul rules a luck roll gives the party a free round. Broc upcasts Witch Bolt at third level, and Allyanna fires two Eldritch Blasts that both connect.
  - Francis casts Bless on his allies. Blue casts Phantasmal Force at Mormesk, who passes the DC 15 Intelligence save and shrugs it off.
  - Blue backs away to join Allyanna and gives Francis Bardic Inspiration.
  - Broc hits Mormesk with a critical Sorcerous Burst after switching to fire damage because the wraith resists lightning, and Mormesk still resists part of the damage.
  - Allyanna rolls a natural 20 with Eldritch Blast, which drops Mormesk to the floor and leaves him barely standing.
  - Mormesk rises and drains Francis's life with a touch. Francis makes his Constitution save and keeps his life force, though the touch hurts badly.
  - Levon swings Choppy at a spot the board missed and finishes Mormesk, who says "Finally" before dissolving into smoke and ash.

## **Looting the Chest**

Blue used Mage Hand to open the iron chest and found it unlocked and untrapped. Inside were about 1,100 copper pieces, 160 silver pieces, about one gold piece of gold chunks, five gems, and a wooden pipe with platinum filigree. Paul placed everything in party inventory, and Francis grabbed the pipe. Vexhar arrived at the doorway after the fight was over. The party agreed to bring Mormesk's name to the Spectator at the Forge of Spells.

## **The Spectator and the Expired Contract**

Broc told the Spectator that Mormesk had sent them, but the Spectator demanded proper credentials and refused to name the masters. Levon broke down a boarded door and found a storeroom with a stone table, crates, and scattered papers. Blue's investigation found a ledger of forged items, and Broc found a signed contract for a summoned guardian lasting 101 years under the name Halvard Price, dated 1385. The current year is 1491, so the contract expired about five years ago. Broc showed the Spectator the contract, and the Spectator checked the date and signature, declared itself released, and vanished.

## **Testing the Forge of Spells**

The party gathered at the Forge of Spells and Levon considered throwing his cheese into the flame before deciding against it. Broc held three jeweled rings over the flame with Mage Hand for ten minutes while Blue ritually cast Detect Magic. The rings gained no magic, and Gundren guessed the forge only works on blades and armor. Broc then held a plain dagger in the flame for several minutes, and it gained a green aura and a transmutation effect. Vexhar's Arcana check showed the enchantment was borrowed, would fade after about eight hours, and had made the dagger a plus one dagger.

## **Shield of Tyr and Gundren's Trust**

Francis searched a corner of weapon debris and found a shield that his natural 20 History check identified as a plus one Shield of Tyr, carved with the god's image. Francis kept the shield and gave his old plus one shield to Broc. Broc also gave a shield to Nundro to experiment on at the forge. The party proposed that Craig's goblins and a bugbear could guard the mine. Gundren agreed on the condition that the party answers for any trouble. Gundren also refused to let the party's 10 percent be reduced and said he would pay the goblins a flat percentage himself.

## **Vexhar Stays Behind**

Vexhar asked to stay at the forge to guard it and research it. The rest of the party was suspicious, and everyone rolled Insight in a contested check against Vexhar's hidden roll. Francis, Levon, and Broc read his interest as curiosity. Blue and Allyanna could not read him. The party gave Vexhar a pat on the back and a warning not to put a body in the forge. Francis also threw him two joints. Everyone left, and Vexhar stayed at the forge with his rations and a potion of invisibility.

## **Return to Phandalin**

Gundren and Nundro knelt briefly over Tharden's body and asked the party to help carry him to town. Francis summoned his steed with Find Steed, choosing a wolf. He laid Tharden's body across his arms and rode out with the party following. They reached Phandalin around midday and found a new watch post near the road. Townsfolk noticed the party, and one jogged ahead to spread the word.

## **End of Session**

The party arrived in Phandalin around midday with Tharden's body. Vexhar remained alone at the Forge of Spells, researching it. The party still needs to give Tharden a proper burial. Gundren plans to return to the forge soon, and the party has not yet written to Craig about guarding the mine. Paul said the next session picks up with the party in town and Vexhar still at the forge.
