## **Gundren Reveals Wave Echo Cave's Secret**

When Sildar asks where Gundren's brothers went, Gundren admits he doesn't know and says the last place he knew them to be was Wave Echo Cave. He explains that he and his brothers rediscovered the long-lost dwarven and gnomish mine, hoping it held both mineral wealth and a legendary site called the Forge of Spells capable of enchanting weapons and armor. He believes this discovery is why the Spider wanted him captured and why goblin forces have been active in the region. The party questions Gundren about the gray-skinned elvish woman from Cragmaw Castle, telling him they believe her name was Vyrith, but Gundren says the name means nothing to him. Gundren offers the party a 10% share of the mine's future profits if they help him reclaim Wave Echo Cave and find his brothers, and after some debate the party agrees to depart at first light the next morning.

## **Sildar Learns the Truth About Iarno**

Francis and Broc tell Sildar that his old friend Iarno Albrek was secretly the wizard Glasstaff, leader of the Red Brands, and that the party killed him during the earlier assault on his hideout. Sildar is visibly shaken by the news and asks to see any letters recovered from Glasstaff's chambers, but the party explains that Caine took the correspondence with him when he left. Broc hands over Iarno's silk pouch, which Sildar recognizes and holds onto while grieving the friend he once knew rather than the man Iarno became. Sildar declines help retrieving the body, saying it's a task he needs to do himself, and asks the party to point him toward the abandoned manor hideout on the hill. Vexhar privately uses Insight to gauge Sildar's honesty and concludes his grief and lack of prior knowledge about Iarno are genuine.

## **Phandalin Pays Its Debts**

Harbin Wester pays out the town's bounties: 500 gold for ending the threat at Cragmaw Castle, 100 gold for dealing with the Red Brands, and 100 gold for driving off the marauders at Wyvern Tor, bringing the party's total to 1,830 gold pieces. The party presses Harbin about hiring proper guards for the town, and Broc openly criticizes his leadership; Harbin retreats to his back office without much of a response. Sildar steps in and promises to organize a town watch, establish patrols, and send word to the Lord's Alliance for aid while the party is away at Wave Echo Cave. Sildar also settles his own debt from the search for Iarno, handing over 200 gold pieces even though the outcome wasn't what either of them had hoped for.

## **Bargaining with Lenine at the Coster**

The party visits Lenine Graywind at the Lion's Shield Coster and tells her the Red Brands are dead, prompting her to pay the promised 50 gold reward. Francis presses for more, and after a successful persuasion check Lenine adds another 25 gold, calling it the best she can do. Broc uses Message to suggest Francis ask for a store discount instead of extra gold, and Francis proposes the party keep the extra 25 gold while Lenine gives them a standing 10% discount on future purchases, which she accepts. The party shops at her discounted rates, with Francis purchasing a plus-one shield and Broc buying a net, while Lenine agrees to help organize the town watch alongside Sildar.

## **A Boisterous Night at the Stonehill Inn**

The party returns to the Stonehill Inn, where Toblin Stonehill welcomes them warmly and remarks that the town finally feels at peace. Over ale and a large trout dinner, Broc introduces a dice-based drinking game, and Vexhar, Broc, and Blue take turns drinking as they roll and fail constitution saves while Allyanna and Francis largely abstain or hold their liquor. Vexhar becomes severely intoxicated, stumbles into a dirty bathing station believing it to be a hot tub, and is dragged out and carried to bed by Levon and Blue after passing out. As the night winds down, Toblin sends the party up to their rooms with the promise of breakfast whenever morning comes.

## **End of Session**

The party turns in for the night at the Stonehill Inn, most of them drunk or buzzed from the evening's celebration, with Wave Echo Cave and the search for Gundren's missing brothers still waiting on the road ahead. Gundren has agreed to depart at first light. Sildar remains behind in Phandalin to organize the town's defenses and to see to Iarno's remains.
