## **Overview**

The session opened by resuming the hobgoblin combat from the previous cliffhanger, with the party picking off the remaining enemies on an elevated bridge structure before Broc's Wild Magic surge ignited the entire wooden gate and bridge, burning them to ash. After a short rest and a brief loot of the fallen hobgoblins, the party pressed on through the Neverwinter Wood and located Cragmaw Castle. They approached from the south, split their scouting between the main wooden door and the castle's arrow slits, and triggered two separate combat encounters, with goblins inside the castle's kitchen area drawing most of the action through blind fire and Vexhar's devastating Shatter spell. The session ended mid-combat with the party fully committed to the southern approach and stealth entirely abandoned.

## **Finishing the Hobgoblin Bridge Fight**

The party resumed combat with two hobgoblins still positioned on an elevated platform above the bridge. Blue held Mirror Image while trading Vicious Mockery at the elevated archer, but the hobgoblin passed his saving throw and the insult landed without effect. Blue instead used a bonus action to grant Broc Bardic Inspiration. Francis, still prone from the previous round and unnoticed by the nearby hobgoblin, cast Shield of Faith on himself before swinging from the ground, missing cleanly. Vexhar landed a Fire Bolt on the hobgoblin captain for minimal damage. Allyanna's Eldritch Blast pushed one hobgoblin back 10 feet with Repelling Blast for 8 points of force damage. Levon scaled the 20-foot cliff face with an advantage athletics check during his rage, reached the upper platform, and cut down one of the remaining hobgoblins with his axe. Broc finished the last enemy with Magic Missile, triggering a Wild Magic Surge that gave him flaming hands for the next minute.

## **The Bridge Burns**

With combat over, Broc touched the wooden gate with his burning hands, igniting it. Within minutes the fire spread across the entire bridge platform, and the structure collapsed entirely into ash. The two hobgoblin bodies on the upper level were consumed. The party looted the remaining ground-level corpses and recovered three poisoned arrows with a greenish coating, which were distributed to Francis for safekeeping. The hobgoblin captain carried no key. The party took a short rest nearby to recover before pushing on.

## **Approach to Cragmaw Castle**

The party traveled through the Neverwinter Wood using directions they had previously obtained, arriving within a mile of Cragmaw Castle late in the night. Broc and Vexhar spotted faint flickering lights on the castle's north side from a distance. The party chose to approach from the south, where no activity was visible. They arrived at the castle's overgrown southern path, finding a large wooden door sheltered from direct attack and arrow slits set 10 feet above the ground. Characters without darkvision were effectively blind. Vexhar checked the wooden door for traps and found none, but confirmed it was locked. Broc simultaneously moved to the arrow slits and heard goblins inside arguing about dishes.

## **Scouting the Arrow Slits and Phantasmal Force**

Broc observed four goblins through the arrow slits, bickering over whether the dining table needed clearing. Two goblins suddenly dashed away, apparently reacting to something near the door. Broc relayed information to Vexhar via Message, and Vexhar returned to the group while Broc tracked the goblins through the slit. A goblin spotted Broc at a 50-50 odds roll, turned to warn the room, and Broc used Phantasmal Force before initiative could be called, forcing the goblin to fail his Intelligence saving throw and become convinced an ogre was outside. The goblin began mimicking an ogre at his companions, creating confusion inside the room. The party rolled initiative, then took a short break.

## **Combat in the Courtyard: Goblins Through the Arrow Slits**

With initiative underway and the goblins in disarray, Vexhar stepped up to the arrow slit and cast Shatter into the dining hall, targeting the densely clustered room for 11 points of thunder damage. One goblin at the arrow slit died instantly. Others took half damage, and the table in the room shattered. The noise was loud enough to alert potentially the entire castle. The goblins fired back through the arrow slit at irregular intervals as different goblins debated whether anyone was actually outside. One goblin landed an arrow on Broc for 3 points of piercing damage. Broc fired Sorcerer's Burst through the slit for minor thunder damage, triggering another Wild Magic Surge check. Francis lit his lantern, illuminating the area and revealing Levon swinging his axe at the locked door to no effect. Allyanna fired an Eldritch Blast at a silhouette through the arrow slit but missed. The session ended with the goblins alerted, a goblin inside mentioning a name that sounded like "Yeag," and mysterious activity described as happening in the background beyond the party's view.

## **End of Session**

The party is committed to the southern entrance of Cragmaw Castle with all hope of stealth extinguished. The wooden door remains locked and Blue has not yet attempted to pick it. Goblins inside the castle are alerted, and at least one fled toward someone named Yeag elsewhere in the castle. The party is unsure what lies beyond the walls or whether Gundren Rockseeker is present, but the DM confirmed next session will be combat-focused from the start.
