## **Combat: Finishing Snarl and Capturing the Elvish Woman**

  - Broc opened with Mind Sliver against Snarl, applying a debuff to its saving throws that weakened its resistance to follow-up attacks.
  - Levon raged and charged the direwolf, landing a heavy hit with Choppy that badly wounded it without quite bloodying it.
  - Vexhar's Toll the Dead cantrip pushed through despite the debuffed save and bloodied Snarl.
  - Snarl retaliated with a bite at Levon but missed entirely.
  - As the gray-skinned elvish woman broke for the door, Blue and Allyanna each attempted to grapple her and failed, Allyanna's attempt blocked by a wall.
  - Levon also missed a grapple attempt on the fleeing woman before Francis closed the distance and pinned her arms, stopping her escape.
  - Allyanna's Eldritch Blast slammed into Snarl and shoved it against the wall, leaving it near death.
  - Francis delivered the finishing blow, ending Snarl's life as it let out a final howl beside its dead master.

## **Questioning and Releasing the Spider's Agent**

With combat over, the party pressed the captured woman for answers. She revealed she worked for an employer known as the Spider and had come to negotiate business with King Grol involving Gundren, though she refused to explain further, deflecting questions about Sildar and claiming no knowledge of him. Broc argued for showing restraint, and after further back-and-forth the party agreed to let her go rather than kill her, with her parting words warning that her business here had concluded and that the Spider would be found only if he wished to be.

## **Looting Grol's Chamber and Reviving Gundren**

Once the woman left, Levon searched King Grol's body and recovered only a small pouch containing about 10 gold and a few silver pieces. With no potions or spell slots readily available, Levon and Vexhar examined Gundren and confirmed he was stable but deeply unconscious, unlikely to wake without magical aid. Allyanna then spent her last remaining pact magic spell slot on Cure Wounds, restoring Gundren's color and consciousness. Once awake, Gundren explained he had last seen Sildar at a tavern before their group was ambushed and separated, and confirmed that Sildar's associate Iarno Albrek was the Lord's Alliance agent later revealed to be the traitor Glasstaff.

## **Long Rest and Reaching Level Four**

The party carried Gundren out of the castle and made camp a safe distance away rather than resting among the bodies. With the immediate threat over, the group completed an uninterrupted long rest through the night and reached level four as a result of clearing Cragmaw Castle and rescuing Gundren.

## **Return to Phandalin and Restocking**

The party escorted Gundren on the roughly seven-hour trip back to Phandalin without incident, arriving to recognition and whispers as townsfolk noticed the missing dwarf's return. At Barthen's Provisions, Elmina embraced Gundren with relief and confirmed his wagon and oxen had been kept safe. The party bought ladders and rope, sold off jewelry and beaver pelts from their accumulated loot for 175 gold, and pooled their money into the shared party fund.

## **Sister Garaele and the Shrine of Luck**

At the Shrine of Luck, Sister Garaele greeted Gundren with visible relief and asked after his condition. The party reported that the hag they had previously questioned traded away Bowgentle's spellbook to a figure named Samber of Erebor generations ago, information Garaele said would be valuable to the Harpers. In thanks, she rewarded the party with three potions of healing and asked after Caine, who they explained had returned to his monastery.

## **Reunion at the Townmaster's Hall**

Gundren led the party to the townmaster's hall, where Harbin Wester reacted with shock at seeing him alive before calling for Sildar. Sildar emerged and embraced the news of Gundren's survival, promising the party would be compensated for finding his friend. When Sildar asked after Gundren's missing brothers, Nundro and the others, Gundren's expression fell as he admitted he didn't know what had become of them.

## **End of Session**

The session ended at the townmaster's hall in Phandalin, moments after Gundren and Sildar's reunion. Gundren has confirmed Iarno Albrek was the traitor Glasstaff, but he has no information on the whereabouts of his missing brothers, leaving that thread open. The party has not yet learned more about the Spider's plans or whether the released elvish woman will resurface, and Sildar's trustworthiness remains an open question the table raised but did not resolve.
