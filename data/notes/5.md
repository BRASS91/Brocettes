## **Looting Klarg’s Den and Healing**

In Klarg’s chamber, the group searches the crates and sacks, identifying the blue lion sigil of the Lionshield Coster and realizing these are legitimate trade goods stolen from caravans. They discover a small locked chest containing copper and silver coins, two potions of healing, and a jade statuette, deciding the coins will go into party loot while the potions are reserved for emergencies. One potion is immediately used to revive the unconscious Francis, and the group checks over the rest of their wounds while debating whether to treat their goblin prisoner and the wolves as future threats or responsibilities. Around this time Vexhar quietly feeds on Klarg’s corpse, but the others notice or hear about it and begin quietly questioning what his strange behavior means, resolving that they will need more answers later rather than confronting him in the middle of the cave. They also confirm with Sildar that the Lionshield freight can be returned in Phandalin for likely gratitude or reward, and start planning how to move the bulky crates back to their waiting wagon.

## **Feeding Wolves and Letting Them Go**

The party decides Klarg’s body should go to the mistreated wolves, dragging his corpse to the narrow chimney and shoving it down into the den where the animals can reach it. As Klarg’s remains tumble down, they use their strength and positioning to maneuver him through, then listen as the wolves quickly set upon the body below. While the wolves eat, they plan to free the animals once they are calmer, and when the moment comes they unfasten the chains using care and a bit of magic so the wolves can run out of the cave rather than turn on the party. The wolves briefly rush toward the group, prompting a mock call for initiative, but instead sprint past and vanish down the tunnel, leaving the cave quiet. With the danger eased, the party takes a short rest, some members training skills such as sleight of hand with dice while others patch wounds and talk, before persuading Yimik and his goblins to help haul the heavy Lionshield crates back and forth to the wagon over several hours.

## **Loading the Wagon and Sildar’s Truth**

Once everything is back at the ambush site, the party finishes loading the wagon with Gundren’s supplies and the rescued Lionshield crates while Sildar confirms that Yimik and the remaining goblins are leaving them to their own journey. With the immediate work done, Sildar gathers everyone and promises a payment of fifty gold pieces once they reach Phandalin, both for saving his life and escorting him the rest of the way. He then shares everything he has kept secret: Gundren Rockseeker found the location of the lost Wave Echo Cave and was carrying that information when captured, and Sildar was separately sent by the Lord’s Alliance to meet Iarno Albrek, a wizard who has since gone missing in Phandalin. The group confirms through questions and insight checks that Sildar appears truthful, and Levon adds what he knows of Phandalin as a small frontier town with light law enforcement and recent trouble with bandits along the Tribor Trail. They conclude that something is seriously wrong in and around Phandalin, and agree that once they arrive they will both pursue leads on Gundren’s whereabouts and help Sildar investigate his missing contact.

## **Journey to Phandalin with Craig**

The group sets out along the road, the way back through the hills quieter than the route they first took, and the DM describes the trail dipping and the trees thinning as Phandalin comes into view in the distance. During the trek, the party talks about Vexhar’s unsettling feeding on the bugbear, with Broc warning Francis and others resolving to keep an eye on him while focusing on their broader mission. They decide Craig should be disguised before entering town, using spare clothes and powder to pass him off as a halfling or otherwise hide his goblin features so he can travel with them without causing a panic. As they roll into town, Sildar rides partway with them, thanks them again, and hops off near the center of Phandalin to secure a room at the Stonehill Inn, asking them to meet him later that night to discuss next steps privately. The party then turns their attention to stabling the wagon, figuring out where to deliver Gundren’s shipment, and planning how to return the Lionshield crates when the time is right.

## **Redbrands at Barthen’s Provisions**

Driving the wagon into town, the group decides to head first to Barthen’s Provisions, hoping to get paid for the delivery and inquire about rewards for the recovered Lionshield goods. As they approach the shop, they hear raised voices and a heavy thump from inside, and then two men in red cloaks shove the door open, shoulder past the party with a rude comment, and stride away without looking back. After brief perception checks and no immediate confrontation, the group heads into Barthen’s, where Elmina greets them, confirms the delivery, and pays them for Gundren’s supplies while expressing worry over his disappearance. In conversation she explains that the red-cloaked ruffians, known as Redbrands, have been shaking down local businesses, ignoring the townmaster, and recently tried to threaten her with “protection” offers before she drove them out. Elmina also shares what she knows about Gundren’s “something big” connected to a mine, hints that his Rockseeker brothers may have more information in the area, and notes that the Redbrands frequent the Sleeping Giant, giving the party both a clearer picture of Phandalin’s troubles and several new leads to pursue.

## **End of Session**

The session ends with the party leaving Barthen’s Provisions, dragging the still-groggy Levon out, and loading him to rest in the back of a now-lighter wagon that still carries a few Lionshield crates. Elmina asks them to let Gundren know she is worried if they find him, and they in turn promise they will not leave him out there alone. The group plans to head toward the tavern and eventually the Stonehill Inn to regroup with Sildar, with Gundren’s rescue, the Redbrands’ harassment, and the mystery of Wave Echo Cave all explicitly hanging as unresolved goals.
