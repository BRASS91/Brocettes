## **Crossing the Broken Bridge**

The party debates whether to investigate the owlbear den, but they decide the cubs are not worth the risk and turn their attention to the broken bridge instead. Levon inspects the bridge and concludes he cannot repair it quickly, so the group shifts to jumping the gap one by one. Francis, Vexhar, and Craig clear the gap without much trouble, while Vexhar also uses a rope to make the crossing safer for the others. Broc nearly falls when he lands badly, but Francis catches the rope and Levon helps pull him back up before he drops into the river. With everyone across, the party leaves the bridge behind and starts searching Wyvern Tor for the marauder camp.

## **Scouting the Cave Entrance**

After about an hour of searching the rugged slopes, the party spots smoke and finds a cave entrance guarded by a lone bugbear. They discuss capturing him for information instead of killing him immediately, and they send Craig forward to hide in bushes and throw a rock as a distraction. Craig approaches carefully, but he crushes a bush underfoot and exposes himself before he can complete the plan. The bugbear calls for help toward the cave, forcing the party into combat earlier than they intended. From that point on, the party is no longer dealing with a single sentry but with the full group inside the cave.

## **Fight at the Wyvern Tor Cave**

  - Craig’s stealth plan fails when he crushes a bush near the sentry, and the bugbear calls for help before the party can grab him.
  - Vexhar repeatedly lands Fire Bolt and later drops a Shatter that catches most of the clustered enemies, badly damaging several bugbears at once and slowing the enemy push.
  - Francis and Levon hold the front while the cave keeps producing enemies, and Levon enters his rage as the fight escalates against multiple bugbears and a large ogre.
  - Broc contributes from range with spells, including a Chromatic Orb on the ogre and later a damaging Poison Spray that helps wear the brute down.
  - Caine uses Bless to support the group, then later heals Francis from unconsciousness with Cure Wounds while he is being grappled and dragged away by a bugbear.
  - Craig switches from ranged support to melee when a bugbear breaks through the line, then returns to cover and continues firing from hiding when the battlefield shifts.
  - Francis finishes the ogre with Talon after the group focuses it down, but the surviving bugbear obeys the orc’s order to take Caine and retreats into the cave with him.

## **End of Session**

The session ends with Levon charging after the retreating enemies as the party realizes Caine has been taken alive into the cave. The remaining orc threatens Caine with a crossbow, and the group is left facing a hostage situation at the cave entrance. The immediate unresolved goal is rescuing Caine before the enemies can disappear deeper into Wyvern Tor.
