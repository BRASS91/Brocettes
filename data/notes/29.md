## **Finishing the Ochre Jelly Fragments**

  - Vexhar lands Toll of the Dead, boosted by Potent Cantrip, for two necrotic damage as the fragment barely makes its save.
  - Allyanna casts Hex on a fragment, then blasts it with Eldritch Blast; it shatters and splatters ooze across Vexhar, Blue, Francis, and Levon.
  - Blue targets a fragment with Vicious Mockery, but the insult doesn't land on the mindless ooze, so she repositions near Gundren instead.
  - Levon rolls an intelligence check to realize a normal swing did nothing last round, then switches Choppy to its flat, bludgeoning side and lands a heavy hit that bloodies the remaining fragment near death.
  - The fragment retaliates with its pseudopod, hitting Levon for 12 acid damage that his rage does not reduce.
  - Francis, unable to damage the fragment with a slashing strike, holds position rather than risk another split.
  - Broc finishes the weakened fragment with a Sorcerer's Burst of frost damage, icing it down to a single hit point.
  - Francis crushes the frozen, nearly dead fragment with the flat of Talon, shattering it and ending the fight.

## **Marking the Trail and Tracking Signs**

Gundren tells the party he's amazed at how capable they've become since hiring them in Neverwinter, and Levon answers only with a hollow stare rather than explain what they've been through. Broc draws a directional arrow east in the tunnel dirt near the door where the party fought the ghouls, using Levon's glowing cheese for light. The group continues south along low branching tunnels, and Blue and Broc make investigation checks while scanning the walls and floor for anything unusual. Blue spots a pile of stacked, non-skeletal humanoid bones tucked in a corner, while Broc's role reveals smeared drag marks leading toward them rather than footprints. Blue signals for quiet, Broc passes the word to the rest of the party, and the group moves forward with stealth checks as they approach a wide cavern.

## **The Black Pool and the Sunken Skeleton**

The tunnel opens into a wide cavern dominated by a still black pool littered with broken shells and pale mussels, an odd brackish smell in the air, and stone steps leading east. Broc pulls Levon back from the water's edge and casts Light on a thrown rock to examine the pool, revealing a shoreline of mussels but nothing more before the glow sinks and fades roughly twenty feet down. Vexhar spots a coiled, scaled shape settled motionless near the far edge of the light, clearly alive and sizable, while Blue's attempt to sense fey energy in the pool turns up nothing. Broc uses Mage Hand to lower his still-lit candle into the water, revealing a half-sunken skeleton with something shiny nearby, and the party briefly wonders if it's the rest of Joe before agreeing it likely isn't. With the creature still hidden and quiet, Vexhar and Blue prepare to send mage hands down to retrieve the shiny object.

## **Giant Snake Ambush at the Pool**

  - As the party plans to send more mage hands down, the coiled shape uncoils and a giant snake erupts from the pool, lunging at Levon and forcing an initiative roll.
  - Allyanna casts Hex on the snake, then hits it with Eldritch Blast for solid force and necrotic damage, shoving it back into deeper water.
  - Francis charges into the shallows, strikes the snake with Talon and adds Divine Smite for slashing and radiant damage, bloodying it and shifting its focus onto him.
  - The snake bites at Francis and misses, then tries to pin him with its coiling length, but Francis shoves free before it can grapple him.
  - Blue casts Silent Image to conjure a phantom brown bear beside the snake, which fails its save, becomes frightened, and immediately flees toward the water.
  - Francis lands an opportunity attack on the retreating snake with Talon, injuring it badly enough that it dives twenty feet underwater to hide, still frightened.
  - Vexhar and Levon both try to finish it off from the shore; Vexhar's Toll of the Dead chips off necrotic damage with Potent Cantrip while Levon's thrown handaxe misses entirely.
  - Broc argues against finishing off the retreating, terrified snake, comparing it to attacking something defending its home, and the party agrees to leave it and gather the loot instead.

## **Recovering the Rings and the Wand**

With the snake gone into hiding, Vexhar and Blue use mage hands and the still-lit candle to dig into the sunken skeleton, recovering three jeweled gold rings from its left hand and a wand from its right. Broc examines the wand and identifies it as a Wand of Magic Missiles, an item that requires no attunement, holds seven charges, and recharges each dawn. Blue maintains concentration on the illusory brown bear, walking it alongside the party as they retreat from the water's edge. The party gives the wand to Levon so he has a magical option, since Choppy is an unenchanted greataxe.

## **Listening at the Goblin Door**

Climbing worn stone steps east into warmer, drier air smelling of smoke, the party reaches a stone door, and Broc presses his ear to it while the others hold back. Broc's perception check picks up gruff, irritated voices arguing on the other side, and a history check reveals the language is Goblin, learned from past dealings with the Cragmaws. He makes out scattered words like "food" and "get him," along with at least four distinct voices bickering over hunger. Broc reports back to the group with hand signals, and the party decides to investigate in case the goblins know something about Gundren's missing brother, arming Levon with the newly found Wand of Magic Missiles before opening the door.

## **Ambush on the Bugbear Den**

  - The bugbear captain orders "kill them" as the party bursts in with surprise, but a bugbear near the door reacts first, slams it shut, and traps Francis and Levon inside with all six bugbears.
  - Levon rages and reckless-attacks with Choppy, landing a charger-boosted hit for solid damage on the bugbear that insulted him in Goblin, then later switches his attack after an inspiration reroll to finish that same bugbear off entirely.
  - Allyanna deflects the bugbear captain's grapple attempt with Shield, hexes him, and blasts him with Eldritch Blast for 18 damage, shoving him back into Levon; she later flies into the room past the fallen bugbears to hex and Eldritch Blast a second bugbear to death.
  - Broc casts an upcast Witch Bolt on the captain for heavy lightning damage that lingers each round, until a bugbear slams the door shut and breaks his line of sight, ending the spell; he finishes another wounded bugbear with a Sorcerer's Burst of acid.
  - Francis invokes Vow of Enmity and strikes a bugbear with Talon, dropping it to a sliver of health and leaving it weakened, while shrugging off a separate bugbear's failed grapple attempt that still lands a few punches.
  - Blue cracks the door open just enough to cast Sleep, dropping two bugbears — including the captain — into unconsciousness on the far side of the room.
  - Vexhar finishes off the badly wounded bugbear with Toll of the Dead, boosted by Potent Cantrip, killing it outright once the door is opened wide.
  - The session ends with the captain asleep, two bugbears dead, and the last conscious bugbear shaking off his own drowsiness too late to rouse his boss before the round ends.

## **End of Session**

The session ends mid-round inside the bugbear den, with two bugbears dead, the captain asleep from Blue's Sleep spell, and three bugbears — one having briefly dozed off before shaking himself alert — still active and now aware their side is losing. Francis and Levon remain trapped inside the room with the bugbears, both bloodied, while Broc, Allyanna, and Vexhar hold the doorway from outside. The bugbears have not revealed anything about Gundren's missing brother, and the giant snake from the black pool remains alive and hidden somewhere behind the party. Combat will resume at the top of the round next session with Francis on deck.
