## **Overview**

The party wraps up in the temple after Nezznar's death, and Gundren leaves for the entrance with the dwarf the party believes is Nundro. Blue opens a locked room with Nezznar's key and finds a second, bound and beaten Nundro, so most of the party rushes back to the entrance. The first Nundro has already vanished outside. A test built on their mother's old nickname proves the prisoner is the real Nundro, and the brothers reunite. The party then finds a hidden shaft in the smelting area, rests, and meets a spectator guarding the Forge of Spells, which refuses them without credentials. While searching a burned out building for those credentials, the party draws out a floating, hateful spirit.

## **Aftermath in the Temple Hall**

Gundren embraces Nundro, who says he slipped free of his ropes while everyone ran toward the fighting. Nundro asks to be taken back to the entrance, where Tharden's wrapped body waits for burial. Gundren offers to escort him alone and tells the party to finish their work, meeting at the entrance or in the temple. Broc offers his dagger so Gundren can use Message, but Gundren hands it back once he hears about the range limits. Broc walks the brothers past the snake and scares it off with a Minor Illusion of a bear and his own roar, and the two dwarves head for the entrance.

## **Nezznar's Belongings**

Vexhar picks up Nezznar's staff, a spider topped weapon that deals extra poison damage and holds 10 charges for spider climb and web. Nezznar's body also yields a potion of healing, an iron key with an anvil shaped head, and a black leather sack holding a fancy ale mug and precious gemstones. Broc confirms the mug isn't magic and gives it to Francis as a gift. Vexhar tosses the key to Blue, who still wants to open the locked door from the previous fight.

## **A Second Nundro Behind the Door**

The key opens the door, and Blue finds a bruised, unconscious dwarf bound on the floor of a draped room with a bed and a brazier. He looks exactly like Nundro. Broc, Vexhar, Francis, and Levon run for the entrance, while Blue briefly follows before returning to stay with Allyanna. Blue revives the dwarf with Healing Word; he demands to know where Nezznar is and won't believe that Tharden or Gundren sent them. Neither Blue's nor Allyanna's Insight check gets a clear read, and Blue's Charm Person fails and leaves him terrified in the corner, begging to be freed or killed. The two decide to keep him in the room and refuse to answer his questions until the others return.

## **The Empty Entrance**

At the entrance, the party finds Gundren alone; he says Nundro stepped outside for fresh air. Broc spots fresh footprints leading out of the cave, but he and Vexhar find no sign of anyone outside in the early morning light. Vexhar's Arcana check suggests either high level magic such as true polymorph or a shapeshifter posing as Nundro. Levon tests Gundren with a question about a sharpening stone left in the wagons, and his Insight check shows Gundren's confusion is genuine. Broc casts Message along the line of the footprints asking "who are you?" and gets no reply. When the party explains, Gundren realizes the truth and says that, looking back, something about his companion had seemed off.

## **The Petunia Test**

Back at the temple, Gundren says only the real Nundro would know "Petunia," the nickname their father gave their mother. Broc asks the prisoner for his mother's middle name, though Blue overheard the answer through the door and objects. The dwarf answers "Petunia," and Gundren frees him with Nezznar's key. Nundro confirms that Nezznar used Tharden's death to torment him, then asks to see the drow's body and kicks it to be sure he is dead. Francis tells the group to stop going anywhere alone, and Blue announces a buddy system.

## **Spoils, a New Zombie, and the Forge Question**

Vexhar declines Broc's offer of a roll off and gives him the spider staff. Nundro panics at the sight of Vexhar Jr. until Gundren reassures him. Broc convinces Vexhar to swap out the worn down zombie for Nezznar, and Vexhar spends a few minutes raising the drow's body as his new zombie. Nundro explains that Nezznar kept him alive and asked about the Forge of Spells every day: where it was, how to reach it, and what the old map said. Nundro told him he didn't know. The party and the Rockseekers agree to press on and find the forge.

## **The Smelter and the Hidden Shaft**

The party climbs down the ropes into the eastern rift and searches the old smelting area near the site of the flameskull fight. Broc finds molds, bellows, a furnace, and a solid iron bar worth roughly 100 to 150 gold. Blue spots a concealed seam in the masonry, but "open sesame," Gundren's Dwarvish, and Mage Hand all fail to open it. Levon rolls a natural 20 on Athletics with Francis's help, and the two break open a hatch over a shaft about a foot and a half wide that drops five feet and turns east. The shaft gives off a cold green light and a faint smell of scorched metal, but the party drops it as too tight and decides to follow the hallways instead, as Levon suggests.

## **Doors of Green Light**

The passage leads to a set of scorched doors: a northern door with melted, fused hinges and a double door hanging ajar with green light leaking through. Broc sniffs the gap and matches the smell from the shaft. He also hears a steady, rhythmic crackling with no voices or footsteps, and he can tell the smell isn't the flameskull's. Gundren quietly names it the Forge of Spells and warns that whatever has kept the flame burning for 500 years won't be waiting quietly for company. At Broc's urging, the party retreats to the bugbear den, barricades the door, and takes a short rest. Everyone takes one downtime action, including Vexhar training with his herbalism kit.

## **Guardian of the Forge**

Francis kicks open the doors to a scorched workshop with an untouched pedestal in the middle, where a small brazier burns with an eerie green flame. A spectator floats behind the brazier and speaks only into Francis's mind, demanding his credentials. Francis doesn't realize no one else can hear it, so he answers out loud. With Broc's help on the Deception check, he claims the party are researchers and names Nezznar as their authority, which the spectator says sounds familiar but isn't tied to its masters. Broc passes Francis the iron bar and then three jeweled rings. The spectator explains that the ingot is only the material and the forge provides the enchantment, but it still refuses entry without credentials and says its duty will end soon. Francis backs away and the spectator doesn't follow. Gundren says legend holds that items bathed in the forge came out enchanted, while Vexhar, with a natural 20, and Blue identify the creature as a summoned spectator bound to guard the forge, with dangerous eye rays.

## **The Burned Quarters**

While hunting for credentials, Broc finds another damaged door jammed shut. The party then descends a staircase into a cavern with glittering minerals in the ceiling, scattered skeletons, and a second freestanding building. With Francis's help, Levon forces the warped double doors, revealing a burned out room with smashed beds, charred bookshelves, a scorched iron chest, and unnaturally cold air. Blue ritually casts Detect Magic and senses faint ambient magic pulling toward the north side of the room, but nothing on the chest or the debris. Broc searches an adjoining room and finds no papers or names. As Francis and Levon approach the chest, a ragged, shadowy figure with pale, hateful light for eyes rises above the ruined beds.

## **End of Session**

The spirit drifts through the wreckage and speaks into the party's minds: "Still warm. I'd forgotten what that looked like. You'll leave something behind when you go. They always do." The spectator still blocks the Forge of Spells, and no one knows who posed as Nundro or where the impostor went. The party reached 5th level during the short rest, and the level up takes effect next session.
