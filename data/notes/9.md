## **Overview**

The session opens with introductions and a recap that brings everyone back to Tresendar Manor’s crypt, where Craig survived on a natural 20 death save and the party still faces skeletal guardians and a trapped Redbrand. The group experiments with how the red cloaks affect the undead, then finishes the fight, destroys the remaining skeletons, and barricades the crypt so they can safely take a short rest. From there, they push deeper into the hideout, discovering a prison block where they defeat a Redbrand guard and free Mirna Dendrar and her children, learning of an heirloom tied to Thundertree. They escort the family out through the caves and then loot a well-stocked armory filled with Redbrand weapons and extra cloaks. Finally, the party finds a hidden passage leading back toward the central crevasse, where an unseen presence speaks directly into their minds just as the session ends.

## **Return to the Redbrand Crypt**

After the question of the day, play resumes in the crypt with Craig stabilized at one hit point, still prone on the floor in a red cloak after his natural 20 death save. The skeleton archers and melee guardians are still active, and the party confirms that any creature wearing a red cloak is ignored while those without are targeted. Levon, Caine, Broc, and Vexhar talk through options at the doorway, trying to move Craig to safety while using cloaked bodies and the narrow hall to block the skeletons’ line of fire. Vexhar questions whether the undead can be commanded, and his arcana knowledge confirms that controlling them would require necromantic or similar magic he does not yet have. Realizing the skeletons are simply keyed guardians tied to the cloaks and too dangerous to leave behind, the group shifts from experimentation to a plan to wipe out both the undead and nearby Redbrand threats before resting.

## **Skeleton Guardians in the Crypt**

  - Craig’s brush with death is underscored as the table confirms his previous natural 20 death save left him at one hit point and still prone amid the fighting.
  - Guided by Broc’s observation that cloaked figures are ignored, Levon and others reposition with their red cloaks to pull attention away from Craig and create safe lanes to move him.
  - Caine deliberately takes off his cloak, stepping up with shield and mace so the skeletons will target him instead, then pours a healing potion down Craig’s throat to get him back into fighting shape.
  - The gambit works as a skeleton immediately shifts focus to Caine, firing with disadvantage at close range and failing to pierce his armor.
  - Vexhar continues contributing bursts of spell damage, including a potent chromatic orb earlier in the fight that badly injures one of the skeletal guardians.
  - Once the immediate danger passes and the skeletons are no longer an active threat, Levon ultimately moves in and decapitates a remaining skeleton while the others systematically destroy the rest to clear the room.

## **Barricading the Crypt and Resting**

With the skeletons shattered and their disguises intact, the party turns to the heavy stone sarcophagi dominating the crypt. They drag lids from the coffins to block key doors, using the stone slabs as makeshift barricades to prevent anything from slipping in during a break. Confident that the nearby cave entrance is quiet and that they can hear anything approaching, they agree to risk a short rest in the sealed crypt. During the hour, they spend hit dice, check equipment, and talk through the cloak-and-skeleton puzzle that kept Craig alive. Once everyone feels recovered and their plan for further exploration is set, they move the barricades aside and prepare to push deeper into the hideout.

## **Redbrand Guard in the Cells**

  - After leaving the crypt, the party follows a corridor to a chamber lined with barred cells and discovers a Redbrand guard on duty.
  - Levon opens the door and immediately triggers the guard’s readied attack, though the sudden swing fails to significantly harm the party.
  - Vexhar attempts a brief bluff, claiming they are Redbrands coming to “deal with” the skeleton problem, but the guard refuses to stand down and threatens consequences from his superiors.
  - As talks fail, Vexhar drops the pretense and unleashes a chromatic orb that strikes the guard hard and bloodies him.
  - Francis rushes in behind the spell, cutting the guard down with a decisive melee strike and preventing him from raising an alarm.
  - Craig, who had rolled extremely well on initiative for this encounter, remains ready to act but is not needed before the others finish the brief skirmish.

## **Freeing Mirna and Her Children**

With the guard dead, the group turns its attention to the cells and the frightened prisoners within. They discover Mirna Dendrar and her children, Nilsa and Nars, who explain that the Redbrands murdered Mirna’s husband and dragged the family here. The party unlocks the cells, reassures the family, and confirms that the path they used to reach the hideout can be used as an escape route. Mirna tells them of a hidden family heirloom in Thundertree, promising a reward if the characters ever recover it, and the party takes note of the lead. Conversation stays focused on getting the family out alive and avoiding further contact with Glasstaff’s forces for now.

## **Smuggling Prisoners Out Through the Caves**

Once Mirna and her children are free, the party quickly decides that escorting them to safety cannot wait. They guide the family back through the corridors toward the cavern and bridge, reasoning that the area is currently clear and safer than trying to walk them through more populated hideout rooms. Care is taken as they move, but nothing attacks them as they retrace their steps. At the tunnel exit, the adventurers send Mirna and her children on toward Phandalin with instructions to lie low while the group finishes dealing with the Redbrands.

## **Looting the Armory and Finding a Secret**

Returning to the unexplored parts of the hideout, the party discovers a chamber filled with weapon racks and rows of Redbrand gear. Inside they find spears, swords, crossbows, bolts, and several more dirty red cloaks hanging from hooks, clearly marking this as the gang’s armory. They examine the equipment, claim a few useful pieces, and note that the supplies are well-maintained and organized. Further inspection reveals crates and markings that hint some of these goods may be stolen from local merchants such as the Lionshield Coster. While exploring the surrounding walls, the group also locates a hidden door that opens toward the central cavern, setting their next direction of travel.

## **Approaching the Crevasse and Unseen Voice**

Using the secret door, the party carefully approaches the crevasse chamber that lies at the heart of the hideout. They move quietly, with signals to keep conversation low as they step into the rocky, uneven space. Just as they begin to orient themselves and consider how to proceed, a strange voice suddenly speaks inside their minds, complaining that they “walk loudly” and “disrupt” its home. The party recognizes that some intelligent, unseen creature is aware of them, and they are left poised on the edge of a new interaction or confrontation.

## **End of Session**

The session ends with the party standing in the crevasse chamber, freshly aware that an unknown entity is watching and communicating with them telepathically. Mirna and her children are safely outside the hideout, but Glasstaff and the remaining Redbrands remain at large elsewhere in the complex. The next session will open with the heroes deciding how to answer the unseen voice and whether to treat it as foe, ally, or something in between.
