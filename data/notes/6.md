## **Overview**

The party re-entered Phandalin, noticed the town’s wary mood, and chose to immediately return the Lion’s Shield crates they recovered from the goblins. At the Lion’s Shield Coster, they met Lenine Greywind, secured a 50 gp reward, and learned that the Redbrands have been acting openly because local leadership is not pushing back. Lenine offered a standing 10% discount if the group could get Harbin Wester at the Townmaster’s Hall to take action, but the party also had to rein in their own talk of “murder” to keep the conversation from going sideways. The group then pursued leads on healing supplies and took on a shrine-related errand involving a vain banshee near Conyberry and a jeweled silver comb. By night, they gathered at the Stonehill Inn to compare notes with Toblen and Sildar, confirm Gundren’s disappearance, and hear fresh accusations that the Redbrands murdered a local man and caused further disappearances.

## **Phandalin Tension on Arrival**

After leaving Barthen’s Provisions, the party observed that Phandalin felt watchful, with doors closing quickly and people eyeing the street more than the newcomers themselves. They reviewed Sildar’s plan to meet them later at the Stonehill Inn, but prioritized returning the Lion’s Shield crates while it was still afternoon. Caine and Levon kept a lookout as they moved through town, while Broc remained comparatively unconcerned and focused on simply getting the cart along. The group’s street-side discussion turned loud enough that it risked drawing attention, and they had to decide whether to keep their plans discreet. They ultimately approached the Lion’s Shield Coster with the recovered shipment and brought the cart close enough to show proof.

## **Lionshield Reward and Lead**

Inside the shop, the party introduced themselves to Lenine Greywind and explained they found her sealed crates in a goblin lair led by a bugbear. Lenine recognized the shipment immediately, thanked them for returning it instead of keeping it, and paid them 50 gold as a reward while also offering the option to take some value in gear. When the party asked about supplies like healer’s kits, Lenine redirected them toward “Sister Garaele” at the shrine, since her stock focused on weapons and armor. Lenine also described the Redbrands as emboldened troublemakers and blamed the town’s lack of response on ineffective leadership. She proposed a deal: if the party confronted Harbin Wester at the Townmaster’s Hall and convinced him to act, she would extend a 10% discount at her store, but the group had to quickly backpedal when someone suggested “murdering them all” and reassure her they would pursue lawful solutions.

## **Banshee Quest at the Shrine**

Following the healing lead, the party spoke at the shrine and learned of a dangerous errand that the shrine’s sister could not safely handle herself. She explained that a vain banshee might be willing to provide information if the party presented a suitable gift, and she produced a jeweled silver comb for that purpose. She directed them toward the area near Conyberry to seek the banshee and clarified that her promised reward would come after they completed the task and returned with the requested information. The party accepted the arrangement and treated the comb as a shared party resource, with the understanding that it would be used as the offering. They also reviewed what the shrine could sell immediately, recognizing it was a small operation with limited stock.

## **Stonehill Inn News and Missing Gundren**

As evening settled in, the party arrived at the Stonehill Inn, arranged lodging for the night, and began ordering drinks and food while they regrouped. Toblen Stonehill engaged them in conversation about why they were in town, and the party connected their presence to Gundren’s disappearance and their concern that a “Spider” had him imprisoned. Sildar, present at the table, confirmed Gundren had gone missing and admitted he had been cautious about who to trust in Phandalin until the party forced the subject into the open. When the group pressed for details on the Redbrands, a woman carrying plates overheard and erupted, shouting that the Redbrands “murdered” a man and storming off despite Toblen’s attempts to quiet her. Toblen added that the town lacks guards or a watch, leaving only the mayor as the closest thing to law, which sharpened the party’s sense that the Redbrands problem would not resolve without direct action.

## **End of Session**

The session ended with the party settled at the Stonehill Inn, having confirmed Gundren is missing and hearing renewed claims tying the Redbrands to murder and additional disappearances. They held multiple active leads, including confronting Harbin Wester, investigating the Redbrands’ presence in town, and pursuing the shrine’s banshee-and-comb errand near Coneyberry. The immediate danger remained unresolved, with the inn conversation highlighting that Phandalin has no real law enforcement to intervene on the town’s behalf.
