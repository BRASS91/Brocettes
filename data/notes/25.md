## **Overview**

The party spends their final night in Phandalin using downtime actions before setting out for Wave Echo Cave. Training and side pursuits produce mixed results, with Allyanna's impromptu lemon juggling earning the group its only coin of the night. The next morning the party breakfasts with Gundren, settles accounts with Toblin, and debates a name for their company before departing Phandalin's gate. After a roughly five hour trek east into the Sword Mountains, they reach the hidden entrance to Wave Echo Cave and find signs that someone has recently passed through. Venturing inside, they discover an abandoned Rockseeker campsite and the body of Gundren's brother Tharden, killed by an unknown attacker, while his other brother Nundro remains unaccounted for somewhere deeper in the cave.

## **Long Rest Downtime Training**

Each party member spends a downtime action continuing a training goal from the Stonehill Inn. Levon practices acrobatics by jumping between beds, clearing the check easily. Vexhar makes progress on his herbalism kit training, working toward brewing healing potions. Broc continues shield proficiency training with a shield he purchased himself, successfully building on his prior progress. Francis fails another attempt at deception practice, and Allyanna and Blue fail their attempts at survival and Sylvan respectively.

## **Secondary Downtime Actions Around Phandalin**

For their second downtime action, Blue takes her pan flute into town to busk for coin, cheered on by Broc, but fails to earn anything. Francis approaches innkeeper Toblin for a favor using persuasion and also comes up short. Vexhar and Levon travel to the crypt beneath Tresendar Manor, where Vexhar examines an old coffin and finds a signet ring and a bundle of sealed letters, choosing to leave the ring behind out of respect. Allyanna juggles lemons in the Stonehill Inn's common room, drawing a small crowd and earning seven gold pieces, which she adds to the party's shared loot.

## **Morning Departure and the Party's Name**

The party gathers for breakfast with Gundren the next morning, and Toblin confirms their tab was already covered the night before. Toblin asks what the company calls itself, prompting Broc to propose "Brock and the Brockettes," which draws pushback from the rest of the group and settles into an unresolved joke. The party distributes their three remaining healing potions among Allyanna, Levon, and Francis and completes a long rest before gathering final supplies. Gundren, eager to move, leads the party out through Phandalin's gate around 8:26 a.m.

## **The Trek to Wave Echo Cave**

The party follows Gundren east into the foothills of the Sword Mountains, covering roughly fifteen miles over about five hours. Gundren says little during the journey, focused on finally reaching the site his brothers found a month earlier. By early afternoon the trail narrows between two rock faces, revealing a dark, unnatural opening in the mountainside. Gundren confirms this is the entrance to Wave Echo Cave.

## **Signs at the Cave Entrance**

Before entering, the party examines the area for danger. Francis spots a smashed tree and several overlapping sets of boot prints nearby, while Vexhar and Blue notice that stone at the cave mouth has been shifted more recently than the cave's supposed centuries of abandonment would suggest. Blue checks for signs of fey activity and senses nothing out of place. Vexhar casts light on Levon's belt cheese to serve as the group's lantern as they prepare to move inside.

## **Entering Wave Echo Cave**

The party steps into the cave's cold, damp passage, where cheese-light reveals old dwarven pick marks along the walls. Brock listens ahead and picks up a distant, irregular dragging sound along with a faint metallic clink, neither of which he can place. The party attempts to move quietly deeper into the tunnel as it opens toward a wider chamber ahead.

## **Tharden's Body and Gundren's Grief**

The passage opens into an abandoned campsite with bedrolls, spoiled supplies, and tools left as though someone meant to return. Gundren rushes ahead and finds the body of his brother Tharden, killed by an unknown attacker. Vexhar examines the body but cannot determine the weapon or number of attackers involved. Gundren confirms nothing appears to have been taken, gathers Tharden's cloak and personal effects, and vows to bury him properly, then tells the party that his other brother Nundro is still somewhere in the cave.

## **End of Session**

The session ends with Gundren grieving over Tharden's body just past the cave entrance. Nundro's location and fate remain unknown, and the source of the dragging sound and metallic clink Brock heard earlier has not been identified. The party has not yet advanced beyond the entrance chamber into the rest of Wave Echo Cave.
