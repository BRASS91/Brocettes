## **Battle on the Staircase**

Combat continues immediately as the party holds the narrow stairs against the remaining bugbears and Redbrand thugs.

  - Caine anchors the front line with shield and mace, absorbing heavy blows while keeping enemies from breaking through.
  - Francis fights beside him with Talon, trading decisive strikes and preventing the bugbears from gaining ground.
  - Levon controls the choke point with wide axe swings, limiting how many enemies can engage at once and punishing those who step forward.
  - Vexhar targets injured foes with focused spellcasting, ensuring wounded enemies fall before they can regroup.
  - Broc supports from the rear with precise magic that drops weakened targets at critical moments.
  - Craig remains behind cover, contributing ranged attacks while avoiding direct melee.

One by one, the remaining bugbears and red-cloaked guards fall, ending the fight and finally securing the staircase.

## **Securing the Hideout and the Wizard’s Escape**

With the immediate danger gone, the party searches surrounding chambers to assess the situation. They find no additional defenders and quickly determine that the wizard is no longer present. Evidence indicates he fled during their previous rest, choosing calculated retreat over confrontation. The group interprets this as a sign of intelligence rather than cowardice, recognizing that he now has time to regroup or prepare retaliation. The anticipated showdown is denied, replacing closure with uncertainty. The hideout is cleared, but the greater threat remains active.

## **The Spider’s Correspondence**

While searching the wizard’s quarters and belongings, the party uncovers correspondence connected to the Spider’s network. Among the documents is a reference to a “drow arcanist,” a phrase the group immediately connects to Vexhar. The wording implies awareness rather than rumor, suggesting someone has identified him specifically. This revelation reframes the Redbrand conflict as part of a broader scheme operating beyond Phandalin. The party understands they are no longer anonymous adventurers solving a local problem. Vexhar’s personal arc becomes more tightly woven into the central antagonist’s plans.

## **Drinking in the Common Room**

With the hideout temporarily secure, the party gathers in one of the Redbrands’ common rooms to decompress. They drink from the gang’s alcohol stores, turning the moment into a rough celebration after surviving the staircase battle. Francis drinks heavily without suffering ill effects, reinforcing his hardened tolerance. Vexhar attempts to match him but ultimately becomes poisoned from overindulgence. The exchange reinforces their sharp-edged camaraderie without destabilizing the group. The rest of the party participates more moderately while discussing next steps.

## **The Snoring Goblin Revealed**

Earlier in the session, before entering Glasstaff’s quarters, the party heard the unmistakable sound of goblin snoring coming from a southern room but chose not to investigate immediately. At the very end of the session, Francis decides to check the room quietly and attempts to sneak a look inside. He confirms that the sound is coming from a goblin who is tied up and asleep. The goblin appears restrained and unaware of the chaos that unfolded in the hideout. The party does not resolve the situation before the session ends, leaving the bound goblin as an unresolved element going into the next game.

## **Level Advancement**

At the conclusion of the session, the party advances to Level 3. The milestone reflects their survival of the hideout’s major threats, the defeat of the bugbear force, and the broader escalation of stakes involving the wizard and the Spider. Subclass choices and new abilities are prepared for the next session, marking a significant shift in their capabilities. The heroes are no longer inexperienced escorts; they are emerging as serious players in the region’s unfolding conflict.

## **End of Session**

The staircase battle is won, the hideout is secured, and Glasstaff has escaped. The Spider’s note confirms that Vexhar is known to outside forces. The final image of the session is Francis quietly observing a tied-up goblin still snoring in the southern room, leaving that thread unresolved as the party prepares for what comes next.
