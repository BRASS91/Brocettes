## **Waking After the Shared Dream**

The party wakes in their rooms at the Stonehill Inn to a faint smell of smoke, and Phandalin looks untouched outside. Levon, Allyanna, and Blue each find soot on one palm. Blue leaves the inn alone, unnerved that someone in her head used her real name, and finds she cannot rub the soot off. Francis thought it was a bad dream, while Levon, Allyanna, and Vexhar say they shared it, and Broc suggests a burned pastry caused it. Caine prays to Chauntea in his room and reviews the dream before joining them downstairs.

## **Broc Apologizes to Trelana**

Broc apologizes to Trelana in the kitchen for his behavior the other night, blaming drink and not recognizing her. A flat Charisma check lands well, and Trelana accepts, saying Toblen speaks highly of the party. She admits her dessert was normal and only undercooked, and they agree they are even. Broc says the pastry was still delicious, and she blushes and waves him off. Broc heads back to breakfast saying no more terrible pastries for him.

## **Breakfast and the Long Rest**

Toblen asks what they want to eat and mentions that Blue ran out the front door looking jittery. Francis tells him about the shared dream, but Toblen is confused and sees nothing wrong in the inn. Blue returns and orders a beer, and Elsa brings drinks for the table while Broc takes juice and Francis asks for pitchers. Paul confirms the party got a long rest after going to bed around 9 p.m., and everyone applies it.

## **Party Funds and a Merchant Tip**

Caine places a bag of coin in front of Francis and gives about 110 gold of his share to the party, bringing the total to 1,953 gold. Blue reminds Francis that some of the money is for renovating their manor. Toblen brings out a tray of bacon, strudels, and turnovers, tells them a traveling merchant has set up at the town green, and hands Broc a turnover from Trelana with "thank you" written in frosting. Broc tries to steal a strip of bacon from Levon, who catches him with a passive Perception of 12 and lets him take one. Broc tells Toblen the party wants to make Phandalin their home, and Toblen declares everything on the house today. The group then moves to Allyanna's table.

## **Soot Resists Cleaning and Study**

Allyanna shows her soot, a small black smudge in the center of her right palm, and Broc asks Toblen for a strong spirit to clean it. Toblen brings a pungent cleaning spirit used by miners, and it cleans the rest of Allyanna's hand but leaves the soot untouched. Blue shows her palm to Caine, who casts Guidance and studies it with an Arcana check of 23. He finds no magic in it, and it does not feel like Francis's curse, though the marking is not normal. With a passive Insight of 16, Caine links it to the shared dream and recommends asking Sister Garaele, and Francis agrees.

## **Francis Drinks the Cleaning Spirit**

Broc slides the leftover half shot toward Francis, who drinks it, fails to keep it down, and vomits into the flower bed outside the inn. Blue casts Minor Illusion church bells to cover the noise, and Vexhar casts Prestidigitation to give the smell a lavender scent. Broc rubs Francis's back and hoses him off while the rest of the group finishes breakfast. Caine kneels at the flower bed and uses his magic to restore the plants. Toblen tells them to keep whatever they are doing outside.

## **Senna Goodmeasure's Cart and Purchases**

At the town green the party meets Senna Goodmeasure, a halfling merchant with a cart of magic items, and she refuses to haggle. The party debates the wares, including a Rope of Climbing, an immovable rod, a Bag of Holding, and Bracers of Defense that cost 4,000 gold. Blue buys the Gloves of Thievery for 500 gold, and the party votes to buy a Wand of the War Mage, which Broc purchases for 1,100 gold. Paul rules that spells above first level with a gold cost in their components will deduct that gold from the caster instead of requiring the item. The party agrees to keep some gold in reserve for spells like Revivify.

## **Selling Loot and Buying Potions**

Blue sells off the party's gems, and Broc sells the jeweled rings at half value, 75 gold each, since the rings could not be made magical. Francis keeps the platinum trimmed pipe after Vexhar and Levon object to selling it, and he sells a dwarven ale mug to Senna for 40 gold. The party buys four Potions of Healing for 200 gold, and Senna then trades two Potions of Greater Healing for their remaining 583 gold. Caine and Vexhar agree to resume their work on making healing potions together, and the party considers returning later for a 1,600 gold Pearl of Power. The party is left out of gold, and Francis recalls the orphaned owlbear cubs left in their cave.

## **Deck of Many Things Lead**

Senna says she has hunted for a Deck of Many Things for 30 or 40 years and will pay well for news of one. Caine, Blue, and Vexhar roll to recall it, and only Vexhar has heard of it in passing, with nothing specific. Broc remembers a fellow actor named Stephen who vanished in Neverwinter after playing cards with a deck of ivory cards. Broc persuades Senna to hear his lead, and she offers a handsome reward and as many spell scrolls as he likes if he brings her the deck personally. The party agrees to go to Neverwinter, and Senna says she will likely stay in Phandalin.

## **Pink Flower at the Shrine**

Caine asks to repeat the offering he made earlier at the Shrine of Luck and casts his flower spell beside the shrine. A small pink flower with a pleasant scent sprouts where he kneels. Sister Garaele is not at the shrine, so the party asks about meeting her to discuss the Harpers. Paul says no day or time was ever set, and the party decides to find her at her home.

## **Ladders and Shopping at Barthen's Provisions**

The party goes to Barthen's Provisions, where Elmina is behind the counter and says she thought Caine had left for his monastery. Broc points out that every party member except Blue and Allyanna carries a ladder, and Caine buys one along with rope, a piton, a tent, and parchment. Allyanna declines to carry a ladder and walks away, and Broc goes after her. Elmina says Johnny is stocking sheds out back with Ander and Thistle after a morning delivery. She asks Caine to tell Sister Garaele that her items are in stock.

## **Checking on John and Blessing the Crew**

Francis surprises the workers with a drawn sword as a joke, and John tells the party he now goes by John and has turned his life around. John hopes to become operations manager and declines Broc's offer of renovation work at Tresendar Manor. Francis tells him the party is taking over the manor, and John suggests the cellar become a real cellar. Caine asks about weird dreams, and John says no and seems confused. Caine casts Aid and Bless on John, Ander, and Thistle for their shipment, and the party agrees to go find Sister Garaele.

## **End of Session**

The party leaves Barthen's Provisions to find Sister Garaele, who was not at her shrine, and about an hour and a half of game time has passed this session. They want to ask her about the soot on Levon, Allyanna, and Blue, the shared dream, and the meeting with the Harpers. The Deck of Many Things trip to Neverwinter for Senna Goodmeasure is agreed on, and Paul says a time skip is coming. The party is out of gold.
