## **Camp Watches and Overnight Events**

The party keeps the agreed watch order with Craig bound nearby as the camp settles under a dimming fire. Vexhar and Levon take first watch, patrol the perimeter, and Vexhar trips Levon with Mage Hand, causing a loud spill and Levon angrily striking a bush before things quiet down again. Caine and Broc take second watch, speaking quietly together as the fire settles lower and the forest stays still. Francis and Craig take final watch after being woken, with Francis keeping a steady watch while openly watching Craig closely, and Craig blinking to stay awake and muttering to himself as the coals dim toward morning.

## **Nightmares and Prophetic Dreams**

  - **Caine:** Caine dreams of a monastery courtyard collapsing into smoke as an ember lands harmlessly in his palm. A whisper tells him to “follow the ember” before the dream fades.
  - **Francis:** Francis dreams he is back in the valley with his old squad as his captain turns away and walks off. A voice tells him to “follow what he started or finish him” before everything goes dark.
  - **Craig:** Craig twitches and squeaks, “Craig dreamed Klarg eat Craig and soup. Bad soup,” then rolls over and returns to loud snoring.
  - **Vexhar:** Vexhar slips into trance and wanders an endless arcane library of whispering tomes and living ink. A presence warns that knowledge he buried is still reaching upward and that he will face it when he is ready.
  - **Levon:** Levon dreams of a vast ancient forest where glowing roots lie beneath the soil and a massive tree is split and fallen. A heavy voice tells him he disturbed what was rooted and must carry that weight forward.
  - **Broc:** Broc dreams he stands on a dim stage beneath a single lantern, sensing an unseen audience as pink flame sparks above his hand. A whisper says his fire will grow but always cost something, then a hollow-eyed figure warns him to choose carefully what he offers.

## **Morning Preparations and Craig’s Intel**

Caine prays aloud to Shantaya as Broc lingers nearby, and their quiet moment turns into awkward small talk. Broc offers crude limericks, while Caine responds with a restrained monastery joke. Caine uses Mending to repair arrow holes in Broc’s clothes, leaving visibly newer patches that Broc notes stand out. The group organizes gear, manages light sources for those without darkvision, and prepares to move. Craig confirms that captured travelers were taken alive and moved deeper into the cave system.

## **Into the Cave and Hungry Wolves**

Inside the cave, the party navigates branching passages, echoing water, and tight corridors that limit visibility. Levon approaches a chamber containing three chained wolves that snarl and strain against their restraints. Rather than fight, the party tosses rations to distract and calm them. The wolves focus on the food, making it clear they are starving rather than trained. With the wolves occupied, the party continues deeper into the cave.

## **The Rope Bridge and Sleeping Sentry**

Levon spots a rope bridge ahead with a goblin sentry asleep on it. The party pauses to debate how to proceed without raising an alarm. They consider sneaking past, distracting the goblin, or removing it quietly. Water noise and limited light complicate coordination. The situation remains unresolved as other movement in the cave threatens their window of stealth.

## **Giant Snake on the Cave Approach**

A giant green snake spots the group and lunges, forcing initiative. Levon helps manage positioning and visibility as the party reacts in the confined space. Francis closes to melee and strikes with his longsword. Using Savage Attacker, Francis maximizes the damage and kills the snake by driving the blade through its neck. The noise wakes a nearby goblin, pulling it into the encounter.

## **Flash Flood Trap Round**

A sudden surge of icy water roars through the tunnel, triggering a flood trap before anyone can reposition. Broc, Levon, and Vexhar scramble to avoid the worst of the rushing water and roll Dexterity saves. Vexhar spends resources, including heroic inspiration, to turn a near failure into a success the table calls clutch. Francis and Caine avoid the worst due to their positioning. The water drains quickly, and the party immediately checks for injuries.

## **Goblin Ambush and Sildar Standoff**

Craig moves with Francis and successfully deceives goblins long enough to create an opening. A goblin with a scimitar attacks Francis, misses, disengages, and flees while shouting to alert Yeemik. Another goblin lands a prepared shortbow shot into Francis’s shoulder. Goblins escalate the fight by throwing green explosive bombs. Vexhar uses a toll-based spell effect as the party tries to reduce enemy threats. A goblin appears on a southern ledge holding a tied-up armored prisoner identified as Sildar and threatens to kill him if the party advances.

## **End of Session**

The party is deep inside the goblin cave after surviving a snake attack, calming chained wolves, and enduring a flood trap. Goblins are fully alerted, ranged attacks are in play, and combat is ongoing. The session ends on a hostage standoff with Sildar’s life at immediate risk.
