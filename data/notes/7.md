## **Overview**

The session opens late at the Stonehill Inn as the party recaps what they know about Gundren’s kidnapping, the growing Redbrand threat, and recent rumors of murder in the streets. After deciding they are too exhausted to act, they head to bed and set plans to chase down better information in the morning. The next day, they visit Levon’s father Alvin at his farm, share food, question him about the Redbrands and the “spider” tied to Gundren, and talk through Levon’s strange eyes and a debt owed to a forbidden tree. Alvin cannot identify the spider but suggests that if trouble is tied to Phandalin, the Redbrands are likely involved or at least informed, pushing the group to confront them. From there the party meets with Harbin and Sildar to lock in rewards and contracts, then marches to the Sleeping Giant, where an encounter with Redbrand ruffians erupts into a bloody fight that ends with one survivor ready for interrogation.

## **Late Night at the Stonehill Inn**

At the inn, the party sits with Toblen and Sildar as the DM reminds them that Gundren is still missing, the Redbrands are growing bolder, and a local man has allegedly been murdered while the town does nothing. The group recalls that Toblen’s wife ran from the kitchen to report seeing Redbrands kill someone in the street, and that no one in Phandalin has stepped up to oppose them. Recognizing that it is already late and they are not fully rested, the players agree above table that heading out now “can be suicide” and choose to take a long rest instead. Toblen points them toward rooms upstairs, and the night ends with everyone turning in and planning to pursue leads after a full night’s sleep.

## **Visiting Alvin at His Farm**

The party travels to Alvin Tostig’s homestead, where Alvin greets them from a rocking chair, shakes Francis’s hand, and comments that Levon is “thinner” but still standing despite whatever he is carrying. Inside, there is a brief joke about Levon’s cheese before Francis explains they are helping people in Phandalin, seeking information on the Redbrands, and trying to track down Gundren’s kidnappers. Alvin listens, admits he has never heard of the mysterious spider, but reasons that if the problem is centered around Phandalin and the Redbrands are on their radar, the group should start with the Redbrands because they likely have their noses in anything important. As they share farm food and fresh bread and cheese, Alvin questions Caine about his background, and Caine explains he comes from a little ways away and has spent his life healing and helping people. Later, talk turns to Levon’s changing eyes, and Levon admits he disobeyed an instruction from his father by taking wood from a forbidden part of the forest, describing how he now “owes that tree a debt” while Alvin insists it is not a curse but a consequence of touching something that did not belong to him.

## **Sildar’s Updates and Harbin’s Bargain**

At the Townmaster’s Hall, Harbin Wester explains that caravans along the Triboar Trail near Wyvern Tor are being attacked by marauders, and offers 100 gold if the party can drive the marauders off permanently. He further promises another 100 gold and public recognition if the Redbrand threat in town is resolved, acknowledging that Phandalin “takes care of those who take care of it.” Sildar later adds that an elongated-headed goblin with unusual eyes was reported among the raiders and ties this to earlier rumors about strange goblins seen when Gundren was taken, reinforcing the urgency of finding Cragmaw Castle and Gundren’s captors. The group also learns more specific intelligence when Harbin, under pressure, reveals that the Redbrands brazenly drink at the Sleeping Giant in red cloaks and use the old Tresendar Manor on the hill as their base, information he admits is “more than I’m supposed to say.”

## **Planning the Redbrand Strike**

Between scenes, the players review their open quests, including Sister Garaele’s request involving a banshee, Harbin’s offer to pay for dealing with marauders, and the need to track Gundren and the spider through Cragmaw connections. Alvin’s reasoning and Harbin’s clues about the Sleeping Giant and Tresendar Manor lead the group to agree that confronting the Redbrands is their most immediate priority and may open paths toward Gundren and the spider as well. They decide to head to the Sleeping Giant tap house to provoke contact, aware that it serves as a Redbrand hangout even if it is not their primary lair. With that plan settled, the party approaches the run-down tavern and spots several rough-looking humans in grimy scarlet cloaks watching them and mocking them as “little puppies” before the situation escalates.

## **Battle at the Sleeping Giant**

  - As the party approaches the Sleeping Giant, a group of Redbrands in filthy red cloaks heckle them and tell them to move along, and the confrontation quickly escalates into open combat when the adventurers refuse to back down and one of the ruffians fails to shove Francis.
  - Early in the fight, Vexhar locks a witch bolt onto one of the ruffians, maintaining the crackling energy over multiple rounds to steadily wear that enemy down and eventually finish him off.
  - Broc opens with a mind-affecting spell that fails to take hold, then shifts tactics and spends the rest of the battle hurling damaging magic at different Redbrands while keeping himself out of easy reach.
    Craig supports from range, lining up careful shots and unleashing a particularly vicious attack that combines his goblin Fury of the Small with Sneak Attack to severely injure one of the thugs.
  - Levon throws himself into the melee and, over the course of the brawl, lands a devastating blow that splits a Redbrand’s head and helps clear pressure off the front line.
  - Francis fights up close alongside Levon while protected by a barklike magical enhancement that had been placed on him beforehand, trading blows and helping to bring down an enemy before that protection ends once its target is dropped.
  - When one of the remaining Redbrands turns to flee, Caine rushes in to grapple him, dragging the struggling ruffian back and holding him fast until the rest of the gang is cut down, leaving this last man alive as a prisoner for interrogation.

## **End of Session**

When the fighting stops, the Redbrands who confronted the party outside the Sleeping Giant lie dead or incapacitated, with only one left conscious and restrained in Caine’s grip. The group discusses tying this survivor to interrogate him about the Redbrand operation, Tresendar Manor, and any connection to Gundren or the strange goblin. Session ends on this cliffhanger, with the captured Redbrand surrounded by the party and ready to be questioned at the start of the next session.
