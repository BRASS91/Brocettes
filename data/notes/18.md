## **Questioning Agatha in Conyberry**

Agatha focused on Vexhar after he suggested testing whether she could be ended, and she warned him not to try. Broc offered the comb and asked what she knew about Bojental’s spellbook. Agatha said she once had the book, but its secrets bored her, so she traded it to Tsarnaev of Erebor over a century earlier. She did not know where the book was now, because it had passed from her care. Allyanna’s comment and continued requests angered Agatha, so she refused another question and ordered them to leave.

## **The Dry Well and Hamun**

Outside Agatha’s home, Vexhar examined the well and found it completely dry, with an echo suggesting long-term emptiness. The group discussed whether the well, necromancy, Agatha, and Hamun might be connected. They chose not to push Agatha further and returned to Old Owl Well. Hamun accepted that Agatha had not answered his question and asked again whether the goblin hideout had been cleared. Once they confirmed it, he paid sixty gold and fifteen silver, gave Vexhar a scroll of Darkness, and agreed to trade future arcane insight for discoveries about the spellbook or necromancer.

## **Choosing Cragmaw Castle**

After leaving Hamun, the group debated whether to return to Phandalin or head directly toward Cragmaw Castle. They discussed travel pace, encounters, and possible rest before the castle. The party chose the faster route toward Cragmaw, accepting disadvantage to save time. Levon was absent from the table, but the group rolled for him and continued. After several hours, they reached a bridge at night and faced hobgoblins in formation.

## **Battle at the Bridge**

  - A hobgoblin shot at Allyanna, but she used Shield from her staff to turn the hit aside.
  - Blue cast Mirror Image and used Mantle of Inspiration to move Francis, Levon, and Broc forward.
  - Broc distracted a hobgoblin archer with a loud sneeze, turning a strong shot into a miss.
  - Francis marked a hobgoblin with Vow of Enmity and Hunter’s Mark, rushed in with Talon, and killed it.
  - The hobgoblin captain struck Francis with a greatsword, dropped him, and advanced on Levon.
  - Allyanna used Healing Light to revive Francis, then missed with Eldritch Blast.
  - Levon raged and engaged the captain while the captain shifted focus to him.
  - Broc hit the captain with Chromatic Orb, bounced the spell to another hobgoblin, and triggered a Wild Magic Surge that caused nearby creatures to heal.

## **End of Session**

The session ended mid-combat at the bridge on the road toward Cragmaw Castle. Francis was conscious again but badly hurt, and the hobgoblin captain remained engaged with Levon after taking heavy damage. The unresolved danger is whether the party can break through the hobgoblin choke point and continue toward Cragmaw Castle.
