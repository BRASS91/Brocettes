## **Overview**

The party opened the session by answering the Question of the Day and recapping the previous session, then delivered six baskets of apples to the Stonehill Inn. They met Sildar Hallwinter, Gundren Rockseeker, and Harbin Wester at the Townmaster’s Hall, where they gave an account of Wave Echo Cave. Harbin presented them with the deed to Tresendar Manor, and Gundren promised them 10% of the cave’s future profits. Torra Ironwell toured the manor with them, took 150 gold to build personalized rooms, and asked them to investigate a damaged drainage system. The party entered the sewers, fought several swarms of rats, and was addressed by an unknown voice in the dark.

## **Introductions and Previous Session Recap**

Paul welcomed a guest named Craig Jr. and the players introduced their characters while answering the Question of the Day. Heroic inspiration was checked, and Broc won the roll to give the recap. Broc summarized the escape from the mines, the night at the tavern, and the day spent picking apples while Harbin waited. Paul added that Tharden’s body is with Sister Garaele, that Gundren went to visit Sildar, and that the party collected six baskets of apples from Daran Edermath’s orchard. Paul then set the morning scene.

## **Delivering Apples to the Inn**

The party decided to take the apples to the Stonehill Inn and keep one basket for Levon, who wanted them to rot. They found the inn empty and Broc knocked on the kitchen door to find Toblen and Trilena Stonehill preparing for the day. Broc gave Toblen five baskets and asked for a pie and apple danishes in return. Trilena offered to cook any dish they named, and the party kept the order to pies and danishes. Levon stashed his basket on the sunny side of a tree, and the party left for the Townmaster’s Hall.

## **Townmaster’s Hall Reports and Rewards**

Sildar and Gundren asked the party to explain what happened at Wave Echo Cave. The party described the Nundro duplicate, the beholder whose contract had run out, and the forge now ready for use. Vexhar reported that undead remain near the forge, and Gundren and Paul’s NPCs suggested Craig’s goblin commune could clear them out. Broc handed Sildar a letter for Craig, and Francis taught Sildar to say Craig in goblin. Harbin then gave the party the deed to Tresendar Manor on behalf of the town, with restoration already paid for. Gundren added that the party will receive 10% of everything Wave Echo Cave earns once it is running.

## **Touring Tresendar Manor with Torra**

Harbin introduced Torra Ironwell, the dwarf contractor who will restore the manor, and she led the party up the hill. Torra walked them through the western rooms, the collapsed sections, and the sound eastern wing while the party requested rooms. Levon asked for a woodshop stocked with the broken mahogany furniture, and the party asked for a bar, a training room, a musician’s study, an alchemist study, and a sunroom. Torra told the party the town covers the structural work but personalized rooms cost 150 gold for all six, and the party paid it from the party loot. She then found a corroded iron pipe that smelled foul and was dripping, and she asked the party to investigate the drainage system before her crew began.

## **Descending into the Drains**

Torra led the party to a drainage opening in the hillside south of the manor and refused to send her crew inside until the cause was found. The party entered a damp network of drainage tunnels, and Francis lit his lantern. Broc cast Spider Climb from his staff and crawled along the ceiling, and Levon crossed a plank that shattered under him without falling. Francis rolled a natural 20 on a perception check and learned the murky water was only old runoff. Vexhar stayed back at the entrance, refusing to enter the water, and Blue and the others failed to persuade him to follow.

## **Combat: Rat Swarms in the Drains**

  - Four swarms of rats burst from debris and cracks and rushed the party while Broc stayed on the ceiling ten feet up.
  - Francis jumped to join the front line, slipped into the water, and fought with his greatsword. The swarms resisted his weapon damage, and he later skewered the last three fleeing rats with an opportunity attack.
  - Blue cast Toll the Dead on an injured swarm and cast it again on a later turn, which broke most of the rats. She also gave Francis Bardic Inspiration and crossed the water with a jump.
  - Levon attacked recklessly with his two attacks and cleave, killing several rats, and he used a magic missile to finish others. He took bites from the swarms, including a natural 20.
  - Allyanna cast Hex on one swarm and later moved it to a near dead swarm. Her greatsword swing killed about half of one swarm, and a natural 20 Eldritch Blast dealt 30 force damage and destroyed another.
  - Broc cast Astral Flood as a cone, dealt 12 radiant damage to three swarms, and left the survivors blinded until the end of his next turn.
  - Vexhar attacked from the back and missed.

## **Exploring Damaged Pipes and the Flooded Chamber**

Broc crawled along the ceiling past a patched pipe that burst with scalding steam, taking four fire damage, and he sealed the leak with Mage Hand. Broc found a barred pipe with damage across its roof and later found one with its grate missing, scraped rim, bent bolts, and a pale slick substance that he identified as organic and recently shed. The party followed the damage into a broad rocky chamber with crushed iron conduits and a dark central pool. Blue and Broc sent their Mage Hands down into the pool with a rope and a candle, and they found the water was at least 20 feet deep. They noticed signs that something very large moved through the chamber.

## **End of Session**

The party stood in the damaged chamber after retrieving their Mage Hands when a distant clanking stopped and the room fell silent. A voice with no direction spoke clearly in their minds, calling them creatures who crawl through the stone and asking why they had come. The party has not answered, and the source of the voice and the damage to the pipes remains unknown. Torra Ironwell is waiting for the cause to be found before her crew begins repairs.
