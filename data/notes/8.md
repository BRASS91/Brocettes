## **Overview**

The party spends the opening of the session deciding what to do with their captured Redbrand, Johnny, ultimately choosing to spare his life in exchange for information and hard labor instead of execution. Using his directions, they plan an infiltration of the Redbrand hideout under Tresendar Manor, disguising themselves in stolen crimson cloaks and distributing healing resources before heading out. Inside the cellar beneath the ruined manor, they quietly explore, recover a hidden satchel of valuables from the cistern, locate a secret door, and engage a squad of Redbrands in their barracks, where one briefly traps Levon and Francis in a room before the group prevails and loots the bodies. Pressing on, they discover prison cells holding battered captives and a locked strongbox of coin and gemstones, then carefully rope their way across a dangerous crevasse that nearly claims Craig. The session ends in the family crypt, where skeletal guardians relentlessly focus their attacks on Craig, dropping him to the brink of death before a natural 20 on his final death save brings him back to consciousness at one hit point, still prone and surrounded as the battle rages on.

## **Handling Johnny and the Redbrands**

The party interrogates Johnny at the Sleeping Giant, pressing him for details about the Redbrand hideout, its entrances, numbers, and the whereabouts of Glasstaff and any bugbear muscle backing the gang. Rather than execute him, they decide to leverage his knowledge, stripping him of his gear and marching him across town to Barthen’s Provisions to negotiate a sort of enforced apprenticeship with Elmina mucking stables for months as penance. After Johnny is handed over and bound to this new arrangement, the group returns its focus to the Redbrand threat, reviewing their gear, redistributing healing potions so that Caine and Levon each carry one, and confirming that Francis can lean on Lay on Hands in emergencies. They also agree on their basic approach: walk openly to Tresendar Manor in stolen red cloaks and attempt to blend in as Redbrands long enough to get close to Glasstaff before blades inevitably come out.

## **Infiltrating the Cellar at Tresendar Manor**

Wearing the crimson cloaks taken from the dead Redbrands in the street, the party approaches the ruined Tresendar Manor and slips into the concealed cellar access that Johnny described. They find a storage room and a large cistern, where Levon dives into the cold water to retrieve a submerged satchel containing coins, travel clothes, and a pair of useful potions, including one of invisibility. After regrouping and quickly stashing these new resources, they discover and open a hidden door leading deeper into the complex. Moving carefully down the hall in their disguises, they come to a barracks chamber where several Redbrands are relaxing, and a tense bit of roleplay turns into a sudden fight once the gang members realize something is wrong.

## **Fight in the Redbrand Barracks**

  - Combat breaks out in the barracks when the ruffians grow suspicious of the cloaked newcomers, and weapons are drawn on both sides.
  - Caine supports Francis with guidance, allowing the paladin to land a heavy hit that badly wounds one of the Redbrands and starts to turn the tide.
  - One Redbrand uses the chaos to disengage and retreat, slipping into the adjoining chamber and locking the door behind him, briefly trapping Levon and Francis away from the rest of the group.
  - Craig, aided by Chauntea’s guidance and a damaged doorframe softened earlier by Vexhar’s firebolt, successfully picks the lock to free his allies and reopen the path into the melee.
  - Broc finishes the last surviving ruffian in spectacular fashion with a sorcerous blast of fire that erupts over the bandit’s head, ending the skirmish.

## **Freeing Prisoners and Crossing the Crevasse**

After the fight, Francis searches the bodies and claims multiple belt pouches of coin and a pair of garnets, adding them to the communal loot while Caine checks the nearby closet and hall for additional exits. Exploring further, they discover dungeon cells holding terrified captives and a chest of silver, gold, and gemstones, which they secure along with key information about the Redbrands’ activities and the prisoners’ connection to Phandalin. The group decides to leave the rescued prisoners safely locked away for the moment, intending to clear more of the hideout and then escort them out once the immediate threat is reduced. Traveling deeper into the complex, they reach a narrow stone bridge over a dark crevasse and rig a rope crossing; Caine nearly loses his balance but recovers with help, while Craig panics mid-crossing, slips, and only avoids falling by desperately clinging to the rope until Levon hauls him back up to safety.

## **Skeleton Battle in the Crypt**

  - Entering a family crypt beyond the crevasse, the party investigates three stone sarcophagi, triggering skeletal guardians to rise and attack.
  - Caine uses his positioning and battlefield awareness to coordinate initiative swaps and front-line coverage while Francis moves in to confront the undead directly.
  - The skeletons repeatedly ignore closer targets wearing red cloaks and instead fixate on Craig, landing both sword and bow strikes that leave the goblin badly bloodied and then unconscious on the floor.
  - Francis spends Lay on Hands to restore a significant portion of Craig’s health, briefly pulling him back from the brink as an arrow slowly pushes out of the wound under divine healing.
  - Vexhar repeatedly hurls firebolts at the skeletons, sometimes scorching stone and sometimes cracking bone, while Broc maneuvers to unleash bursts of arcane damage from safer angles.
  - One skeleton squeezes past Caine and Levon despite their attempted opportunity attacks and grapples, continuing the relentless pattern of ignoring them to shoot Craig on the ground, inflicting two failed death saves.
  - At the bottom of the round, with two failures already marked, Craig rolls his final death save, gets a natural 20, and suddenly jolts back to life at one hit point, still prone amid the melee as the session cuts to black.

## **End of Session**

The session ends in the middle of the crypt battle, with skeletal archers and swordsmen still standing and several party members crowded in the doorway and along the walls. Craig has just regained consciousness at one hit point after a miraculous death save and remains prone and extremely vulnerable on the floor. The rest of the party is still engaged in a tight, dangerous fight with the undead guardians, and the fates of both the adventurers and the nearby prisoners remain unresolved as we pause play.
