## **Overview**

The party enters the wrecked ore-processing chamber of Wave Echo Cave, avoids engaging a floating green flameskull, and instead descends into a bone-littered cavern strewn with dead dwarves and skeletal remains. Five reanimated corpses lunge from the rubble, and the party fights through a brutal melee that drops Francis unconscious before Eliana's Eldritch Blast finishes the last of them. Searching the chamber afterward stirs a second wave of zombies and draws out the flameskull itself, which unleashes a fireball and a rolling fire sphere that nearly overwhelm the group. The party whittles the flameskull down with Brock's Witch Bolt, a Wand of Magic Missiles shot from Levon, and a final Eldritch Blast from Eliana, then purifies its remains with holy water on Vexhar's advice to keep it from reforming. Gundren reveals the chamber was an ore-smelting room and that the true prize, the Forge of Spells, lies deeper in the cave, but insists on finding his brother Nundro first. Badly wounded, the party retreats from Wave Echo Cave to camp near Phandalin, where Levon's greataxe Choppy is magically upgraded while he carves a strange piece of wood during his watch.

## **Recap of the Previous Session**

Chris recaps the party's fight against a band of bugbears, sparing their leader in exchange for a promise to help Craig's goblin farming commune. Vexhar reanimated one of the dead bugbears into a zombie companion and used it to scout ahead, narrowly avoiding a spider that noticed only the zombie. The party retreated to plan, unbarricaded a door the bugbears had sealed, and glimpsed a flaming green skull hovering on the other side before ending the previous session.

## **Facing the Flameskull's Chamber**

The party stands at the doorway of a wrecked mining chamber containing cracked pillars, a dead furnace, a stilled water wheel, rusted ore carts, and more than a dozen dead dwarves, with a motionless green flameskull hovering above the wreckage. The group debates whether to engage the skull or the spider seen earlier, ultimately deciding neither fight is worth the risk while they search for Gundren's brother. They spot an unexplored staircase leading down and choose to investigate it instead, moving quietly to avoid drawing the flameskull's attention.

## **Discovering the Bone-Littered Cavern**

Descending the stairs, the party enters a three-level cavern split by rock ledges, with two stone tables, cold braziers, and a smaller table on an eastern ledge. Bones and skeletal remains from what appears to have been a fierce past battle are scattered across the floor and piled in the corners. From his elevated position, Brock notices something crouched and waiting among the rubble near the tables just before the bones stir to life.

## **Corpses Rise in the Cavern**

  - Five reanimated corpses (ghouls) lunge from the rubble as combat begins, with Vexhar's zombie companion, Vexhar Jr., positioned at the front line.
  - Francis charges the nearest ghoul but is bitten twice and dropped unconscious, taking heavy piercing and necrotic damage.
  - A second ghoul rushes past Vexhar's zombie and bites Vexhar for additional necrotic and piercing damage.
  - Blue casts Mirror Image on herself, then uses her Mantle of Inspiration to grant Eliana (Allyanna), Vexhar, Brock, and Levon temporary hit points and a free reaction move.
  - Levon charges a ghoul, triggers Reckless Attack, and finishes it off with his greataxe Choppy.
  - Allyanna heals Francis with Healing Light, then casts Tasha's Hideous Laughter, which fails before she is bitten and loses concentration.
  - Vexhar's Firebolt and Brock's empowered Cloud of Daggers whittle down the remaining ghouls, with Brock's wild magic surge granting the party regeneration.
  - Allyanna lands a natural-20 Eldritch Blast for 33 damage, shattering the final ghoul and ending the fight.

## **Searching the Smelting Chamber**

After the fight, the party spreads out to search the tables and a throne-like chair carved with dwarven sigils, finding old pipes, candle holders, and tobacco remnants suggesting the room was once a break area for dwarven miners. Brock and Levon both remark that the skeleton on the throne resembles Levon's ancestors. From the elevated seat, the party spots a faint green light in the distance, confirming the flameskull is still nearby and prompting Vexhar to send his zombie ahead to scout.

## **The Flameskull's Fireball and Fire Sphere**

  - More corpses rise as zombies but are mysteriously unable to cross the chamber's doorway, crowding the entrance instead.
  - Francis and Vexhar fire arrows and a firebolt at the stalled zombies while the party waits to see if the flameskull will engage.
  - The flameskull unleashes a fireball that catches five party members, dropping several near death, though the party notes it rolled well below average damage.
  - Brock's Sorcerer's Burst and a natural-20 hand axe throw from Levon finish off individual zombies pinned at the doorway.
  - Vexhar's second reanimated zombie, Little Vexhar Jr., is destroyed by a zombie's bite before it can land a killing blow.
  - The flameskull conjures a rolling fireball sphere that scorches Brock, then Levon, then Blue in succession as it rolls down the stairs.
  - Brock's Witch Bolt latches onto the flameskull, dealing lightning damage each turn and knocking loose one of its flaming eye sockets.
  - Levon breaks the flameskull's concentration with a shot from the Wand of Magic Missiles, and Allyanna lands the killing Eldritch Blast that shatters the skull, ending combat.

## **Purifying the Flameskull's Remains**

Vexhar's arcana check reveals that the destroyed creature was a flameskull, the animated remains of a spellcaster who kept casting even after death, and that it can return within hours unless its remains are purified with holy water or similar magic. The party gathers the scattered skull fragments with Mage Hand and pours their vial of holy water over them, causing the bones to hiss and settle into ordinary, inert remains.

## **Gundren's Warning About the Forge of Spells**

Gundren, who stayed out of the fighting, examines the dry channel and identifies the chamber as where the mine's ore was smelted into ingots. He reveals that the true prize, the Forge of Spells, lies deeper in the cave, but insists that finding his brother Nundro comes first and leaves the decision to rest up to the party given how injured everyone is.

## **Retreat and Long Rest Outside the Cave**

Recognizing the party is too wounded to continue, the group opts to retreat from Wave Echo Cave rather than risk a long rest near the spider or a possible flameskull revival. They travel roughly an hour back toward Phandalin and make camp among rock formations as night falls.

## **Downtime and Watches at Camp**

During downtime, Brock fails to focus on shield training, Vexhar fails his herbalism practice, Allyanna succeeds at a survival check while keeping watch, and Blue focuses on recovering physically and mentally. The party splits into three watches: Vexhar and Brock sit mostly in silence, with Vexhar considering reanimating a new zombie companion; Levon and Blue discuss Levon's home and Blue's uncertain memories of her own; and Francis and Allyanna keep the final, uneventful watch, though Francis dozes through his shift unnoticed.

## **Levon's Carved Sapling and Choppy's Upgrade**

While whittling during his watch, Levon is drawn to an oddly grained piece of wood and carves it, unknowingly recreating a pattern from a memory of a tree he once cut down. A natural-20 wisdom saving throw keeps him fully present through the experience, and the finished carving becomes a small wooden tree keepsake. Immediately afterward, Levon notices the same pale pattern has appeared in the handle of his greataxe Choppy, which is now warm to the touch and permanently upgraded to a +1 weapon.

## **End of Session**

The party completes a full long rest just before dawn, restoring their strength after the costly battle against the flameskull and its zombies. Levon's greataxe Choppy has been permanently upgraded to a +1 weapon following the carving event during his watch. The group has still not located Gundren's brother Nundro, has yet to deal with the spider it avoided earlier, and knows the Forge of Spells lies somewhere deeper in the still partially unexplored cave.
